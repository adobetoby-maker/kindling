  Non-negotiable canon.

  movement emotionally as well as physically. Do not let grief resolve quickly or
  neatly — Milo's death should still be raw, not processed, by Ch. 23's close;
  full grieving is Movement 6's to carry forward, not this movement's to complete.
  Rook giving his real name to Wyck first (Ch. 23) should read as a real,
  specific act of trust earned across the whole book, not a formality.
- **Knowledge boundaries:** No remaining reserved terms from earlier movements
  need special handling here — "the Path," "Satori," "a Reading," Homura, and
  Vera are all already spoken vocabulary as of Ch. 17. The one release specific to
  this movement is Rook's real name (Elias Marsh) — see Reserved facts below for
  exactly how it should land.
- **Relevant system or science rules:** Full detail in `CANON.md`. Key points:
  - This is the book's `arsenal-climax` movement — the group's distinct tools and
    roles (Toren's Ember, Wyck's Ember-rank plus his third-door discipline, Dessa's
    now-stabilized Ember rank and her latent Satori-adjacent potential, Milo's
    light, Rook's Blaze-level unbound range) should each do a distinct,
    irreplaceable job under real, irreversible stakes — not just "everyone
    fights."
  - Human opponents are a different register from Riftspawn and should read that
    way on the page: they retreat, feint, negotiate, panic, surrender, or die
    in ways that carry moral weight Riftspawn combat doesn't. Do not write Cinder
    Company fighters as reskinned Riftspawn.
  - The Yield/ash economy does not apply to human combat — do not have anyone
    collect ash from a person.
  - **Craft caution, from a recent cold-reader review of Ch. 1–18 (both editions
    share this weakness):** the manuscript leans on a repeated cadence — "that was
    the thing," precise elapsed-time counts, "which was worse," "he said it
    flatly," and a mechanical lesson → injury → debrief rhythm — often
    excellently, but frequently enough that it starts reading as visible
    machinery rather than voice. This movement is exactly the kind of high-stakes
    material that tempts a return to that rhythm at its most mechanical (wound,
    then explanation). Vary the reveal-and-consequence structure where the scene
    allows it; let some costs land without an immediate debrief attached.
