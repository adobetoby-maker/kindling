# Living book map — Book 4 (HORIZON silhouette)

Working title: `OPEN`. Status: `POSSIBLE`, deliberately silhouette-depth only
— per the operating loop, HORIZON material stays a shape, not a plan. Shared
across editions (per agreement, pending the Tallow Gate/Undern session's own
operator confirmation): the Mother/Father myth first becomes undeniable for at
least one point-of-view character here. Everything else is `OPEN`.

## The one-sentence promise

Someone the clan trusts gets close enough to real multi-door mastery that the
Mother/Father myth stops being a story Rook half-believes and starts being
something at least one living person has brushed against — without the book
confirming, resolving, or explaining what it actually is.

## Why this book, not Book 3 or the series endpoint

Book 3 proves the Path scales past one mentor. Book 4 is the first place a
character has *reason* to wonder whether the ladder itself has an end that
isn't Glory-as-abstraction — because Book 3's Blaze crossing (Toren's, per the
shared agreement) puts a second door-opening on the page for the first time
since Rook's own, decades ago. Watching it happen to someone else, up close,
is what makes the old myth stop being safely distant. This is explicitly not
the series endpoint: nobody reaches Glory, nobody meets a Maker, and the
disk's true nature stays permanently unexplained per the cross-series Artifact
rule. The point of Book 4 is proximity, not arrival.

## Candidate pressures (not yet architecture — options, not commitments)

- **Rook's own ceiling.** Having watched Toren cross into Blaze in Book 3,
  Rook — a confirmed two-door Blaze since Book 1 — faces the question of
  whether his own stall is grief, discipline, or a real limit. His private
  premonition (the "flat dark thing with cuts across the face of it") and the
  Dream-Covenant question (deliberately left ambiguous since Book 1) are the
  most natural places for this book to finally lean on that ambiguity, without
  resolving which it is.
- **A third practitioner**, met in Book 3's cross-zone cooperation, who has
  gone further than either Rook or Toren and is willing — or unwilling — to
  say what's actually on the other side of a second or third door. This
  person should not be a mentor-replacement for Rook; the house discipline
  against a second Homura-style authority figure applies here too.
- **The disk's next fragment of truth.** Not an explanation — another
  Meridian-archive-style deepening (per Book 1's "released to bearer" pattern)
  that raises the stakes of what Toren is carrying without naming what it is.
  Whether this ties to the Mother/Father myth directly or stays a separate
  thread is an open craft choice, not a commitment.
- **Cinder Company's true commissioner** — the "who benefits from the
  fracture staying open" question, open since Book 1, could surface partially
  here if Book 3 didn't already spend it. Not required.

## What this book must not do

- Resolve the Mother/Father myth as literally true or literally false.
- Put any character at Glory rank or within sight of it as a completed state.
- Explain the disk's true origin.
- Introduce a new mentor figure who out-authorities Rook rather than
  complicating what he and Toren already know.

## Series-endpoint bookkeeping

Per `SERIES_MAP.md`'s HORIZON section, the "god level"/Glory tier and any
literal encounter with the Makers stays explicitly unscheduled, "far beyond
any currently planned book." Book 4 moves the series one step closer to that
horizon without spending it — the next planning pass after Book 4 is drafted
should re-evaluate whether a Book 5 silhouette is warranted or whether the
series' long tail stays deliberately unplanned until Book 4's actual text
exists to build from.
