  Caul Hill/Sag behavior.

- Home Line exists under Dessa's recorded command.
- Provisional defender standing is voluntary, reviewed in spring, with adult support,
  written floors, and no debt clock.
- Callie/Tilda and Jab/Tonk household terms are stable for a year or longer as
  specified in Chapter 47. Forty households remain on twenty-eight-day review.
- Jab teaches once weekly and takes chosen Sag watches.
- Three lanes and the seven-column ledger exist; the reasons column is essential.

## Resources and unresolved objects

- The treatment machine is repaired and honest.
- Caul Hill retains a major ash field, marked jars, the half-open 140 box, ten
  unopened rooms, a drum, and the green-wire gate.
- A rope was left at the wash for somebody coming the other way.
- Four east-road arrivals are in Meridian's south huts.
- The new-stamped brass is held as evidence and must return to the ledger.

## Hard limits

- Sustain removes active burden, not fixed injury or disease.
- Ward moves matter/force, not radiation.
- Guard costs breath and load. Edge requires release. One configuration remains
  binding below earned unbound mastery.
- Ash is finite, graded, transported, accounted for, and capable of running out.
- No infinite Yield, soul theft, time reversal, universal cure, surprise door, or
  untrained climax solution.
