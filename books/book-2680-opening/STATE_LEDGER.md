## Prologue and opening day

- Year 2680, during the six-hundredth-anniversary season of the Fall.
- The prologue's final historical snapshot is 2676: **Kiva Rowan**, thirteen,
  reaches the Kindling uninjured and opens all three known doors.
- Chapter One begins four years later. Kiva is seventeen and seeking collegiate
  placement for the 2680 season.
- Kiva lives with her aunt and legal guardian, **Mara Rowan**, in Meridian. Mara
  reads and tunes the city's ash banks for the Municipal Works and repairs their
  instruments.
- The Satori disk reached Kiva through a sealed Home Line appointment by its prior
  bearer, not through blood descent. Kiva knows it is old and entrusted; she does
  not know its full public history or origin.
- Meridian's schools expect one first door and have procedures for unusual strength,
  but no accepted process for three simultaneous traditions.

## Knowledge boundaries

- General public: knows the Fall, Kindling, ranks, schools, Riftspawn, and ordinary
  filter language as history/science appropriate to the final world-state choice.
- Students: know the School's version of Home Line history, not the Home Line's own
  customs or the Order's telling.
- Orla Dane: knows the disk family and at least one suppressed prior anomaly.
- Kiva: does not know the disk's true origin, the full filter theory, or why
  all three doors opened.
- Kiva remembers the 2676 fever dream in sensory fragments: Glory-level figures
  converging, the sky/filter absent, pure presence pressing into the world, and every
  ash-lit thing responding. She does not know the later name **Unroofed Sky**, whether
  the dream predicts anything, or that older Glory accounts overlap it.
- Orla went to the Order of the Even Hand in 2664 about a two-door boy and its Warden
  told her what Annick saw; she does not begin Book One with proof that Kiva's dream
  matches it, and she has told no one she went.
- Reader: sees evidence before explanation; no prologue solves the mystery.

## Physical and progression state

- All three doors open at Kindling, but none is mastered.
- The event leaves three fine, asymmetrical heat-lines from Kiva's sternum toward
  her left shoulder. They remain visible and become tender when the doors compete.
- The disk warms once, only after Kiva completes the doors in a correct deliberate
  sequence. It never instructs her or grants technique.
- The disk can amplify or carry the Unroofed Sky fever dream, but it cannot trigger
  on demand, answer questions, or supply a combat/engineering solution.
- Initial classification is incomplete or contradictory, not a secret top rank.

## Social state

## Institutional and world locks

- Campus compact: **the Meridian Compact**.
- Mixed-path college: **the Crooked Line School**.
- Resilience/body college: **Stonehand Hall**.
- Medical, Ward, Sustain, and ash-engineering college: **Wren House**.
- Collegiate competition: **the Meridian Collegiate Circuit**; final regional
  event: **the Regional Gates**. Events include the singles, team lanes,
  ashcraft trials, rescue/extraction problems, and controlled Riftspawn engagements.
- The collegiate curriculum includes a staged field license: contained spawn anatomy
  and kill drills, supervised Handful kills, live-rift recovery, then higher-yield team
  responses. Book One devotes several connected chapters to Kiva's team learning how
  to kill Riftspawn and recover the ash cleanly before the first major live response.
- Above college are **chartered rift companies**: salaried professional teams that
  fight Riftspawn, close or contain rifts, rescue civilians, protect infrastructure,
  and recover ash. Standings reward closure, lives saved, protected infrastructure,
  clean ash, and rule compliance—not kills alone.
- Book One is regional. Book Two expands toward national competition and company
  politics. Book Three becomes international as the filter and ash grid destabilize.
- Mature hardened regional technology and scheduled wormhole stations exist. Travel
  is expensive and bounded; interplanetary normality does not.
- Classification uses physical instruments, projected tables, and human rulings.
  There is no private retinal or software-like status overlay.
- **Draw** is ash spent on standard work divided by that task's book/bench expectation:
  `1.0` is standard and lower is more efficient. A contested four-minute hold uses a
  book expectation of twelve hundredths of a dram.

## Ash-electric civilization and Glory

- Refined ash is the dominant finite energy source. **Ash induction** converts a
  controlled ash reaction into ordinary current for lights, pumps, heat,
  communications, medicine, workshops, transit, arenas, and rift sensing.
- Ash does not create free energy. Cells are graded, metered, consumed, reconciled,
  and vulnerable to contamination or runaway.
- **Ashcraft** blends magic and ash: a trained soul supplies pattern, intention,
  and control; ownerless ash supplies most of the fuel. Different traditions shape,
  contain, conduct, or sustain the reaction differently.
- Modern cells continue running after a Kindled operator starts and tunes them, but
  still require maintenance, replacement ash, and emergency shutdown.
- At **Glory**, a rare master can impress stable patterns at civic or industrial
  scale. Glory does not make ash infinite; it expands safe throughput, duration,
  precision, and the complexity of blended systems.
- That capability produces ash-power industrial dynasties as well as school orders
  whose masters pursue near-monastic devotion to one tradition. Some orders reject
  corporate ownership; some industry houses sponsor them; some Glory masters move
  between both worlds.
- Pro rift companies feed the ash grid. This creates prestige and public service,
  but also incentives to overhunt, hide risk, manipulate ash grades, or protect
  wealthy districts first.
- A sanctioned kill produces a yield. Civic power, casualty reserve, school, and
  team shares are removed in that order. Student shares fund training cells,
  metered combat techniques, equipment, travel, and medical reserve; they are not
  unmetered personal treasure.
- Ash is measured by **yield** (Handful, Flask, Barrel, Well), **grade** (purity and
  stability), and **resonance** (which patterns can use that spawn expression safely).
  A large dirty yield may be worth less than a small stable one.
- Killing does not grant rank automatically. Real combat can deepen capacity because
  the practitioner works at the edge of owned control and must later reproduce the
  lesson. Ash funds the next threshold; training makes the threshold survivable.
- Filter thinning increases both human potential and Riftspawn pressure. The opening
  book presents this first as unusually capable students and an unusually varied rift
  season. Later books prove the common Glory-pressure cause.

## First-season lasting cost

During the high-stakes regional sequence Kiva will permanently lose fine sensation
in the first two fingers of her left hand. Healing closes the injury but does not
restore exact touch. The cost is planted before it occurs and changes how she grips,
trains, and asks for help. Rank cannot erase it.
