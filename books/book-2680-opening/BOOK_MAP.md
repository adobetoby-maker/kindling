# Kindling 2680 — Book One map

Status: drafting map. Opening locks resolved 2026-10-08; prose run authorized.

## 1. Book promise

Kiva Rowan opened all three known doors at thirteen and spent four years classified
below what any one of them might have earned alone. In 2680 she enters Meridian's
collegiate competition system, the regional feeder for professional rift companies.
Across one season, she must turn breadth into reliable control, learn through school
combat, train through several connected chapters to kill Riftspawn and capture their
ash, keep friendships strained by ranking, yield shares, and recruitment, and answer
a real rift event where category matters less than whether the old Home Line method
still works. Beneath that season, Kiva's recurring Unroofed Sky fever dream shows
Glory users converging as the planetary filter disappears.

## 2. Ending promise

Kiva completes the collegiate season, earns a public place without receiving a
finished explanation, and deliberately combines the doors once under rules the
reader understands. The win costs something that cannot be reset by rank. The disk
is recognized as belonging to the old bearer line; a professional company makes a
bounded recruitment offer; and evidence from the emergency shows the filter trend is
accelerating. A second historical account confirms that part of Kiva's fever dream
predates her, without explaining it. Book One closes regionally. Book Two's national
competition, energy-industry conflict, and shared-prophecy problem are opened, not
substituted for the ending.

## 3. Numerical and structural shape

- One planned historical prologue precedes Chapter 1. It must establish the EMP,
  fallout, failed restoration, first Kindlings, ash's gradual industrial adoption,
  radiation-safe nuclear recovery, ash-nuclear synthesis, distributed reactors,
  flight, Glory industry, devoted school orders, and rebuilding through ten human
  scenes without answering the reserved cause of the Fall. Its final snapshot shows
  Kiva's anomalous Kindling and first Unroofed Sky fever dream at thirteen in 2676.
  Chapter One jumps four years to the 2680 collegiate season.
- Six movements, eight chapters each; 48 chapters working total.
- Working length 270,000–315,000 words, adjusted for actual scene need.
- Approximately 87% protagonist viewpoint/focus and 13% purposeful secondary
  focus across four or five characters.
- O'Connor numerical prose targets apply: 14.6-word mean, 11-word median, about
  27.7% sentences of five words or fewer, about 3.3% of forty words or more,
  short paragraphs, accessible approximately seventh-grade readability, and
  front-loaded progression teaching.
- Major fights receive developed tactical space. No required fight count replaces
  story need, but every movement has a physical test, field exercise, or deliberate
  quiet reason.

## 5. Progression design

### First third

Teach the three doors, rank/skill distinction, soul/ash limits, ash induction,
magic/ash blending, collegiate rules, professional recruitment, classification, and
institutional stakes aggressively. Make the earned-ash loop concrete: kill, contain,
recover, grade, divide, invest. Let failed attempts and demonstrations carry the
exposition. Kiva is not secretly powerful; breadth creates control problems, fatigue,
poor efficiency, and team risk before it becomes an advantage. The first supervised
spawn kills should occupy developed scenes across several chapters, including practice,
fear, tactics, dissolution, and recovery—not a montage or a single summary paragraph.

### Middle third

Reduce vocabulary repetition and let relationships, competition, and tactics carry
the system. Two-door use appears only after single-door discipline is visible.

### Final third

Surface rules at true turning points. The first intentional three-door sequence is
short, costly, and planted. It proves configuration, not mastery or Glory.
