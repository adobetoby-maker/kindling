## 4. The object chosen

**The leaning ninth post of the Home Line lane under Meridian's south wall** (the
"crooked post").

One-line justification: it is the only recurring object the plot already pays off
(the handle of a sluice gate, Ch. 43 and 48), it is canon-planned to survive, the
prologue already ends on it, and it needs nobody to write, copy, or explain anything.

Considered and rejected:
- The disk: the prologue's Snapshot 3 hearsay disk and Kiva's Satori disk are not
  confirmed to be the same object. Threading it through every era would assert that,
  against the plan's rule not to settle the disk's origin.
- The bowl and stones: a single demonstration in Snapshot 6; it does not persist.
- A wind-up watch: invented; no payoff.

Consequence: the post cannot exist before the Home Line, so Snapshots 1-3 and 5-9
carry no thread. The ticket allowed this ("remove the requirement ... in nearly every
era"). Snapshot 4 introduces it (reason spoken once), Snapshot 10 mocks it (reason
printed once, then lost), Snapshot 11 ends on it.

Inference to confirm at the prose pass: Snapshot 4's lane is placed under Meridian's
south wall because the recovered sheet in Ch. 43 says "At Meridian, under the south
wall, on the line of the hill drain." The existing draft says "north yard." The plan
follows the manuscript's later, load-bearing text. Also unresolved: who "S.V." is.
The initials come from Book Two Chapter 48; this plan does not name a person.
