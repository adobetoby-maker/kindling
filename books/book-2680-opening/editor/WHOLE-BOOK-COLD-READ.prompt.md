Read only the complete current manuscript, in this exact order:

1. `manuscript/prologue.md`
2. `manuscript/chapter-01.md` through `manuscript/chapter-48.md`

Read every file in full before judging. Do not read plans, maps, canon files, provenance,
authorship, frozen editions, prompts, movement reviews, repair reports, rechecks, git history,
or model labels. Searches may verify an observation after the full read but may not replace
coverage. State any context/coverage limitation honestly. This is a fresh-context simulated
cold read, not a real human audience test.

Respond to the novel as a whole:

- what the book seems to promise and whether the prologue helps;
- where attention catches, slips, and renews;

demographic research. Give concise 1–10 judgments for opening pull, keep-reading pressure,
clarity/flow, character attachment, action/suspense, progression payoff, connection, ending
payoff, and read-aloud quality, with exact chapter/scene evidence and confidence.

Finish with at most three high-leverage repair priorities. Do not propose broad shortening,
chapter-by-chapter polishing, or replacing the book's documentary voice. If the manuscript
needs no further required repair, say so and identify optional tastes separately.
