## Repair brief

Three priorities.

### 1. Make the climax trackable on one pass

- **Location:** ch 45 L2057–L2135 and L2337; ch 46 L2449, L2525–L2741; L3035.
- **Observed:** three things blur at the peak. The yard won't hold one shape (items 2 and 3 above: post numbering, the direction of the lean). Numbers collide: knots are called Eight and Seven beside Team Seven, drum twenty-eight, Team Twenty-Eight, a seal count of fifty-two and house fifty-two; "Finch has the eleventh" reads as an eleventh cloth before it resolves as the eleventh knot. Three counts run together in the carry, and the hand-off count disagrees with itself (item 4).
- **Effect on the reader:** at the best scene in the movement I stopped to redraw the map. A listener cannot stop.
- **Proposed scope:** anchoring words and punctuation only. Fix the post count and numbering once and keep to it. Say "the seventh knot", never "Seven", for a knot. Tag each count with its owner the first time it changes hands. Reconcile six, nine and ten. Do not shorten the scene.
- **Preserve:** all of the action as developed. The step tested first and finding the drain. "East's the drain" and the turn north. The catch that whoever has the corner must not be at the bar. Perrin minding it into the bar. The pump house in its entirety.

### 2. Take the repetition out of the aftermath, not the aftermath

- **Location:** ch 47 L3293–L3323 and L3437–L3491; ch 48 L3731–L3793; also ch 41 L383–L457.
- **Observed:** three full account read-outs in one movement. Most closing scenes restate the same lesson. Perrin's "I'd like it known" recurs about eleven times, three of them after its payoff at L2617–L2623. Noa's Saturday scene sits out of order (item 11).
- **Effect on the reader:** the thirteen-year-old drifts in the decimals. The adult feels the point being underlined. The excellent last scene arrives later than it should.
- **Proposed scope:** compress, don't delete. Reduce each read-out to its one human line plus the figure that matters. Keep one use of Perrin's phrase after ch 46. Move or date the Noa scene. If this movement closes the book, two chapters of resolution is a fair proportion, and the repair is to redundancy only.
- **Preserve:** Pru's finger on the page (L3157). "Three and a bit" once its count is right. The keeper's book. The cast and the plate. Dray's third line. Jude's dram for drum twenty-nine. Orla's card. The juniors' run and the final image.

### 3. Close the small logic gaps that touch the stakes

- **Location:** items 1, 12, 13 and 14 above (L535 and L787; L1723 and L2977; L2097–L2257; L2319 and L3303), then the remaining table items as a sweep.

- **Effect on the reader:** this movement's authority comes from its bookkeeping, which holds to the minute elsewhere. Each gap is a moment of doubt at a payoff: the seal-breaking, Sorn's forced idleness, "Lives: none lost".
- **Proposed scope:** one clause or sentence each. No new scenes.
- **Preserve:** the exactness itself, especially the timings of the night of the 5th, which check out everywhere I tested them.

The text guards against this (L3393: it took more than last time and returned it slower), and I think that is enough. I would not change the outcome and have not made it a priority.

- **Priority 2 depends on a fact I don't have:** whether movement six ends the book.
