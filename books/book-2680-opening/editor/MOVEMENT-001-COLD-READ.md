## 5. Repair brief — three priorities

### Priority 1 — Make the book's own numbers right

- **Location:** items 1–8, 13 and 17 above. Chiefly Ch4 L2687–2847 and L2945; Ch8 L4729; Ch4 L2607; Ch7 L4247; Ch1 L1463 and L1627; Ch8 L4705; Ch3 L2547.
- **Observed issue:** a score that does not reach three, two date slips, a reset time that contradicts the trip time, a draw comparison stated backwards, and two characters who know something a little too early.
- **Effect on the reader:** larger than in most books. The movement's argument is that a number written down by the person who measured it is the thing you can trust, and Kellan pulls the quench because Kiva's numbers "had been right all run." A reader who catches the referee miscounting stops trusting the page at the moment the page is asking for trust.
- **Proposed scope:** about ten single-line edits. For the ring, the lightest fix is to make the first touch land on the body for a full point (then "Renn leads, two," and "cost her a point" at L2707, and the book entry), or to change the last call so the bout is not yet won. No beat needs to be removed.
- **Strength to preserve:** the full length and order of the Terrace Ring bout, especially the two minutes of single-door defence and the riser. Do not shorten it to solve an addition error.

### Priority 2 — Let the reader see the Cellhouse climax on one pass

- **Location:** Ch5 L3229–3329 and L3383–3425; Ch6 L3459–3499 and L3697–3735 (items 9–12, 16, and the rail vault).
- **Observed issue:** Perrin's orientation in the doorway, the dummy's direction of exit, Kiva's view into a room beneath her feet, and which hand is on the drum all shift or go unstated.
- **Effect on the reader:** at the peak of the movement I stopped to redraw the room three times. A listener cannot go back at all.
- **Proposed scope:** clarifying sentences and punctuation only. Fix Perrin head-in or feet-in once and keep it. Give Kiva a sightline she could really have (his hands on the shutter edge and Anwen's voice would be enough). Name the hand on the drum and keep the other for the bar. Add half a line of reason for Kellan's vault. State the mezzanine height one way.
- **Strength to preserve:** every beat of the run at its present length: the declared role change, the joy, the cell keeping the fight, "I've stopped. It hasn't," over the rail, "one" under impact, Perrin holding, and Kellan's decision told from inside his own head.

### Priority 3 — Stop re-listing the run in the aftermath

- **Location:** Ch6 L3843–3855; Ch7 L3943–3947 and L4011–4041; Ch8 L4409–4461 and L4653–4675.
- **Observed issue:** five speakers walk through the same sequence (tuned, frightened, over the rail, held, called the quench, he believed the numbers) before giving the one judgment that is theirs.
- **Effect on the reader:** after the strongest chapter, momentum drops, and the new information in each scene (Ysra's hand on the stop, "responder," the disk) arrives behind a list the reader already knows. This is the main place the thirteen-year-old lens waits.
- **Proposed scope:** trim inside dialogue and inside Rhea's viewing only. Each scene keeps the judgment only that person can give and drops the shared list. No scene is removed, and no action is touched, since none of these are action scenes.
- **Strength to preserve:** Orla's "I'm not going to pick one" (L3951); Ysra's "I wanted to see whether your team could stop what you'd started" (L4453); Rhea's reading of the left hand that came up and threw no ward (L4665); the two cards.

## 6. Tradeoffs and uncertainty

- **The prologue is the largest reader risk I saw, and it is not in the repair brief on purpose.** It is 16,090 of about 58,200 words, and Kiva appears roughly 13,200 words in. The two lenses split hardest here (6 against 8). I left it out because any real repair is structural, the eleven-snapshot design appears deliberate, and the chapters depend on it: the seven-column books in Ch3, cut ash stamped First, the red handle, and the 2446 register entry all pay off prologue plants. If the owner wants a non-structural option, the cheapest is to let two or three of the ten "written entry" endings close on the image instead of the pen, so the pattern is felt and not predicted. That is an owner decision, and my confidence in the younger lens is only medium.
- **Terminology load is a second-order clarity cost.** "Ember" as door, rank and object, and "Kindled" as both a state and the lowest rank, will matter most in audio (for example L1597 and L4465). A gloss at first use of "Sustain" and "the filter" would help. I did not rank this above the three priorities.
- **The shared confessional register** ("I want that said," "say it out loud") is the movement's motif and also the reason several characters sound alike when they are being honest. Varying how two of them do it would keep the motif and restore the difference. Low confidence; another reader may hear it purely as a strength.
- **No run comparison was made.** Only one version was supplied. One movement does not show which seat is better across a series, and these scores should not be set beside scores from a review that used different coverage or a different rubric.
- **The scores are judgments.** I would expect another careful reader to land within a point on most rows and to disagree most on Opening pull and Progression payoff under the younger lens.
