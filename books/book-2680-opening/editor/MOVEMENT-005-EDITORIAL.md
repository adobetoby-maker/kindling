## 5. Repair brief (three priorities)

Nothing here asks for a scene to be cut, added or shortened. The lanes and the Pit keep their
length.

### Priority 1 — Make the Pit hold to its own stated facts

- **Location.** Ch 38, l.197–239 and l.359; ch 39, l.33–41, l.161–165, l.265–277, l.301,
  l.353, l.383–391; ch 40, l.87–91.
- **Observed.** The knot rate and budget do not fit the time the dialogue takes. One line of
  sight is thirty, forty and eighteen paces. The third rung's bag is left open. The page does
  not say where the wall stands once the corner is carried. The notch is timed to an instant
  when the floor is lit, and described as if it were dark. Perrin's "first weight" is not his
  first that day.
- **Effect.** This is the scene the whole book has trained its reader to audit: counts,
  paces, bags, who is lit. Each slip is small. Together they let a careful reader out of the
  moment the price is being read.
- **Scope.** Numerals and single clauses. Choose a knot rate the consent can afford and move
  the three sightings to match. Settle one distance. Give the third rung a seal. Say in a
  clause which side of the line the carriers are on. Either bring the lamps forward to the
  fall of the sheet, or let Noa and Adlam say the hair was on every pen, lit and dark alike,
  which is stronger evidence. Make Perrin's line true (the first person, not the first
  weight) and settle the date he was passed.
- **Preserve.** The order of the consent (limits, sponsor, healer, Chair, the direction with
  cost first, "I'm frightened… Then yes"). Every count in the catch. The beat left "on the
  other side of a shut door." Perrin's section with his back turned. Dray's "I can hold
  both."

### Priority 2 — Let a one-pass reader, and a listener, through the paper

- **Location.** Ch 33, l.3–81; ch 34, l.3–131; "the Works" at ch 34 throughout, ch 39,
  l.469–481, ch 40, l.13–15 and l.431; ch 34, l.87; ch 35, l.173; ch 38, l.163; ch 40, l.357.
- **Observed.** The movement opens on the room's customs before its stake. "The Works" names
  two bodies. A handful of referents are ambiguous when heard ("from Thursday"; "Tuesday,
  isn't it? The sixth"; "at the end"; "a first-year with wax on her back").
- **Effect.** The younger lens loses the first scene and the hearing; the adult lens loses
  the thread of who owns the bank that the next movement turns on.
- **Scope.** Reorder inside the first scene so the reader knows a recall can follow before
  the offices are explained; no new material. Qualify "the Works" wherever it is not Halden,
  or wherever it is. Name the referent in the four small places.
- **Preserve.** "I thought the book was what we were for." The two votes written so that she

  Cobb.

## 6. Tradeoffs and uncertainty

The largest uncertainty is the unmeasured formula alignment: sentence rhythm, reading level
and progression density could each hold a material departure that this pass cannot see. The
scores for the younger lens are the least certain; the book's understatement and name load
may cost that reader more, or its humor may carry more, than judged here. The elders' shared
voice and the periphrasis are noted as weaknesses and deliberately left out of the brief:
they are the register of the whole book, and a bounded repair is the wrong place to change
it. Chapter 40's long coda is likewise left alone; each of its scenes pays something owed.
One movement, reviewed once by its author's own model, does not settle how the book reads.
