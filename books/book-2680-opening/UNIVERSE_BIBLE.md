# Kindling 2680 — reconciled planning bible

Status: drafting bible. `INHERITED` means established in the present Kindled canon.
`LOCKED 2680` marks the opening-book choices delegated by the owner on 2026-10-08.
This file does not silently promote the older storyboard's claims.

## Timeline

- **2080 — Fall (`INHERITED`):** stratospheric detonations cause the visible EMP
  collapse; their relationship to filter damage remains uncertain.
- **~2090 onward (`INHERITED`):** Mars-recovered disk activation, unstable
  wormholes, Riftspawn, and human Kindling begin.
- **2150 (`INHERITED`):** the present Kindled trilogy and first Home Line.
- **2680 (`PLANNED`):** six-hundredth anniversary of the Fall; roughly 530 years of
  institutional growth from Meridian's first lanes.

## Power invariants

- One underlying source is approached through three known traditions: Ember,
  rift-granted magic, and disciplined internal cultivation.
- The soul is what fills. Ash is ownerless fuel left by Riftspawn; it is finite.
- Kindled → Ember → Flame → Fire → Torch → Blaze → Glory remains the human rank
  ladder. Skill/control and rank/breadth remain separate axes.
- Glory stops ordinary aging through the shedding mechanism already recorded, but
  reaching it is rare and cannot be granted, bought, or stolen.
- Glory also allows a master to hold larger, longer, and more intricate ashcraft
  patterns. It improves throughput, stability, duration, and precision; it does not
  make ash infinite or remove the need for equipment, workers, meters, and shutdowns.
- Satori is both realization and the disk name. The disk's true origin is never
  explained in-manuscript.
- Riftspawn expressions echo worked powers without a person home to direct them.
- The filter may be failing, but characters distinguish a reading, a theory, a myth,
  and a proof.

## Combat progression, Riftspawn, and earned ash (`LOCKED 2680`)

The visible progression engine joins student combat, field service, and the ash
economy. It should be experienced repeatedly on the page rather than explained once.

1. **Train:** discipline schools teach body mechanics, door control, team roles,
   spawn anatomy, terrain, containment, quench procedure, casualty extraction, and
   ash recovery. Several Book One chapters must show students learning specifically
   how to kill Riftspawn, not merely how to duel other students.
2. **Contest:** school teams fight one another in the singles, brackets, and mixed-role
   lanes. A human opponent adapts, remembers, and exposes habits that a spawn does not.
3. **Hunt or respond:** students graduate through licenses from contained Handful
   releases to supervised live rifts. Riftspawn are dangerous animals or expressions,
   not training dummies. A clean kill still leaves containment and rescue work.
4. **Recover:** defeated Riftspawn dissolve into finite ownerless ash. The team must
   catch, quarantine, grade, and reconcile it before it can be used. Contaminated or
   badly captured ash can poison a cell, attract another rift, or injure an operator.
5. **Invest:** the public authority takes its civic and casualty shares; the school,
   team, and licensed fighters receive their yield shares. Earned ash pays for
   training cells, equipment, travel, medical reserve, and harder sanctioned entries.
6. **Advance:** rank is not an automatic reward for a kill. The soul grows through
   repeated owned exertion, correction, recovery, and reproducible control. Ash lets a
   student attempt more difficult work, but cannot purchase judgment or replace what
   the soul has learned.

This produces two linked ladders: personal capability (rank, Hold, Draw, control,
and Call) and team yield (clean ash recovered after rescue, closure, and public shares).
Kills alone say little and are a persistent source of institutional abuse,
but kills and usable ash must remain materially important to students and companies.
The readouts are system flavor, said where a character would say them; nobody counts,
times, or tallies as a habit, and no scene is settled by a figure
(`universe/SEED-2680.md` §11).

**Draw** is efficiency: ash spent on a defined piece of work divided by the governing
book or bench expectation. `1.0` is standard and lower is more efficient. The expected
cost is task-specific: a contested four-minute hold has a book figure of twelve
hundredths of a dram, so a hold that spends 0.112 reads as `0.93` (0.112 / 0.12). That is
how Kellan's 0.93 is reached; it is a readout, said once, not a scene.

Recovered ash is described three ways. **Yield** is quantity on the
Handful -> Flask -> Barrel -> Well ladder. **Grade** is purity and stability;
fraudulently stamped ash can kill an operator even when the quantity is correct.
**Resonance** is the defeated spawn's expression and therefore which training,
medical, containment, transit, or industrial patterns can use the ash efficiently.
A rare yield is valuable because of what it can safely fund, not because it grants the
killer a packaged new power. Student teams carry metered combat ash for permitted
techniques and equipment, so decisions about spending or saving an earned share have
visible consequences in the next match and the next rift.

### Filter escalation

As the planetary filter thins, more Glory pressure reaches the world. Prepared humans
can hold stronger, stranger, and more blended expressions, so each generation appears
more naturally gifted. The same pressure also gives rifts more available pattern:
spawn arrive more often, express greater variety, coordinate or adapt in unfamiliar
ways, and reach higher yield classes. Early institutions read the two trends
separately—an exceptional collegiate cohort and a bad rift season. The series gradually
proves they are one escalation. More ambient Glory creates opportunity, not free power:
untrained people overload, cells drift, doors interfere, and stronger spawn kill anyone
who mistakes new capacity for mastery.

## The Unroofed Sky (`LOCKED SERIES PROPHECY`)

The series uses a recurring fever dream, not a reliable spoken prophecy. Across
centuries, a small number of people have dreamed overlapping fragments. Later schools
call the pattern **the Unroofed Sky**; the Order of the Even Hand holds its own master's
version as doctrine and calls it *the Unroofing*.

- The dream shows Glory-level people arriving from different directions and
  traditions until their fields touch. Whether they gather to prevent the break,
  cause it, claim it, or survive it is not revealed.
- Above them, the filter does not crack wider. It disappears. The planet stands in
  unmediated **Glory presence**: immense, intelligent-feeling pressure and possibility,
  but not a speaking god and not automatically hostile.
- Every ash cell, reactor lattice, living door, and rift responds at once. Some
  systems brighten, some fail, some change category. The dream never supplies a
  technical solution.
- Kiva's three-door Kindling includes the clearest known version, apparently carried
  or amplified by the Satori disk. The disk may preserve earlier contact, respond to
  future resonance, or both. Book One does not settle which.
- The vision recurs in pieces as Kiva advances. She cannot summon it for answers.
  Physical fever, ash overload, filter instability, or close contact with Glory can
  trigger it, and each recurrence has a bodily cost.
- Independent Glory masters and older tellings eventually prove Kiva is not the
  only dreamer. Industrial houses, school orders, professional companies, nations,
  and religious movements interpret the pattern differently.
- Book Two puts the dreamers in one room and makes the dream a national/international
  political fact. Book Three brings the Glory users together and fulfills the fixed
  event: complete planetary filter loss and enduring pure Glory presence. The
  characters' choices determine what that collision becomes.

The fixed ending is the loss of the filter, not a predetermined winner, savior,
battle, or moral interpretation. Do not use the dream to excuse unearned power or
replace evidence, training, logistics, and choice.

## Meridian after 530 years (`PLANNED`)

Meridian has grown from hardened medical enclave into a regional learning and
response center. The recommended institutional shape is one campus/compact with
several traditions, not a single harmonious academy:

1. **Crooked Line School (`LOCKED 2680`):** mixed-door field method descended from
   Toren, Rook, and the declared-role lane. It preserves method rather than turning
   a founder's name into a correctness claim.
2. **Stonehand Hall (`LOCKED 2680`):** Resilience/body discipline, contact, load,
   safe failure, and recovery.
3. **Wren House (`LOCKED 2680`):** Ward, Sustain, medicine, ash depth,
   ash engineering, rotation, radiation recall, and abort-line ethics.
4. **Home Line (`PLANNED` retained name):** non-chartered or semi-chartered field
   response. It tests whether school methods survive real terrain.

Together they operate through **the Meridian Compact**. Their sanctioned collegiate
season is **the Meridian Collegiate Circuit**, ending in **the Regional Gates**.
It is a major public institution with teams, scholarships, standings, recruiters,
broadcast coverage, and real consequences. Events test dueling, declared-role lanes,
rescue/extraction, ashcraft, controlled Riftspawn response, and licensed live-yield
work. The schools function partly as colleges, partly as discipline orders, and partly
as military-response academies. Students train to fight people without killing them
and to kill spawn without losing the ash, their team, or the civilians behind them.

The collegiate system feeds **chartered rift companies**: professional salaried teams
licensed to answer live rifts, fight Riftspawn, rescue civilians, protect
infrastructure, and recover clean ash. Public standings reward closure, lives saved,
damage prevented, usable ash, and violations. Home Line remains the professional
arm most suspicious of spectacle and most insistent that the cost be said aloud, at the
cart, before anyone goes home.

Callie's boundary practice and the habit of asking *why* after a drill are embedded
across these institutions rather than requiring a fourth founder cult.

## Technology and travel

- Mature hardened technology, medical/Ember synthesis, reliable regional
  communications, and ash-electric infrastructure are `LOCKED 2680`.
- A rebuilt network connects established regional wormhole stations on schedules.
  Transit is costly, capacity-limited, and vulnerable to rift conditions.
- Interplanetary/intergalactic normality is not part of the opening book.
- Classification instruments read door behavior through physical rigs, projected
  tables, and accountable human rulings. There is no retinal HUD or private status
  overlay.

## Ashcraft and electrical power (`LOCKED 2680`)

Centuries of work have turned ash from carried healer fuel into the rebuilt world's
principal finite energy commodity.

- **Ash induction:** refined ash sits in a warded cell. A trained operator impresses
  a stable magical pattern on it; the induction assembly converts the controlled
  release into ordinary electrical current. Once tuned, a modern cell runs without
  continuous soul expenditure, but never without finite fuel, maintenance, metering,
  or risk.
- **Magic supplies form; ash supplies fuel.** A soul cannot be replaced by ash because
  ash has no owner, judgment, or intent. Ash cannot safely do complex work without a
  pattern held by a person or preserved in a carefully built device.
- **Traditions remain distinct:** Edge-derived work defines conductive paths and
  precise release; Ward-derived work contains reaction and redirects force;
  cultivated/internal work holds resonance, distributes load, and prevents collapse.
  Composite devices normally require teams or inherited patterns. Three-door access
  gives Kiva possibility, not instant mastery.
- **Uses:** lighting, water and waste pumps, heating, refrigeration, communications,
  medical machinery, tools, transit, station gates, arenas, and rift sensing.
  Critical services retain mechanical and hardened backups.
- **Failure modes:** contaminated ash, false grading, pattern drift, runaway heat,
  operator backwash, grid damage, and rift attraction. Every serious device has a
  meter, a shutdown path, and a hand that answers for it.
- **Glory scale:** a Glory master can stabilize industrial patterns that lesser
  practitioners can only maintain in teams or for short intervals. This has produced
  powerful industrial houses—titans whose refineries, grids, transit works, and
  professional companies shape governments—as well as near-monastic school orders
  devoted to perfecting one discipline across a lifetime.
- Industry and devotion are not clean moral opposites. Some houses exploit schools;
  some fund public infrastructure; some orders hoard knowledge; some resist monopoly;
  and a few Glory masters belong credibly to both worlds.
- **Geographic ladder:** Book One remains regional around Meridian. Book Two widens
  to national circuits, intercity grids, and company charters. Book Three becomes
  international as rival grids, schools, Glory masters, and rift companies must
  cooperate under accelerating filter failure.

## Energy revolution after Meridian (`LOCKED 2680 HISTORY`)

The decisive technological leap occurred during the first century after the first
Home Line, then compounded for more than four hundred years.

1. **Ash as emergency fuel:** Meridian-era practitioners used ash mainly for healing,
   training, lamps, and survival work. Its finite nature and dangerous grading were
   understood before its industrial potential.
2. **Radiation return:** Sustain-trained Kindled learned to remove active radiation
   burden from living workers while medicine treated exposure and fixed damage.
   They could enter zones ordinary crews could not, bring out components and the
   plants' own drawings, and work in short, timed rotations. This never made
   radiation harmless or
   reversed tissue already destroyed.
3. **Nuclear recovery:** engineers combined ordinary shielding, remote machinery,
   disciplined rotations, ash containment patterns, and Glory-scale oversight to
   reopen surviving nuclear sites and build safer compact reactors. Ward does not
   move radiation; the breakthrough is containment, control, monitoring, and safe
   human access—not a quiet rewrite of the old power rules.
4. **Tamar Vey's question:** an itinerant Home Line teacher and storyteller visits
   an early recovery workshop whose engineers keep trying to make ash burn longer.
   With an ordinary bowl, a conductor, a pinch of ash, and a short-held
   pattern, Tamar demonstrates that ash can preserve an instruction after the
   operator releases it. The lesson is conceptual: the reactor's fire already knows
   how to make heat; perhaps ash should hold the rules around the fire. Tamar does
   not design the reactor or hand civilization a finished device. Engineers spend
   years turning that question into safe, reproducible work.
5. **Ash-nuclear synthesis:** ash patterns stabilize reaction geometry, manage heat
   and load, and improve electrical conversion. Nuclear fuel supplies dependable
   baseline energy; ash supplies control, high-density response, and compactness.
   Both fuels remain finite. Waste and failure still matter.
6. **Distributed power:** licensed Glory-impressed patterns allow factory-built
   small reactors and ash cells to run homes, buildings, farms, hospitals, and
   workshops without a Glory master standing beside each one. Local technicians
   maintain them; the Works meter fuel and answer for failures.
7. **Displacement:** within roughly a century of Meridian, ash induction and compact
   nuclear-ash systems displaced fossil fuels and large solar fields as primary
   energy. Legacy wind, water, solar, combustion, and mechanical systems remain as
   local backups rather than vanishing from knowledge.
8. **Flight and advanced equipment:** ash-electric lift craft, personal lift rigs for
   suitably trained Kindled, powered armor/tools, precision medicine, climate control,
   high-speed fabrication, and hardened communications become ordinary enough to
   reshape cities. Personal flight remains skill- and fuel-limited, not universal.
9. **Far-future society:** four more centuries of refinement make 2680 materially
   beyond most pre-Fall dreams. Its abundance is real but uneven. Grid access, clean
   ash, reactor licensing, professional protection, and Glory-pattern ownership are
   central political and class questions.

Tamar's role is remembered unevenly. Some accounts make him an inventor; others call
him a fraud who happened to ask the right question. Canonically he is mortal, human,
original to this world, and catalytic rather than omniscient. The science belongs to
the generations who test, fail, and build after him.

## Historical artifacts

Story devices tagged `LOCKED 2680` below were provisional until the owner approved them on
2026-10-28.

- **The Satori disk (`INHERITED` → `LOCKED 2680 survival`):** nine grooves; released
  through an appointed bearer line, not blood descent. It warms once after Kiva's
  first correct deliberate cross-door sequence. It does not teach or speak.
- **The crooked post (`PLANNED survival`):** the series' one durable physical thread.
  The ninth post of the Home Line lane under Meridian's south wall, with a bar through
  its top. The School set the other posts straight in 2390 and could not shift this
  one. Its working function is a Movement 6 reveal (the handle of a sluice gate on the
  hill drain; see `STATE_LEDGER.md`). It is the only object the 2680 prologue threads
  across eras: a working lane in 2152, a joke in 2603, the last image of 2676.
- **Orla's debrief (`LOCKED 2680`):** after a drill Orla asks her student, aloud at the
  lane: *What went wrong. Who was hurt. What it cost. Second go. Why.* The Home Line asks
  the same at the cart. Coaching, not ceremony. Nobody writes the answers.
- **The saying (`LOCKED 2680`):** *Because the road isn't straight.* A flat spoken answer to
  "why does it lean." Spoken three times in Book One, never printed, no lore around it.
- **The Order's belief (`LOCKED 2680`):** the Even Hand believes the sky will come off and
  trains one door for a lifetime to be standing when it does. Its Warden says what Annick
  saw, in Annick's images, to people the Order wants. The Halden house claims Annick's
  Ward patterns.
- **The watch of seven (`LOCKED 2680`):** the Home Line's custom for the plate. A bearer
  names the next before the watch and says why, aloud; the watch witnesses and does not
  choose, and can recall the plate on a vote for cause said before it. It sits twice in
  Book One. The Ferren, its oldest officer, sat the watch in 2669.
- **Lumb (`LOCKED 2680`):** the Ferren pump house keeper had the dream himself, forty years
  ago, and says so once.

## Filter and Glory horizon

The filter's failure remains a theory supported by increasingly abnormal rifts and
generation-scale readings. Book One proves acceleration, not cosmology. If the
2680 trilogy later reaches full integration, Glory becomes minimum viable contact
with the larger realm rather than a final victory. Populated worlds and the next
ladder remain `HORIZON` until the owner approves their concrete form.

## Guardrails

- Do not bring a present Kindled character onstage as an immortal unless the owner
  separately approves that character having reached Glory.
- Do not turn disputed founder history into a secret villain rewrite.
- Do not make the protagonist powerful because the disk grants finished skill.
- Institutions keep records; the story does not. Owner rulings, 2026-10-27: *"i dont
  like all the write it down. record it talk inside a book. it annoys me"* and *"No
  wiring to ledger talk. Just the story."* No scene is built on, or resolved by, someone
  writing, copying, filing, shelving, or reading a document; no character explains why
  records matter; and no scene's point is that something was kept, passed on, or handed
  down. What the plot needs happens as story (`BOOK_MAP.md` §8; `universe/SEED-2680.md`
  §6).
- Readouts are flavor; counting is not a motif. Owner ruling, 2026-10-27: *"look to remove
  counting as a motif"*, *"only cut the tics."* Hold, Draw, Call, rank, Grade, and yield
  class stay as the system's vocabulary, said where a character would say them. No
  character counts as a trait, a comfort, or a method of control; nobody "keeps the
  count"; time in a fight is not given in "counts"; no beat is resolved by a tally, a
  timing, a reading, or a sum; no number word (*nine*, *eleven*) recurs as a refrain. The
  post is a gate found by a call, a feel, a sound, and a float that does not arrive, not by
  a count (`universe/SEED-2680.md` §6.1, §11).
- Do not copy source-novel names, terminology, scene order, signature dialogue, or
  character analogs from the research.
- Every climax capability must be demonstrated and priced earlier.
