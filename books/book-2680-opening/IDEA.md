# 2680 opening book — planning idea

Working series label: **Kindling 2680**. Book title is unset.

Status: drafting concept. The owner delegated the continuing names and opening locks
on 2026-10-08.

## Calendar

- The Fall: 2080.
- Present-day Kindled trilogy: begins 2150.
- This book: 2680, the six-hundredth anniversary of the Fall and approximately
  530 years after Callie, Jab, Toren, Rook, and the first Home Line.

## One-sentence promise

Four years after thirteen-year-old Kiva Rowan opened all three known doors at once,
the six-hundredth anniversary of the Fall brings her into Meridian's collegiate
competition system—a feeder for professional rift companies and an ash-powered
civilization built to classify every path except the one its founders preserved.

## Reader promise

Collegiate progression, a clear training system, friendships and rivalry, longer
learning fights, public team competition, and an old mystery made personal. Above
the collegiate circuit are professional rift companies whose fighters defend cities,
close breaches, and recover the ash that powers modern life. Kiva improves through
practice and loss, not destiny shortcuts. The first book closes a full collegiate
season and belonging arc while revealing that filter instability is accelerating.
Her recurring fever dream—the Unroofed Sky—shows Glory users converging as the
planetary filter disappears, but never tells her whether their collision saves the
world, breaks it, or merely determines what survives afterward.

## Prologue promise

Through the protagonist's Kindling, a substantial scene-led prologue carries a cold
reader from the 2080 EMP through immediate fallout, the failed restoration decade,
the first wormholes/Riftspawn/Kindlings, and the centuries of reconstruction that
produce ash-electric Meridian. It follows ash from scarce healer fuel through
radiation-safe recovery work, reopened nuclear facilities, compact ash-nuclear
reactors, household power, flight, and a society beyond pre-Fall expectations. Its
final snapshot is Kiva's anomalous Kindling and first Unroofed Sky fever dream at
thirteen in 2676; Chapter One opens four years later, at the start of the 2680
collegiate season. It uses emotionally anchored snapshots
and a recurring physical thread rather than a summarized history lecture. It makes
the visible EMP and social consequences understandable while preserving the unknown
source of the detonations and the reserved filter cosmology.

The prologue receives its own plan in `PROLOGUE_PLAN.md`. It does not count as one
of the six eight-chapter movements, and no prose is authorized yet.

## Relationship to Kindled

Book Three's ending becomes history, not required reading homework. Readers of
Kindled recognize the crooked lane, reasons ledger, Home Line, and Satori. New
readers receive them as present-day institutions and mysteries. Historical accounts
may be incomplete or wrong, but they cannot contradict the surviving records merely
to manufacture a twist.

## What survives from the older 2680 proposal

- A protagonist whose Kindling opens all three known traditions.
- The inherited/released nine-grooved disk.
- Meridian as institutional and geographic home.
- Competitive training as the visible progression engine.
- A failing filter, rifts as advance symptoms, and Glory as an entry threshold
  rather than a comfortable final ceiling.
- A default single-lead viewpoint with a small set of purposeful cutaways.

## Opening choices now locked

- Kiva Rowan is a girl living with her aunt and guardian, Mara Rowan.
- The disk passes by appointed bearer line rather than blood descent.
- The Meridian Compact contains the Crooked Line School, Stonehand Hall, Wren House,
  and the field-facing Home Line.
- The colleges compete regionally in Book One and feed licensed professional rift
  companies. Books Two and Three widen through national and international circuits.
- Ash induction supplies most electrical power. Magic patterns control and shape
  ash; ash provides finite fuel. At Glory, rare masters can sustain industrial-scale
  patterns, creating both titans of industry and monastic school lineages.
- Nuclear-ash synthesis emerged after Sustain-trained workers could enter radiation
  zones in measured rotations. Factory-built compact reactors now power homes and
  institutions; ash-electric flight and advanced equipment are normal but metered.
- The complete loss of the planetary filter and arrival of pure Glory presence are
  fixed for Book Three. The Unroofed Sky predicts the convergence, not its moral
  meaning or winner.
- Regional scheduled wormhole travel exists; interplanetary normality does not.
- Classification is physical and publicly interpreted; there is no retinal overlay.

## What remains deliberately unresolved

- The exact timing of the filter's full break. The opening book only proves
  acceleration and an approaching threshold.
- Any returning immortal from the present Kindled cast.
