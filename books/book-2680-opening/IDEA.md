# 2680 opening book — planning idea

Working series label: **Kindling 2680**. Book title is unset.

Status: drafting concept. The owner delegated the continuing names and opening locks
on 2026-10-08.

## Calendar

- The Fall: 2080.
- Present-day Kindled trilogy: begins 2150.
- This book: 2680, the six-hundredth anniversary of the Fall and approximately
  530 years after Callie, Jab, Toren, Rook, and the first Home Line.

## One-sentence promise

On the six-hundredth anniversary of the Fall, thirteen-year-old Kiva Rowan, appointed
bearer of an old
nine-grooved disk opens all three known doors at once and enters a Meridian training
system built to classify every path except the one its own founders were trying to
preserve.

## Reader promise

Competitive progression, a clear training system, friendships and rivalry, longer
learning fights, and an old mystery made personal. The protagonist improves through
practice and loss, not destiny shortcuts. The first book closes a full competitive
and belonging arc while revealing that filter instability is accelerating.

## Prologue promise

Before the protagonist's Kindling, a substantial scene-led prologue carries a cold
reader from the 2080 EMP through immediate fallout, the failed restoration decade,
the first wormholes/Riftspawn/Kindlings, and the centuries of reconstruction that
produce Meridian in 2680. It uses a small number of emotionally anchored snapshots
and a recurring physical thread rather than a summarized history lecture. It makes
the visible EMP and social consequences understandable while preserving the unknown
source of the detonations and the reserved filter cosmology.

The prologue receives its own plan in `PROLOGUE_PLAN.md`. It does not count as one
of the six eight-chapter movements, and no prose is authorized yet.

## Relationship to Kindled

Book Three's ending becomes history, not required reading homework. Readers of
Kindled recognize the crooked lane, reasons ledger, Home Line, and Satori. New
readers receive them as present-day institutions and mysteries. Historical accounts
may be incomplete or wrong, but they cannot contradict the surviving records merely
to manufacture a twist.

## What survives from the older 2680 proposal

- A protagonist whose Kindling opens all three known traditions.
- The inherited/released nine-grooved disk.
- Meridian as institutional and geographic home.
- Competitive training as the visible progression engine.
- A failing filter, rifts as advance symptoms, and Glory as an entry threshold
  rather than a comfortable final ceiling.
- A default single-lead viewpoint with a small set of purposeful cutaways.

## Opening choices now locked

- Kiva Rowan is a girl living with her aunt and guardian, Mara Rowan.
- The disk passes by appointed bearer line rather than blood descent.
- The Meridian Compact contains the Crooked Line School, Stonehand Hall, Wren House,
  and the field-facing Home Line.
- Regional scheduled wormhole travel exists; interplanetary normality does not.
- Classification is physical and publicly interpreted; there is no retinal overlay.

## What remains deliberately unresolved

- The exact timing of the filter's full break. The opening book only proves
  acceleration and an approaching threshold.
- Any returning immortal from the present Kindled cast.

## Drafting constraints

- Public byline Monroe Jackson; internal O'Connor 1.3.0 seat.
- Claude Opus 5.5 is the later default author unless the owner changes it.
- Six movements of eight chapters is the working structure.
- Default O'Connor POV formula resumes: approximately 87% protagonist focus and
  approximately 13% brief secondary focus across four or five people.
- Numerical sentence, paragraph, readability, progression-density, development,
  and action targets from the O'Connor formula are active.
- The prologue and first continuous eight-chapter prose run are authorized.
