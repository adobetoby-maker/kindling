It is Monday the 26th of the ninth month, 2680. The Regional is over and the official
result stands: Halden License first, Old Four second, Twenty-Eight third, Team Seven
fourth and still attached. Kiva's three-door sequence is publicly real but uncertified,
explicitly not to be asked of her, and medically barred before the 23rd. Her first two
left fingers bend, grip, and report pressure but have permanently lost fine sensation.
They cannot tell edge from flat, hot from cold, or one thread from two. Pike has begun
teaching her to name the handoff before she fumbles. No victory, new rank, ash, device,
or last-chapter miracle may restore that sensation.

The Pit emergency produced a held four-post spawn square around a tear, stepped ash, the
second T-0 on the dead ring, and sixteen simultaneous pen notches about a dozen seconds after
Kiva's second cliff. The hold was not designed for the competition. No one knows why it
formed or why every lamp and pen answered. Kiva's public action was a bounded sequence
under a struck floor and written field direction, with the price read first. Students
cut posts, held the gate, carried the casualty, and bagged their rungs; professionals
closed the tear. Keep that division of responsibility honest.

The immediate civic threat is already on the page. The Ferren pump bank—twenty-four
municipal cells—fell into step during the Pit hold and has remained one coupled engine
since. The pumps still run. The low streets are dry. That apparent success is
dangerous because one drifting or fouled cell can now take the whole bank with it. The
Step is walking the Brickfields trunk through open links at about one drum every two
days: drums nineteen, twenty, and twenty-one are already coupled, and at that pace the
Crooked Line School's core house is eighteen days off. Nobody knows how to take a bank out of
step. The Home Line has suspended licensing of held tears. Bells at mast five, the
weigh-house, and the weir have already begun.

Team Seven's book holds 5.62 drams, plus 2.0 sealed medical ash that has never been
opened. Their Pit allocation was reconciled. Their red-tagged rung remains stepped and
under Kiva's tag. Kellan has not answered Halden's offer of its winter yard. Anwen has
earned a summer relief place on Mace's night-van ward line. Perrin is passed to lift and
is trying his Hall's plate. Pru owns the cord when Kiva says so. Jude has six drams by
name and a real Kill line. Selka Dray remains a rival who is unpersuaded and fair. Rhea
has made no offer. Every one of these people must retain an independent skill, desire,
and future.

The disk remains cold and non-instructional. The Home Line watch has sat twice (Kiva
before it in Movement 4; the recall vote, two to five against, in Movement 5) and does not
sit again in this book; the plate is held of the Line and borne by K. Rowan. The Order of
the Even Hand's Warden has told Kiva what Annick saw, as the Order's belief and as the
reason it wants her for a year; it shares the roads, unlike lights, touching, absent sky,
*Full*, attention, lamps answering, and the nameless color, but gives no date, cause, or
tactic. The Order's offer is on the latch. Kiva has had no new fever or dream since the
Pit. Nobody has proved the filter theory, the cause of the Fall, the disk's origin, or the
meaning of the Unroofed Sky.

The post is found out by a drill, a float, and two hands on a bar, not by a manual or a
sheet. Kiva has stood on the cross in the still door all summer and felt nothing but
ground; she thinks the post leans *toward* something. Mara, walking the Ferren main uphill
from the pump house for the Works, drops a float at each manhole and watches for it at the
next; above the School's gate, where the Works' plan shows the main running straight, the
float never comes, and she cannot say where the main goes. Vane
runs Seven dark through the Old Yard lane as a Home Line drill whose call is *call the
turn before the eighth; say who has the bar*; Perrin takes the bar because the call says
so and asks why the post leans, and Vane says *because the road isn't straight*. Kiva,
planted at the ninth, feels water move under it. Two to the bar; the screw turns until the
bar comes up hard against silt in the groove; the shaft answers with a sound the whole yard
hears, and under Kiva's hands the water falters rather than lying still; at the manhole
below the gate Mara's reading hand feels the rise and fall falter. *It's a gate.* The gate
is found, not shut: the penstock stays hanging on its screw (Ch. 44), silt jams it (Ch. 44),
fitters work it from Tuesday (Ch. 45), and Perrin and Lowen run it home in Ch. 46. Nobody
counts anything: the gate is found by a call, a feel, a sound, and a float that does not
arrive

## What this movement is for

Finish Book One, not merely its next incident. The eight chapters must form one
continuous civic-emergency arc with entry pressure, preparation, failed assumptions,
escalation, a developed multi-role climax, recovery, and a genuine regional ending.
Resolve the collegiate season, this book's ash accounts, Kiva's place, her relationship to
her team and sponsor, the immediate stepped-grid danger, and the emotional meaning of
the crooked method. Leave national and international problems open for later books,
but do not make Book One feel interrupted halfway through a scene.

The core physical story is the Step walking toward the Crooked Line core house while a
new, higher-yield Riftspawn expression forms along the same induction/rift boundary.
Do not simply repeat the Pit square or enlarge a Flask. Let the new expression obey
known anatomy—breath, posts or load paths, hem, cord, tear, closure, ash grade and
containment—while combining or redirecting those rules in a way the trained teams can
learn and fight. Its behavior must arise from the coupled ash grid and the unstable
boundary, not from a villain pressing a switch. Human potential and spawn strength are
both exceeding recent records on the mast and in the field; characters may establish correlation and
acceleration, not final cause.

The students and professionals need time to prepare. Use developed scenes rather than
a briefing montage: map the trunk; test isolation and quench sequences; rehearse
school evacuation, casualty routes, meter calls, clean-ash capture, and the named
handoffs Kiva's changed hand requires; run a contained fight or controlled drill that
reveals one bad assumption before the real event. The earlier training must become the
response plan: declared aborts, kill roles, fold, catch/carry/plant, the Bench, shutdown
practice, clean recovery, tape/pen reading, and the difference between a result and a
reason. Let the students make choices and errors rather than merely stand behind adult
experts.

The climax must be substantial, spatially trackable, and shared. Kiva may deliberately
sequence hand, Ember, and still once when the page has earned it, under new authority
and with a cost/risk stated before the action. That sequence stabilizes or redirects a
boundary long enough for the response to work; it does not kill everything, close the
tear, solve the grid, or turn her into a Glory master. Professionals own professional
closure. Kellan, Anwen, Perrin, Pru, Jude, Noa, Selka, Lowen, Rhea, Mara, Orla, Pike,
Vane, and the relevant workers must own developed tasks that cannot be replaced by
Kiva: kill, wall, carry, rescue, mast reading, shutdown, communication, triage, clean
capture, or the decision not to spend ash. Rival competence matters.

Let the crisis force a real ash decision. The sealed 2.0 medical reserve is not casual
fuel. If it is opened, it must be for a shown medical necessity and entered as such; if
it remains sealed, show what else is sacrificed. Every dram spent, fouled, recovered,
returned, or credited must reconcile. The red-tagged stepped rung is evidence, not free
power. Do not let abundant climax yield erase scarcity or the cost already paid.

The emotional line is Kiva learning that being the catcher does not mean doing every
part. Her permanent hand change should matter tactically and privately. She must name a
handoff early enough, accept help without turning the moment into a speech, and still
make an irreplaceable judgment. Let Mara's technical knowledge, Orla's accountable
sponsorship, Rhea's field authority, and the younger team's earned autonomy disagree
before they align. Kellan's Halden future, Anwen's night-van future, Perrin's Hall place,
Jude's yield line, and Pru's emerging craft should remain futures of their own rather
than rewards Kiva grants.

## The ending that must land

Give the emergency a complete answer: people rescued, spawn killed or contained by
named hands, tear closed by the licensed crew, grid made safe by a method the reader can
follow, clean ash accounted for, and the school district still standing. The solution
may be costly or provisional at regional scale, but it cannot be “wait for Book Two.”

Close the regional season and book with these earned developments:

- Kiva receives a public place based on judgment, repeatable control, and service—not a
  surprise power rank. Her three-door configuration remains real, bounded, costly, and
  unmastered.
- A professional company makes a **bounded** offer: training, observation, reserve work,
  or a future seat with written limits. It is not an instant elite contract and Kiva may
  accept, decline, or condition it in character.
- A credible professional, reader, or Home Line witness recognizes the disk as part
  of the old appointed-bearer line. Recognition clarifies custody and history only. The
  disk remains cold and supplies no answer or tactic.
- A second independent historical line or account confirms one limited element of the
  Unroofed Sky imagery and establishes that the shared phenomenon is older and wider
  than Kiva. It must not become a reliable spoken prophecy or explain who the converging
  figures are.
- Instrument and field evidence shows that both prepared humans and Riftspawn are
  becoming stronger or more varied as the same trend accelerates. The evidence is enough
  to justify national attention, not enough to prove the complete filter theory.
- The local accounts, injuries, placements, and ash shares close cleanly. Kiva's fingers
  remain as before.
- The School runs its lane dark with the call again; Orla has fourteen juniors in the
  Old Yard. Orla asks Kiva whether the juniors should be told why it leans; Kiva has her
  speech ready and does not give it: *Run it dark. Make them say who has the bar.* Then
  she asks for help with her cuff. The book ends on a junior in the dark saying who has
  the bar, not on anyone counting.
- The Thursday gathering at the Home Line hall is not a sitting of the watch. The Ferren
  says to Kiva's face that the Line will call her as catcher at a line-hand's day rate,
  *hand as before*; the Reader lays the School's plaster cast beside Kiva's plate and says
  which is which. No motion, no vote, no roll, no edition, no printer, no clerk's fair
  copy (`editor/MOTIF-BUDGET-REVISION.md` §5, beat 14).

Pay off the title **Why It Is Crooked** through action and choice. The crooked line is
not superior because it is mysterious or chosen by fate. Its strength is that it
names the cost, leaves room for another hand on the bar, and changes direction without
pretending the first direction never existed. The final chapter should let Kiva act on
that understanding rather than explain it in an essay. End on a strong image or human
choice that completes this regional book while opening the road beyond Meridian.

## Seeds for Books Two and Three — plant, do not spend

Book Two widens nationally: intercollegiate competition beyond Meridian, company and
energy-industry pressure, stronger rift work, competing schools of the doors, and the
first time the dreamers are put in one room. Book Three widens internationally:
rival grids and nations, Glory-level users, stronger and stranger spawn, and the need
for cooperation as the filter destabilizes. Plant concrete invitations, notices,
readings, or witnesses that make those expansions inevitable. Do not leave named
characters merely announcing the sequel.

The longer 2680 series arc includes ash replacing earlier energy systems, ash-nuclear
synthesis, flight, compact reactors, industry titans, and devoted schools or orders.
Those systems already exist in the world; use them naturally in the emergency and its
aftermath. Later books will advance collegiate competition to regional, national, and
international levels, and rift fighting toward professional and Glory-level stakes.
Do not compress that ladder into this ending.

The recurring fever material is a shared, fallible set of accounts—not a trustworthy prophecy.
Across later books it drives Glory users toward one another and toward the eventual
complete loss of the planetary filter and enduring pure Glory presence. This movement
may add one historical echo, one changed instrument reading, and one reason
to compare accounts. It may not reveal the final event, identify a savior, or settle
whether the figures cause or answer the loss.

## What must remain true / withheld

- One source, three traditions. Soul supplies owned intention and pattern; finite ash
  supplies ownerless fuel. Ash cannot purchase judgment, experience, or consent.
- Rank, Hold, Draw, Call, control, yield, grade, resonance, licensing, and standings
  remain separate. Do not reward one number with every other form of status.
- Kiva's first two left fingers do not regain fine sensation. Her adaptations and
  handoffs remain necessary when the pressure rises.
- A three-door sequence includes two expensive empty gaps. A stack remains a miss and
  vulnerability, not a cheaper secret technique. Kiva cannot repeat the sequence at
  will, teach it as mastery, or use it outside shown authority without consequence.
- The disk stays cold and non-instructional. It may be recognized, carried, or
  contested; it does not speak, teach, map, choose, open a door, or save a scene.
- Annick and any new historical fragment supply imagery and human testimony only. Do
  not prove the filter theory, identify the Makers, explain the disk, reveal the cause
  of the Fall, or name the converging Glory users.
- Students do not take over professional closure, radiation work, or licenses they do
  not have. Adults do not solve the student choices or erase their earned field work.
- The Municipal Works and the Halden Works remain distinct. Institutions and rivals may
  protect lawful interests without becoming villains.
- Team and civic ash accounts stay exact. No free practice fuel, duplicate yield, erased
  contamination, or unopened medical reserve used by implication.
- Keep the accepted Movement Five clock and map: a knot rising about every minute and a
  half; the Pit corner eighteen paces from Kiva; the bound carried four paces east; all of
  Dray but the pinned left leg south of the line; the notch about a dozen seconds after
  the second cliff. Nobody counts these aloud; time in the fight is breaths and paces.
- Preserve differentiated dialogue, listener-oriented proper nouns, narration-ready
  punctuation, one-pass physical clarity, warmth, humor, and full action scenes.
- Do not add a sixth major secondary lens merely to distribute screen time. Purposeful
  established cutaways are allowed when Kiva cannot witness an essential action.
- Do not end on a lecture, a prophecy explanation, or an unresolved battle.
