## What this movement is for

Let recovery time create story rather than a skipped wait. Public results, the seventh
reed, the disk's one warmth, the T-0 markings, and the rich dirty spring cause different
people to go looking for different answers, and the answers are people. The Order's
Warden comes to Coper Street because the Order wants Kiva for a year, and says why, in
Annick's images; Noa follows instrument and sensing-mast evidence within his consent
boundary; Orla and Vane take Kiva before the Home Line's watch of seven, where the
Ferren, who sat the watch in 2669, says what Corran said that day. The School's founder
history, the Halden house's patents, the Order's belief, and the Home Line's custom
should disagree in useful, human ways: proprietary claims, renamed methods, a cost
nobody thought was a foreman's business. Do not turn this into eight chapters of adults
delivering archives, and do not make any scene about what was kept or handed on. What
people learn must change choices, relationships, access, or risk on the page. No document
moves the plot (`BOOK_MAP.md` §8; `editor/MOTIF-BUDGET-REVISION.md` §5).

Pay the dark-floor aftermath between Kiva and Anwen through actions and imperfect
conversation. Anwen's summer line is her own earned future. Kellan, Perrin, Noa, Jude,
Pru, Lowen, Selka, Gower, and Rhea may make independent placement or recruitment
decisions, but do not expand the viewpoint roster simply to report them. Preserve the
single-lead-dominant book shape and use only purposeful brief cutaways.

After the thirteenth, let the six sanctioned sequence attempts begin or meaningfully
advance. Kiva does not leap from one success to six. A familiar step should compress;
the new complication should expand. The teaching should clarify sequence, ash cost,
body position, abort conditions, and why a correct transition is not a simultaneous
stack. At least one attempt should fail for a reason different from the first fourteen.
At least one supporting person should have to correct Kiva rather than admire her.

The movement's physical spine is a supervised professional shadow shift that becomes
a real multi-type Riftspawn call. Use a district whose people and infrastructure matter,
not a spectacle floor. Introduce several comprehensible spawn expressions whose
interaction creates the danger; do not merely enlarge the Pit string. Collegiate habits
should initially fail because students optimize for score, fixed lanes, or a single
opponent. They recover only when roles, calls, handoffs, aborts, rescue, closure,
kill, and clean recovery are declared again. Professionals retain the work beyond the
students' license. Give the response developed tactical space, clear terrain, starting
positions, changing decisions, injury/fatigue, and a recovery/reconciliation aftermath.
Clean ash matters because the threatened district needs it and contaminated capture
would compound the emergency.

The professional shift must teach the difference between being impressive and being
useful without humiliating the students into irrelevance. Kiva may contribute within
her actual paper, observation, carrying, recovery, or one bounded trained action, but
the disk and fever fragment supply no tactic. The team should leave with a concrete
correction they can attempt again and with a sharper understanding of professional
rift-company standings: lives, closure, infrastructure, usable ash, and violations.

By the movement's close, Kiva learns before the watch that the disk was released to her
by a bearer, and why; she does not learn its cosmic origin or receive a chosen-one
guarantee. The Order's belief, said to her as a reason to join, contains an unmistakable
fragment of the Unroofed Sky from before she was born. It confirms shared imagery across
time but not cause,
interpretation, timing, or solution. Let the discovery cost trust, access, certainty,
or safety. It should open Movement Five's fight over ownership, proof, sponsorship,
and the Regional Measure—not replace that conflict with an answer.
