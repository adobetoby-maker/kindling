Kiva has two of Orla's required six sanctioned crossings. The four completed goes were:
a called *off* at 0.03, a counted crossing at 0.30, a soft 0.11 stack that Noa correctly
called a miss, and a second counted crossing at 0.31. The team has 1.25 drams left—four
crossings and one hundredth, no miss in the glass. Aske allows no go before Monday the
2nd and only one a week afterward, with the hand examined the next morning. Her knee is
passed for landings but remains supported. Her first two left fingers report slowly and
late. After the Weighbridge cord found an already depleted nerve, the whole hand remained
gone until supper, about fifteen hours. Permanent fine-sensation loss in those first two
fingers is reserved for this movement and cannot be cured by rank, ash, or victory.

The disk stayed cold. By the Home Line's custom it is held of the Line, released to a
bearer, and recallable by a watch of seven for cause said aloud before them. Kiva has
told Hollis Vane of its one warmth and he will say so to the watch on Sunday the 1st
(stage the recall vote as people deciding; this is the watch's second and last sitting in
Book One; nobody reads a book aloud and nobody cites an earlier bearer). The Pit tape also
opens that day. Selka Dray's objection is heard Monday the 2nd. On Tuesday the 3rd the
Placement Chair wants all parties, the catcher, the tape, Noa, Orla, and the sponsor.

The Weighbridge produced 113 drams of stepped ash, red-tagged *not for the ground*, in
the Wren House cold room. Halden claims it through the “however produced” patent; the
Pellow wardens claim the five tags made in their street; the Compact Library claims a
reference share. The district lost a core, received emergency ash, and has a factual
stake. Noa's evidence supports several explanations and proves none. Nobody may turn
stepped ash into a solved filter theory.

Team Seven is sixth of thirty-one by yield, attached to Rhea Sorn, and remains under
its existing paper. Kellan has accepted Halden's license team for the Regional while
remaining on Seven's book. Anwen is on Mace's Graywater line until late next month and
has not repaired things with Kiva. Perrin remains headed for his Hall's second team in
the autumn. Pru holds Rhea's yellow reserve card (*a corner and a lamp, not a wall*).
Jude Talley has been suggested as Seven's blade but not appointed on the page. Nairn is
out until autumn; the night van is short a blade and a ward. Lowen is on a Home Line hold
crew. Selka is a legitimate competitor with an unresolved objection, not a villain.

The Regional Measure falls in the autumn. Use the elapsed weeks honestly: the remaining
crossings cannot be compressed inside Aske's one-per-week limit, and other people's
placements continue while Kiva waits.

## What this movement is for

Bring ownership, proof, sponsorship, and collegiate competition into the same physical
story. Resolve or materially advance the watch, objection, Chair, patent, district, and
Order pressures early enough that they change who may enter, what equipment and ash may
be used, whose evidence counts, and who stands beside whom at the Regional. Do not spend
eight chapters in hearings. Every institutional result must alter a relationship, a
training choice, an eligibility condition, an ash account, or a field decision.

The emotional engine is friendship under divided obligations. Kellan must stand for a
Halden team against or apart from Seven without becoming disloyal by fiat. Anwen's line
service remains her own earned future. Perrin, Pru, Noa, Jude, Lowen, Selka, Orla, Mara,
Rhea, and the professionals keep independent reasons. Let risk, credit, sponsorship,
kill responsibility, and ash shares fracture and reform alliances through choices on
the page. Kiva must stop treating support as automatic. Do not add another secondary
viewpoint; use Kiva-dominant narration and only established purposeful lenses when she
cannot witness necessary action.

Complete the four remaining sanctioned crossings at no more than one per week. A
familiar successful step may compress, but any changed problem must expand. Reconcile
every 0.31 spend and the remaining hundredth. Show the hand after each go. The sixth
counted crossing proves repeatability under Orla's terms; it does not certify mastery,
explain the expensive gap, or make the stack correct. Before any three-door work, state
the difference among a deliberate sequence, a simultaneous stack, and an uncontrolled
competition of doors.

Train one controlled three-door transition with explicit order, body position, ash
price, stopping authority, and abort conditions. The first public configuration may
occur in the Regional when earned by the training, but it must be brief and bounded.
It proves that the configuration is real, not that Kiva is secretly high-rank, ready
for Glory, safe alone, or able to repeat it casually. The disk and Unroofed Sky supply
no tactic. The permanent finger cost must follow a physically and causally legible
choice, not arrive as bookkeeping after the win.

The physical spine is a long Regional Measure rather than one duel. Let its bracket or
course alternate developed human combat, team Riftspawn response, rescue/extraction,
and ashcraft or grid work. Keep positions, roles, scoring, ash limits, equipment,
adaptation, fatigue, and audience/institutional consequences trackable. A human contest
should teach something that later helps or misleads. Rival teams must remain competent;
Lowen and Selka adapt, and Kellan's Halden commitments produce real tactical pressure.

During the Measure, filter instability introduces a stronger Riftspawn variety or
interaction that no event designer ordered. It should follow understandable existing
rules while exceeding expected strength, timing, or combination; do not solve it by
simply making the last Flask larger. The event's safety assumptions fail. Rival teams
must cooperate to protect people, stabilize the field, rescue the hurt, close or hold
what students are licensed to touch, and recover usable ash without contaminating the
grid. Professionals retain work beyond student paper. Kiva's contribution is essential
but not solitary: other fighters must own kills, rescues, containment, shutdowns, and
recovery that she cannot replace.

The ending must make the three-door configuration publicly real and institutionally
unignorable while leaving it unmastered. Kiva permanently loses fine sensation in the
first two left fingers. She must change grip, accept help, and face a cost no ranking,
patent decision, sponsorship, or recovered ash reverses. The Regional produces a real
result and fair accounts, but it opens Movement Six's civic emergency rather than
serving as the book's final climax.

## What must remain true / withheld

- One source, three traditions. Soul supplies owned intention and pattern; finite ash
  supplies ownerless fuel. Ash cannot purchase judgment or automatic experience.
- Draw is spent ash divided by the governing expectation. Rank, Hold, Draw, Call,
  control, yield, grade, resonance, licensing, and standings remain separate.
- Kiva begins with two sanctioned crossings counted, two not counted, 1.25 drams in
  the glass, and a one-go-per-week medical limit. Do not create free practice ash or
  skip the next-morning hand checks.
- A crossing includes the expensive empty gap. The 0.11 stack is a miss, not a superior
  secret technique. Three-door work must be sequential and bounded, never an effortless
  simultaneous blaze.
- The disk stays non-instructional. It may be recognized or contested under the Line's
  rules, but it does not speak, teach, map, choose tactics, open doors, or answer.
- What the Warden says Annick saw confirms shared imagery but no cause, date, timing,
  identity, or solution. Do not prove the filter theory, name the Makers, explain the disk's origin,
  identify why the Fall happened, or state whether Glory users cause or answer loss.
- Stepped ash is evidence with competing lawful claims. It does not become a magic
  battery that removes scarcity or a complete answer to what lies beyond tears.
- Kiva keeps her actual certificate and field paper until a shown authority changes it.
  She cannot silently act as Hold, Carry, or professional closure officer.
- Anwen remains on Graywater service until her time is honestly elapsed. Kellan keeps
  his Halden pledge. Perrin's paper, Pru's reserve limits, and Nairn's broken leg matter.
- Team and civic ash accounts remain exact. Sealed medical reserve is not training fuel.
  Every Regional allocation, spend, loss, share, and return is reconciled.
- Lowen and Selka are adaptive legitimate rivals. No institution is made evil merely
  because it has a lawful claim or safety objection.
- Use the supported eleven-or-twelve-second Pit interval. If Kiva's old forty-second
  line is confronted, amend it on the page rather than silently changing chapters 1–24.
- Preserve differentiated dialogue: younger characters plain and quick; Pike, Nairn,
  and Rhea operationally brief; formal/period idiom concentrated in elders, wardens,
  and institutional voices. Preserve narration-ready punctuation and listener-oriented
  referents.
- Preserve accessible one-pass prose, short-paragraph rhythm, varied sentence weight,
  warmth, humor, and full tactical scenes. Do not turn the numerical formula into filler
  or shorten successful action merely to meet a mean.
