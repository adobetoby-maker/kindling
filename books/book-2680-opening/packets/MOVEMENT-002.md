## What this movement is for

Make the series' central pleasure unmistakable: serious students at discipline and
military-response schools get stronger by training, fighting one another, learning to
kill Riftspawn, and earning the finite ash that funds the next threshold.

Give several connected chapters—not a montage—to spawn-kill preparation and the
team's first licensed progression step. Let the reader learn with the students:
spawn anatomy and behavior; terrain and approach; declared kill, containment, rescue,
and recovery roles; abort calls; how dissolution becomes dangerous loose ash; how to
catch, quarantine, grade, and reconcile a yield; and why a kill can still be a failed
response. Let fear, humor, rivalry, equipment mistakes, and bodily recovery live in
the scenes. Kiva cannot enter live work until Orla has evidence sufficient to sign.

The movement should include a developed supervised Handful-yield kill and clean
recovery. The team receives its first small recorded ash share after civic and casualty
shares. Ash is measured by yield, grade, and resonance. The team must choose whether
to spend, save, or return part of its share for training cells, permitted combat ash,
equipment, travel, or medical reserve. Killing does not automatically raise rank;
the usable gain is the correction they can reproduce and the ash that funds the next
attempt.

Then deliver the first sanctioned collegiate meet. Human opponents remember, feint,
adapt, and exploit habits the spawn drills created. Give the school-versus-school
combat developed tactical length. A useful team loss should prove that access is not
control while still producing a visible, earned improvement. Noa Bexley begins
recording Kiva's output, Draw, recovery efficiency, and door behavior because the
numbers do not fit the one-door model.

Seed the coupled escalation without explaining it: this student cohort is stronger
than recent records predict, and the contained/live spawn season shows a small but
specific variation instructors do not expect. Characters may call these separate
oddities. They do not yet prove that rising Glory pressure beyond the filter causes
both.

## What must remain true / withheld

- One source, three traditions. Soul supplies owned intention and pattern; ash is
  finite ownerless fuel. Ash can power a permitted technique or device, but cannot
  buy judgment, transfer a spawn's ability, or act as automatic experience.
- Rank, Hold, Draw, control, Call, yield, grade, and resonance are distinct. Preserve
  their different meanings on the page.
- Kiva may improve single-door control and the transition drill. She does **not**
  complete the first deliberate two-door sequence in this movement. The disk stays
  cold. That payoff belongs to Movement Three.
- The permanent loss of fine sensation in two fingers remains reserved for Movement
  Five. Current dullness may complicate grip and recovery but cannot quietly become
  permanent or disappear.
- Orla's sponsorship, Mara's license review, Anwen's independent field goal, Perrin's
  bursary and envy, and Kellan's legitimate excellence all carry real consequences.
- Do not identify the cause of the Fall, prove the filter theory, explain the disk,
  name the Makers, or turn the Unroofed Sky into tactical guidance.
- Professional and collegiate standings value rescue, closure, infrastructure,
  clean ash, and violations as well as kills. Do not make kill count meaningless;
  do not make it the only score.
- Preserve clear spatial action, developed fights, narration-ready punctuation, and
  the O'Connor numerical craft targets. Do not fall into uniform one-line sentences.

## What the author owns

Own the eight-chapter route, scene boundaries, training failures, spawn expression,
school opponent, tactics, conversations, humor, ash-share decision, and the exact
shape of the useful loss. Invent new minor names only when the scene needs them and
collision-check them against the registry. Keep Kiva near the established 87% focus;
use brief secondary cutaways only where they create dramatic irony or independent
stakes.

The training and fights should feel like lived collegiate progression, not a manual.
Let readers understand the rules early enough to anticipate a tactic, then allow both
the spawn and the human opponents to make the students revise it.

## Where we leave pressure

By Chapter Sixteen, Kiva retains her roster place after a useful sanctioned loss;
Team Seven has killed and cleanly recovered its first live Handful, owns a small ash
share, and has made a consequential choice about it. Anwen's field-placement path has
moved independently. Noa holds measurements that the institution cannot dismiss.
The reader has seen why school combat and Riftspawn combat teach different things,
and has one concrete sign that both the student ceiling and the spawn season are
rising. Movement Three opens with the circuit accelerating toward a live Flask-level
response and Kiva's first true two-door sequence.
