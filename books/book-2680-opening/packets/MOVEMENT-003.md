## Where we enter

Chapter Sixteen ends on the evening of Monday the sixth. Kiva Rowan has retained the
lowest defensible place: provisional Third Division, Crooked Line third team, with
all of Ysra Crest's conditions still in force. Her cross-school field-license Team
Seven stands seventeenth of thirty-one by its first clean yield. The Circuit board has
moved the Flask qualifiers forward to the last week of the current month because the
district has produced nine tears since the thaw and the unusually strong entering
class supplies more candidates than expected. Team Seven has about three weeks.

Kiva is certified for recovery only: dark hands, under a declared team call, with no
door opened within three paces of open ash or a charged cell. She is not certified for
Kill, Hold, Carry, or tuning. She can hold a single door through a four-minute carry,
can move through her three-door drill in 8.4 seconds without watching the metal home,
and can keep all three doors shut under fear by calling a number. She has felt a clean
handoff across two people but has never attempted or completed a deliberate two-door
sequence. The Satori disk is cold.

Her left knee still cannot take hard landings. Her first and second left fingers remain
dull and late, with the second about a week behind; the hand failed once without warning
near the end of the meet. The three heat-lines retain their new length to mid upper arm.
Permanent fine-sensation loss remains reserved for Movement Five.

Lowen Brask beat Kiva's habits in the Stonehand lane and promised to think of something
else next time. He is the primary adaptive human rival, capable and legitimate rather
than cruel. Selka Dray carries institutional resentment and pressure but does not replace
him in the rival role. Team Seven owns 1.4 drams of purchased Kennel-floor work and a
new catch kit; its 1.0 dram W-trace medical reserve stays sealed unless a true medical
need justifies it. Kellan chose Team Seven over transfer, Anwen has begun a Graywater
shadow rotation, Perrin's cracked ribs cost him the load plate, and Noa now takes
sanctioned measurements under Kiva's monthly consent.

Read Chapter Sixteen and the full Movement Two continuity checkpoint closely. Carry
every injury, date, certification, instrument reading, ash share, relationship, and
knowledge boundary forward.

## What this movement is for

Accelerate the first collegiate season without turning it into a montage. Let the team
invest its earned ash and purchased Kennel floors in specific corrections. Show the
ceiling through professional response footage or an in-person demonstration that makes
the students understand how far judgment, recovery, and coordinated control still have
to go. Give Anwen, Perrin, Kellan, Noa, Jude, Pru, and Lowen independent pressures and
choices rather than making them extensions of Kiva.

Develop Kiva's first deliberate two-door work across failed attempts. The problem is a
sequence, not a stack: one owned piece of work must finish into the next without both
doors being held at once, without spilling the pattern, and without using the disk as a
teacher. Let body position, breath, count, ash expenditure, recovery, and the feel of the
across-people handoff matter. Failure should cost time, opportunity, confidence, fuel,
or safety, not merely produce a red number. Orla must remain accountable and unwilling
to certify what she has not witnessed.

Deliver a substantial learning fight against Lowen Brask or the Stonehand team. Lowen
has already learned Kiva's pause, flag gap, concern for a fallen person, and reliance on
declared change. He must adapt again. Kiva and her teammates must read him, test an
approach, misread something, correct it, and carry a usable lesson out whether they win
or lose. Preserve clear terrain, starting positions, scoring or objective, changing
decisions, bodily cost, and aftermath. The fight is not a disguised Flask drill.

Then move into the accelerated Flask qualifier or an immediately connected live Flask
response. A Flask is twenty Handfuls; it cannot be written as a bigger dog-shaped husk.
Introduce a new, comprehensible spawn expression that threatens an arena induction grid,
containment system, or adjacent civic service and cannot be solved with duel habits.
The variation should grow from the established dirty spring and W-trace skin without
proving the filter theory. A team response must still include rescue or infrastructure,
closure/containment, developed killing decisions, and clean ash recovery. Professionals
may own the work beyond student certification. Students must not become implausibly
strong merely because the scene needs a climax.

During the live response, let Kiva's first correct deliberate two-door sequence work
briefly for one bounded task she has trained toward. It should arise from demonstrated
single-door discipline and the handoff principle, not panic unlocking a new level. The
sequence has a measurable bodily and ash cost, cannot be held indefinitely, and does not
grant a new rank. Only after the correct sequence finishes may the disk warm. The disk
does not speak, instruct, map the rift, or solve the threat.

The team should recover a meaningful yield whose grade and resonance matter. Preserve
the full loop: train, contest, respond, kill, recover, grade/reconcile, divide, and make
an investment choice. Let success alter placement, licensing, recruitment, or access
without erasing the team's mistakes.

A fragment of the Unroofed Sky may recur after the costly sequence or exposure. It can
add sensory evidence and bodily cost, but it supplies no tactic and no explanation. The
reader should finish the movement wanting the inquiry promised by Movement Four: the
Order's telling, the Home Line's watch, and the professionals who have seen this before.

## What must remain true / withheld

- One source, three traditions. Soul supplies owned intention and pattern; finite ash
  supplies ownerless fuel. Ash cannot purchase judgment, grant a spawn ability, or act
  as automatic experience.
- Draw is ash spent divided by the task's book/bench expectation; `1.0` is standard and
  lower is more efficient. Rank, Hold, Draw, control, Call, yield, grade, and resonance
  remain distinct.
- Kiva's first deliberate two-door sequence may succeed once, briefly and at a clear
  price. She does not master it, repeat it casually, open a stable three-door
  configuration, reach Glory, or acquire a secret top rank.
- The disk warms only after that correct deliberate sequence. It never teaches, speaks,
  answers a question, grants power, or triggers on command.
- The Unroofed Sky is an involuntary fever fragment. It does not guide tactics. Do not
  identify the cause of the Fall, prove the filter theory, explain the disk's true
  origin, or name the Makers.
- Current finger dullness remains recovering and intermittent. Permanent fine-sensation
  loss belongs to Movement Five and cannot be spent early or quietly cured.
- Mara's Works hearing before Examiner Rusk occurs on the ninth and must have a concrete,
  proportionate outcome. Her right-palm numbness and left-hand adaptation remain true.
- Anwen's Graywater placement, Perrin's Stonehand advancement and ribs, Kellan's chosen
  loyalty and legitimate First Division excellence, Noa's consent-bound measurements,
  and the Crooked Line third team's five remaining meets continue independently.
- Team Seven's ash accounts stay exact. The W-trace medical reserve is not ordinary
  training fuel. Every spend, yield, public share, and investment is reconciled.
- A clean kill is not the whole response. Professional and collegiate standings value
  rescue, closure, infrastructure, usable ash, and violations as well as kills.
- Lowen Brask is a serious adaptive rival, not a bully or secret villain. Selka Dray is
  unresolved institutional pressure, not a substitute for Lowen.
- Keep Kiva near the established dominant viewpoint share, with purposeful brief
  cutaways rather than alternating equal protagonists.
- Preserve accessible one-pass prose, narration-ready punctuation, short-paragraph
  rhythm, varied sentence weight, and full tactical scenes. Do not inflate dialogue to
  chase formula means or compress substantial action to hit a word budget.

## What the author owns

Own the eight-chapter route, exact qualifier design, training failures, professional
demonstration, arena and grid geography, Lowen's adaptation, new spawn anatomy and
behavior, tactics, conversations, humor, viewpoint cutaways, ash arithmetic, and the
precise bounded task accomplished by Kiva's sequence. The author may let plans change
when the prose earns a stronger route, but must record any surviving change in the
continuity checkpoint rather than silently overwriting canon.

Use movement-sized causality. Do not write eight disconnected episodes. The team's ash
investment should change its preparation; the human fight should teach something that
helps or misleads during the Flask; the Flask aftermath should create the next
movement's pressure to go and ask the people who might know.

## Where we leave pressure

By Chapter Twenty-Four, Kiva has completed one brief, deliberate, properly priced
two-door sequence and the Satori disk has warmed only afterward. She cannot yet treat
either event as mastery or explanation. Team Seven has survived a live Flask-level
response, contributed within real licensing limits, and recovered a meaningful clean
yield after protecting people or infrastructure. Lowen remains a credible rival with
his own advancement. Recruitment and licensing paths are beginning to separate the
friends. The spawn expression and the disk response make Kiva's anomaly searchable,
while a short Unroofed Sky fragment gives no answer. Movement Four opens on the
School's founder history, the Halden house's patents, the Order's doctrine, and the Home
Line's custom disagreeing.
