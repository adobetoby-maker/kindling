## The opening promise

Write the substantial historical prologue first, using the eleven-snapshot architecture
in `PROLOGUE_PLAN.md`. A cold reader must understand the visible EMP: stratospheric
detonations cause a broad electromagnetic pulse, powered systems fail without a
ground-level blast, communications and infrastructure collapse, and the survivors
do not know who caused it or whether the detonations relate to later filter damage.
Carry the copied-reasons habit through 2080, the failed-restoration decade, the first
wormhole/Riftspawn/unnamed power event, and the Homura/Home Line era seen only through
records. Then dramatize the first century after Meridian in enough depth for the
reader to understand the transformation: radiation-safe Sustain rotations recover
nuclear sites and knowledge; ash and nuclear systems are blended; stable electrical
induction develops; licensed compact reactors reach homes; fossil and large solar
systems become secondary; flight, advanced equipment, professional rift companies,
industrial Glory houses, and devoted school orders follow. Do not compress this into
a montage or claim the benefits had no cost.

Give Snapshot 6 to **Tamar Vey**, an original mortal itinerant teacher/storyteller.
An engineer is trying to make ash behave like a longer-burning fuel. Through one
plain story and a small physical demonstration, Tamar makes the engineer see that
ash can hold instruction after a person releases it: the reactor already has heat;
ash might hold the rules around that heat. Tamar does not design the plant or possess
future knowledge. The breakthrough belongs to the engineers' later testing, failure,
records, and labor. Capture the catalytic wise-teacher pleasure without borrowing
any existing fictional character's identity, voice, history, cosmology, or phrasing.

The final snapshot shows Kiva's three-door Kindling at thirteen and the first full
version of **the Unroofed Sky** fever dream: Glory-level figures converge, the
planetary filter disappears, pure Glory presence presses into the world, and every
ash-lit system responds. An older Glory account in the preceding history shares
some images, but neither Kiva nor the reader receives a reliable interpretation.
Keep the cause, timing, winner, and meaning unresolved. End on the crooked post.
Chapter One opens **Four years later — 2680**.

The prologue is scene-led and human. New historical figures may receive original
one-scene names, but none may become a surprise immortal or a hidden ancestor who
explains Kiva. Do not dramatize the present-trilogy cast directly.

## Movement One entry

Kiva lives with her aunt and guardian, Mara Rowan, who repairs Meridian's municipal
instruments and reconciles their paper records. The disk reached Kiva through a
sealed Home Line appointment by its prior bearer, not blood descent. Kiva knows it
is old and entrusted. She does not know its origin or complete history.

The Meridian Compact includes the Crooked Line School, Stonehand Hall, Wren House,
and the field-facing Home Line. The Meridian Collegiate Circuit ends in the Regional
Measure and feeds licensed, salaried professional rift companies.
Regional wormhole stations are scheduled, expensive, and capacity-limited. There is
no retinal status overlay: classification is done through physical rigs, projected
tables, and accountable human rulings.

Ash induction drives most electrical life: lights, pumps, heat, communications,
medical equipment, machine tools, transit, arenas, and rift sensing. Magic supplies
pattern and control; refined ownerless ash supplies finite fuel. Modern cells run
after a Kindled operator tunes them, but require grading, metering, replacement,
maintenance, and shutdowns. At Glory, a rare master can hold industrial-scale
patterns, creating both industrial dynasties and near-monastic school orders. Book
One reveals this regional ecosystem; national and international consequences belong
mainly to Books Two and Three.

Kiva remembers the Unroofed Sky only in sensory fragments. She does not know that
the name exists or that older Glory records overlap it. The dream may recur briefly
under fever, ash overload, filter instability, or close Glory pressure, but it never
answers a question or provides a technique.

## What the movement does

Across one connected eight-chapter run:

- Begin four years after Kiva's Kindling. She has partial training in each door but
  no stable combined category, no secret top rank, and no automatic collegiate place.
  Her three heat-lines remain visible and tender when the doors compete.
- Teach the physical basics of each door, rank versus control, soul versus finite ash,
  ash induction, magic/ash blending, collegiate events and standings, professional
  recruitment, and why the Compact cannot safely classify simultaneous access.
  Demonstrations, failed attempts, Mara's repair work, team practice, and consequences
  carry the explanation.
- Establish Orla Dane as Kiva's limited-authority mentor; Anwen Pryce as a practical
  equal with her own field-placement goal; Perrin Cade as warmth and honest envy;
  Kellan Renn as a legitimately excellent single-path rival; and Mara as a guardian
  whose safety concern has material grounds.
- Noa Bexley, Ysra Crest, Rhea Sorn, and Hollis Vane may enter only as the run earns
  them. Do not crowd the opening merely to complete a roster.
- A collegiate placement trial combines controlled combat, ashcraft, and rescue. It
  fails in an original, spatially clear way. Kiva protects someone but worsens her
  mark. The answer comes from limits, help, and judgment—not finished three-door
  mastery.
- The disk warms only **after** Kiva completes the first correct, deliberate door
  sequence. It never teaches, speaks, unlocks technique, or causes the success.
- Give the Unroofed Sky one brief recurrence during the movement. Treat it as a
  physical interruption with consequence, not foreshadowing dialogue or a tactical
  solution. The full international collision belongs to Books Two and Three.

Kiva owns approximately 87% of focus. Brief secondary cutaways must have purpose and
must not create a second protagonist. Relationships carry humor, abrasion, envy,
affection, and independent wants.

## Hard boundaries

- One underlying source, three traditions. The soul supplies owned intention and
  pattern; Riftspawn leave finite ownerless ash that supplies fuel. Rank, control,
  ash efficiency, and team judgment are separate axes.
- Collegiate competition has scholarships, teams, spectators, standings, recruiters,
  and serious consequences. It is not a children's tournament. Professional rift
  fighters are licensed emergency-combat specialists; kills alone do not define rank.
- Electrical ash systems always have fuel cost, meters, maintenance, failure modes,
  and shutdown paths. Kiva's three doors do not make her an instant master engineer.
- No destiny shortcut, secret top rank, inherited finished skill, unlimited reserve,
  surprise door, or unpriced climax solution.
- Do not explain the disk's true origin, Makers, source realm, or first cause of the
  Fall/filter damage.
- Do not confirm whether the Unroofed Sky is prophecy, recorded memory, future
  resonance, or manipulation. The series ending is fixed—complete filter loss and
  pure Glory presence—but Book One's characters do not know that as fact.
- Do not copy names, terminology, dialogue, system presentation, or scene sequence
  from the research corpus.
- Use the O'Connor numerical formula as an influence and audit target, not as prose
  that sounds counted. Keep punctuation clean for narration, paragraphs short, fights
  and tests developed, and reading level accessible without flattening emotion.
- The later permanent loss of fine sensation in Kiva's first two left fingers is
  planted but does not occur in this movement unless the book's earned scene demands
  an earlier minor precursor. It cannot be healed away later.

## Exit state

By Chapter Eight, Kiva has a provisional collegiate roster placement in the lowest
defensible division,
her household stakes are live, the core friendship/rivalry lines have independent
goals, her mark has a real cost, and a professional scout has noticed the disk only
after Kiva earns attention. The reader understands the collegiate-to-professional
ladder and ash-electric stakes without receiving a complete cosmological answer.
One historical fragment should make the dream harder to dismiss while leaving its
meaning open. Finish with a precise continuity checkpoint.
