## 3. World facts established on the page

- **Circuit axes:** Rank, Hold (out of ten), Draw (ash per standard work vs 1.0; lower is better), Call (team judgment; dark on Classification Day). Projection boards; draw bench (lamp-cell, sticky needle at "two"); hold posts; lit frame. No overlay.
- **Divisions:** First, Second, Third. Third = tuition only. Provisional placements carry a sponsor line.
- **Doors in 2680 speech:** Ember door / hand door / still door. Sustain is glossed on the page as the healing side of the hand door (the read of where a body is hurt). Kindling stations: Ember slab in a brazier, bowl of rift-pool water (Wren House), stance stone ("five hundred years of feet"); then the lit frame.
- **Ash terms (updated canon):** **yield** is quantity (Handful, Flask, Barrel, Well); **grade** is purity/stability (First, Second, Third); resonance is not yet on the page. The manuscript uses "a Handful" and "a Barrel yield / Barrel-yield" for spawn size and keeps "Grade First/Second/Third" for cells and stamps.
- **Open lane:** Stonehand's Terrace Ring — sunken bowl, three knee-high steps a pace and a half deep, ~12-pace raked sand floor, rim sensing mast, standard dram of clean ash settled before bouts; dark Edge only; 1 point body/head, ½ limb; three wins. Board marks a requested bout with the word *requested*.
- **Cellhouse:** ex-refinery, ~40 × 14 paces; Gate and green line west; gallery behind glass along the north wall (about four hundred public seats, assessors' booth with master stop, recruiters' section); mezzanine substation east, **2.5 m** off the floor (12-step stair on the **south** wall rising east, wall on the left and rail on the right for someone facing down it; drum regulator, red quench with wire seal, quench box of wet salt, load board on the east wall, winch on the same circuit); pump room under the mezzanine with a jammed shutter (~400 kg) and one wide west-facing doorway — from the mezzanine rail Kiva can see the threshold and Perrin, not the inside; chute mid-south wall from **the Kennel**, a bottle-rift in the basement vault in a Wren House–maintained lattice impressed by a Glory master ~60 years ago. Trial husks are dog-sized, a **Handful** each in the yield books; **four releases to a run**. Objectives 1–4 and violations as briefed by Ysra. Breaker trip at 8.5 = uncontrolled; breaker cuts load but **does not stop a pattern; only the quench does.** After the run, clerks gather the husk ash into sealed tins for the Wren House graders.
- **Cells:** a cell keeps what you leave in it, including mood (Brannock's "hurry"). Kiva's three-door contamination persisted after release and **called husks** (rift attraction) — never before seen in the Compact; nobody predicted it.
- **Paper law:** every serious ash device has a chained paper cell book, "since the first licensed cells, more than four hundred years ago"; the *Transient Register* records unexplained flickers (more than four hundred years of volumes). **T-0** = transient, no cause. Three multi-grid T-0s: **2446** (night, every lamp, every district; fragile volume, copy pending), **2604** (03:10, Compact Quarter; margin: "Again. Same as the '46 entry in the old book. Every lamp at once."), **2676** (14:52, Eastbank + Lantern + Weighbridge grids; Kiva's Kindling). The 13th's trip was **local only** (one shared core).
- **Sag Line masts:** baselines creeping upward on every mast for years; recalibration masks it; Mara declined to recalibrate mast six. Measurement only — no theory confirmed. "The filter" is glossed once as the old idea that the sky has a lid that is wearing thin.
- **Institutions/colours:** Graywater (slate blue, white wave; hall on the Brickfields road), Saltmere (green), Halden companies (black and gold); Order of the Even Hand (Ward devotion, one door for life; Annick reached Glory). City banner: *SIX HUNDRED YEARS. WE WROTE IT DOWN.*
- **The crooked post:** last post in the Old Yard lane, black, split, two iron bands, leans left. Standard manual: "retained for ceremonial continuity" (since the 2603 committee vote, 11–6). Orla: "There used to be one. It's not in the manual anymore." Marisol's pencil note survives in the superseded stacks.
