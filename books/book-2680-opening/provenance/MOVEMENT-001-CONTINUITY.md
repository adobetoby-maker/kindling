- **Doors in 2680 speech:** Ember door / hand door / still door. Kindling stations: Ember slab in a brazier, bowl of rift-pool water (Wren House), stance stone; then the lit frame.
- **Open lane:** Stonehand's Terrace Ring — sunken bowl, three knee-high steps a pace and a half deep, ~12-pace raked sand floor, rim sensing mast, standard dram of First settled before bouts; dark Edge only; 1 point body/head, ½ limb; three wins.
- **Cellhouse:** ex-refinery, ~40 × 14 paces; Gate and green line west; mezzanine substation east (12-step south-wall stair, drum regulator, red quench with wire seal, quench box of wet salt, load board on the east wall, winch on the same circuit); pump room under the mezzanine with a jammed shutter (~400 kg); chute mid-south wall from **the Kennel**, a bottle-rift in the basement vault in a Wren House–maintained lattice impressed by a Glory master ~60 years ago. Trial husks are dog-sized, Handful-grade. Objectives 1–4 and violations as briefed by Ysra. Breaker trip at 8.5 = uncontrolled; breaker cuts load but **does not stop a pattern; only the quench does.**
- **Cells:** a cell keeps what you leave in it, including mood (Brannock's "hurry"). Kiva's three-door contamination persisted after release and **called husks** (rift attraction) — never before seen in the Compact.
- **Paper law:** every serious ash device has a chained paper cell book; the *Transient Register* records unexplained flickers. **T-0** = transient, no cause. Three multi-grid T-0s: **2446** (night, every lamp, every district; fragile volume, copy pending), **2604** (03:10, Compact Quarter; margin: "Again. Same as the '46 entry in the old book. Every lamp at once."), **2676** (14:52, Eastbank + Lantern + Weighbridge grids; Kiva's Kindling). Last night's trip was **local only** (one shared core).
- **Sag Line masts:** baselines creeping upward on every mast for years; recalibration masks it; Mara declined to recalibrate mast six. Measurement only — no theory confirmed.
- **Institutions/colours:** Graywater (slate blue, white wave; hall on the Brickfields road), Saltmere (green), Halden companies (black and gold); Order of the Even Hand (Ward devotion). City banner: *SIX HUNDRED YEARS. WE WROTE IT DOWN.*
- **The crooked post:** last post in the Old Yard lane, black, split, two iron bands, leans left. Standard manual: "retained for ceremonial continuity" (since the 2603 committee vote, 11–6). Orla: "There used to be one. It's not in the manual anymore." Marisol's pencil note survives in the superseded stacks.
