# Kindling 2680 opening-book prep

Planning is locked and no prose has yet been drafted. The opening begins with a
historical prologue ending at Kiva's 2676 Kindling, then jumps to her 2680 collegiate
season. Collegiate competition, professional rift companies, ash induction,
magic/ash blending, industrial Glory houses, and devoted school orders are core
architecture rather than later additions.

The prologue is an eleven-scene historical narrative, not a brief montage. It traces
ash from survival fuel through Tamar Vey's catalytic teaching, radiation recovery,
ash-nuclear synthesis, compact household power, flight, and the far-future society.
It also plants the Unroofed Sky fever dream that drives the Books Two and Three
convergence and the eventual complete loss of the planetary filter.

Start review with `IDEA.md`, then `SERIES_MAP.md`, `UNIVERSE_BIBLE.md`, and
`BOOK_MAP.md`. `PROLOGUE_PLAN.md` holds the required scene-led bridge from the
2080 EMP through fallout and rebuilding to 2680. `CHARACTERS.md` is the owner naming sheet; `STATE_LEDGER.md` lists
the six locks required before a Movement One packet. The locally compiled Opus
planning packet is `planning/OPUS-5.5-PLAN.prompt.md`; compiling it did not run a
model.
