# Kindling 2680, Book Two — universe bible

> Built by TAC

Status: **planning.** This file does not replace
`books/book-2680-opening/UNIVERSE_BIBLE.md` or `universe/UNIVERSE_BIBLE.md`; read both. It
restates the rules Book Two leans on and adds only what Book Two needs. Three labels are used
and kept apart:

- `ESTABLISHED` — on the page of Book One or locked in its bible.
- `PLANNED` — intended for Book Two. Not canon until surviving prose says it.
- `RESERVED` — true or possible in the series, and not to be spent in Book Two.

## 1. Rules that do not move (`ESTABLISHED`)

- One source, three traditions: Ember, rift-granted magic (the hand door: Ward, Sustain), and
  internal cultivation (the still door). The soul fills. Ash is ownerless, finite fuel.
- The rank ladder: Kindled, Ember, Flame, Fire, Torch, Blaze, Glory. Rank and breadth are one
  axis; skill and control are another. Hold, Draw, Call, yield, grade, resonance, license,
  and standing are separate measures, and none pays for another.
- **Draw** is ash spent on a piece of work over the book expectation for it: 1.0 is standard,
  lower is better.
- A kill does not raise rank. Ash funds the next attempt. Learning is what makes it survivable.
- Ash is recorded by **yield** (Handful, Flask, Barrel, Well), **grade**, and **resonance.**
  The comb's third reed sings for ward-trace (W). The seventh (I) is cut to the Step.
- Glory stops ordinary aging, is rare, and cannot be granted, bought, or stolen. It widens
  what one person can hold: throughput, duration, precision. It never makes ash free.
- Riftspawn expressions echo worked powers with nobody home to direct them. Spawn anatomy
  as trained: breath; posts or load paths; knots; a cord; a hem; the tear; closure.
- Students close no tear. Licensed crews do. Radiation work and licenses not held are not
  taken over in a crisis.
- Classification is physical: rigs, masts, pens, tapes, projected tables, human rulings. No
  private overlay.
- **Doors, for Kiva.** A competition is all doors open and fighting. A stack is two open
  together and agreeing: soft, cheap, and open to anything near; forbidden to her. A sequence
  shuts each door before the next opens, and each gap costs about a quarter of a dram with
  nothing open. A crossing is one gap. Three doors are two gaps.
- **The disk** does not teach, speak, map, choose, open a door, or answer. Two bearers have
  written *warm, once,* after a first crossing, and *cold* ever after.
- **The dream** is a fever, not a prophecy that can be consulted. It cannot be summoned. Each
  recurrence has a bodily cost. Triggers on record: fever, ash overload, filter instability,
  close contact with Glory.
- **The Step** (Halden's foundation patent, 2391): a row of cells salted late with one pinch
  of ash already made to keep a beat thereafter beats as one and can be driven by one
  operator. Used in Halden's yards, refineries, lift yards, and big tram sheds. Pump houses
  and tenement boxes were never meant to be stepped. The claim covers "all ash exhibiting the
  Step, however produced." The caution: "Not to be introduced to an unstepped bank under
  load." Before the patent the Wren House called it *counting ash.*
- Shown, not explained, in Book One: stepped pumps make water rise and fall on a count of four
  and four; a cell joined to a stepped bank by nothing but that water goes into step; a tear
  wants "the thing that keeps time" and not the bright thing; a string with an arm in the
  water is itself a pump; cut cords spill open ash that can cook a bank.

## 2. The country (`PLANNED`, built on `ESTABLISHED` pieces)

- **The Charter Board** (`ESTABLISHED` as a name) licenses rift companies nationally, keeps the
  national roll, and runs the National Measure. It can ask a nation to look at its banks. A
  Circuit cannot.
- **Six circuits** send their first company of four fours each: **twenty-four fours.** Meridian
  sends Halden License, Old Four, Team Twenty-Eight, and Team Seven. Saltmere hosts. The other
  four circuits are named as the lanes need them.
- **The National Measure,** Monday the 14th to Saturday the 19th of the third month, 2681.
  **Four boards,** as at the Regional, each marked by place, twenty-four down to one, and
  added. The Hunt stands where the Regional's Call stood:
  1. **The Bench** — banks of cells with a fault planted; find, quench, re-tune, restart.
  2. **The Carry** — rescue and extraction against a clock.
  3. **The Hunt** — a licensed live hunt on tidal ground under company officers, marked on
     clean yield after shares, less every remark. Students close nothing.
  4. **The Lane** — the declared-role lane, as at home: three for the litter over a team's own
     line, one for a down, two halves a down. Run as a bracket over three days.
- **The national license roll.** The first eight fours are licensed for the year to stand
  Barrel-class responses under a company officer. Placement therefore decides who is sent for.
- **Gate rules.** Every dram is combed. Dray's rule arrives under seal: any candidate with
  more than one door is read from a sanctioned mast by a reader of the Meridian office. The
  host's laid candidates fall under it.
- **The warrant.** A circuit's fours travel on their own books. Planned at three drams a head.
- **Draft boards** sit through the Lane days. A company may card a student; a student may say
  no. A card is not a seat.
- **Travel.** Two hundred miles: the road round the Sag, a ferry, a river, and the coast boat.
  Station transit exists and is too dear for a student four.

## 3. Saltmere (`PLANNED`)

- A harbour city. The Saltmere Company's green is `ESTABLISHED`, as is its harbour bank of
  thirty-six cells, in step since twenty past two on the 17th of the ninth month, 2680.
- **The Ropewalk School** leads the host circuit. It teaches two-door candidates to open both
  doors together and twist them to agree: **a lay,** *laid* doors. It is what Meridian calls a
  stack. It is cheap, quick, and soft, it wins lanes, and its circuit has a column for it. It
  is not foolish. It was built against people and against the spawn of forty years ago.
- **The Graving:** a dry dock roofed with tiers. A floating gate keeps the harbour out. With
  the gate shut and the sluices closed, the dock is a basin of still water whatever the tide
  does outside.
- **The Longshore:** miles of tidal flat and creek where the coast's tears open.
- **The harbour.** Pumps keep the low town dry. The harbour's water moves twice a day and is
  still only at slack, for about twenty minutes. The Ferren method needs still water. Mara's
  sheets were tried at Saltmere in the winter and failed for that reason.
- **Many in Saltmere like the stepped bank.** It has not dropped a stroke. One operator can
  drive it. That is a fair position held by sensible people.

## 4. Spawn in a national season (`PLANNED`)

All new behavior obeys trained anatomy and combines or redirects it. None is a bigger Flask.
Working descriptions follow; the crews on the page give the names, as Book One's did.

- **Packs.** Husks that keep a count among themselves and move on it. Cutting one out of time
  breaks the pack for a few counts. Cutting in time does nothing.
- **A folding hem.** A hem that folds from its far edge, as a catcher's ward does, and so
  comes off a purse sheet.
- **An entering form.** A cord that goes to an open stack as a string goes to water that
  keeps time. A sequenced door gives it nothing to enter. A laid door is an open door.
- **Coast forms.** Things that stand in tidal creeks and work the flood and the ebb, where no
  sheet can be laid and ash goes into the water.
- **The first Barrel.** The expression in the harbour basin, grown at each spring tide. Its
  shape is the author's. It is not the Pit's square and not Stopgate's string abreast.
- **Yield** is higher on average and nearly all of it reads I above trace. Rich ash that
  cannot go on a floor, into a pump house, or into a medical tin.

## 5. Ash in a national season (`PLANNED`)

- **Plain ash is disappearing.** Natural yield carried the Step in trace by the spring of 2680
  (`ESTABLISHED`: "it's all had the hair since the thaw"). By the winter no fresh kill reads
  plain, in Meridian or in the hills.
- **Plain ash is what un-stepping needs:** a jar a cell, of ash that "has never heard a beat."
  Old stock is finite: reference shelves, wardens' winter stores, sealed medical reserves, and
  the cellars of an Order that never stepped anything.
- So the price of ash turns over. The richest red bags are close to worthless for safe work,
  and eight-tenths of a dram of plain ward-trace is worth an argument.
- A healer can ease a **starved** hand with plain ward-trace and cannot ease one that is
  **turned out** (`ESTABLISHED`). Stepped ward-trace is no use for it.

## 6. The handed crossing (`PLANNED`; the working term may be replaced on the page)

- **What it is.** Catch, carry, and plant, each done by a different person through one door,
  with a caller. A hand door lifts and folds. An Ember door takes the folded load across. A
  still door plants it. Between people there is no quarter-dram gap, because nobody shuts and
  opens.
- **What it costs instead.** For an instant at each pass the load belongs to nobody. A pass
  called half a count wrong drops it or nips someone (`ESTABLISHED`: Pru's fingers). Each
  holder must take a count that is not theirs. It needs rehearsal with those exact people.
- **Why it was not already done.** Schools teach one door and declared roles; a pass between
  unlike doors belongs to nobody's curriculum. The Home Line had a word for it once. A
  single-door fighter cannot feel the far side of a gap. Somebody who has been through both
  can call it.
- **Limits.** It is slower to set than a three-door sequence. It fails if any one of three
  people is hurt, late, or unwilling. It cannot be done by strangers. It does not close a
  tear, kill anything, or mend a bank.
- **Kiva's part** is the call, the ground test, and the written columns. Under her certificate
  she may also stand as one link by making one declared crossing on a sanctioned mast.

## 7. What Book Two establishes about the Step, the filter, and the dream (`PLANNED`)

Each line is to be earned by evidence on the page and said by somebody who can be wrong.

1. **The Step is a mark.** It is what rising pressure from beyond the filter leaves in ash.
   Halden did not invent the beat in 2391; the foundation found counting ash, learned to
   induce it, and built an industry on it.
2. **Tears come to it.** A stepped bank is a worked pattern keeping time at scale, and spawn
   expressions echo worked patterns. That is why a hold stood, and why a string went to the
   drain.
3. **People and spawn are answering one pressure.** Noa's columns get a fourth, for ash, and
   it rises with the other three.
4. **It is quickening.** Old Halden bench books give the Step a slower count than today's
   four and four. (The figures are the author's, after reading the patent scene in Book One.)
5. **It is not uniform.** The notch that follows Kiva's cliff by eleven seconds at Meridian
   follows it sooner on the coast. Nobody can say what that distance is a distance to.
6. **The dream is shared.** Annick (Glory, 2431; her entry undated); the Ferren keeper and the
   Saltmere keeper on one night in 2604; Kiva in 2676, proved independent by a sealed and timed
   page; Ambrose Halden in 2604 and since; Clemency Thrale; and returns from other cities'
   remarks columns.
7. **A T-0 can be local and can be wide.** When two Glory fields from unlike traditions touch
   at the Slack, every lamp on the coast shows a point of light, and ports overseas log the
   same minute.
8. **The readings disagree.** Halden: step the country, so that its lamps go white and not
   out. The Even Hand: one door, one quiet thing, and let nothing in that asks. The Works and
   the Line: whatever is coming, do not be one engine tonight. Book Two acts on the third and
   does not disprove the first.

- What the pressure is; why it keeps a count; what is "paying attention."
- The cause of the Fall. The Makers.
- The disk's origin and use, the nine rings, and the first bearer's book. In Book Two the disk
  is cold, including beside two Glory masters.
- The color with no word. Only Kiva and Annick's page have it.
- Whether Hesper Annick died in the winter rifts of 2461.
- What the 2446 and 2604 lamp nights were *caused* by.
- Kiva's classification. No column is made for her in Book Two.
- Whether three doors can be made safe or taught. They are not done in Book Two.
- Any returning figure from the first trilogy.
- A single doctrine. The lay keeps its uses. The Order keeps its door. The handed crossing is
  one method.
- Anything settled between nations.

## 9. Guardrails for the author

- Every climax capability is shown and priced earlier.
- A Glory master on the page has a limit the reader can see: a place they cannot leave, a
  time they cannot exceed, a thing they must hold or let go. No Glory master does a student's
  or a fitter's work.
- No fingers heal. No rank, ash, device, or result restores them.
- No institution is a villain. The Halden Works and the Municipal Works stay distinct.
- Ledgers reconcile to the hundredth.
- Dream knowledge moves only by a decision made on the page.
- Nothing from the research corpus: no names, terms, scenes, or lines.
