Friday the 11th to early Saturday. Kiva focalizes throughout. Morning cold at mid-forearm; three nights remain. Mara and Hart seal a falsifiable basin prediction: Wednesday 23rd dawn, 6:09 within 6:07–6:12; eighteen to twenty-two minutes; ring four feet eleven, at least four ten; inside water 57 or faster, elsewhere no count by hand despite running pumps. Peverell and Yeo witness; Rusk watches writing without endorsing. Clerk receives unopened at 10:07; opens noon 23rd. Mara protects Kiva's choice. Before eight's visit Kiva asks Perrin and Orla to let her refuse for herself. At the Counting House her pulse falls to 57 and doors want to fall in, unlike their lying down near Thrale. One chair farther restores control. The Proprietor has held the foundation cell since 2611; he admits fear of letting go. Plate remains cold, disappointing his hypothesis; he accepts the limit. He recounts his own 2604 fever and white stepped lamps versus extinguished independent lamps, but does not disclose a dream here. Kiva keeps her ferry knowledge private. Forty drams and a Torch-wage roll cannot buy her three-door; she offers certified crossings on ordinary terms. Public authority must publicly change its conditions. Perrin and Orla honor her request, then witness her no. Fever rises to 112 after departure, breaks about three. No roof opens, no dream comes.
