## Entry state and pressure

It is Sunday the 1st of the second month, 2681. Thirty days remain before the
road south; forty-four days remain to the National Measure's first board. Team
Seven has a signed entry and an unwilling captain, but no Flask license in its
own right. A four without one hunts attached.

The team book holds **9.64 drams**. Of that, 4.0 is spoken for by Hunt Marshal
Dinah Coombe's floor and 1.8 was planned for hand-to-Ember work, leaving only
3.84 uncommitted before call reserve, cord, food, travel, or failure. The sealed
0.8 of plain ward-trace remains unopened and is worth more than the rest of the
book because no new yield is coming up plain.

Kiva Rowan remains seated, unclassified, and limited by Aske's card: single doors,
declared, one at a time; **one crossing in seven days at most**; the hand seen the
next morning; no three-door sequence. Her first two left fingers report pressure
and whether a thing keeps time, never what time. Her knee is strapped. Her three
doors are three clean. Her one certified crossing so far was Ember to still on
the 7th of the first month. The disk is cold. The dream has not recurred.

Pike's hands are taken above both wrists for six weeks. Four nights remain on
Rhea Sorn's card, two in this month and two in the third: Varley handles the
cloth, Pike rides as a voice, and Kiva remains second to Pike. The far edge is
the second's to say—two words, once.

The competition four is Kiva (catcher), Perrin Cade (captain/carry), Anwen Pryce
(hold), and Jude Talley (kill), with Pru Finch as reserve and second catcher.
Kellan Renn stands for Halden License and remains on Seven's book for ordinary
calls in Meridian.

The pressure is practical: build a four that can survive a licensed hunt against
forms whose behavior is changing, learn without spending the road money, and find
plain ash for a medical reserve in a country where nothing killed comes up quiet.

## Movement change

Several connected chapters should let training become knowledge rather than a
montage. Dinah Coombe is the visible standard above Torch: a Blaze, the Charter
Board's Hunt Marshal, and a teacher who uses drills rather than speeches. She has
run mixed fours on the coast for thirty years. Under her, Seven encounters three
new problems in useful sequence—failed attempt, isolated drill, then pressure:

- packs that keep a count among themselves;
- a shed hem that folds like a catcher's ward;
- a form that treats a stacked or laid door as an invitation.

Do not turn those into three lectures or three interchangeable sparring chapters.
Each should change the team's language, spacing, or responsibility. Develop at
least one training sequence over multiple scenes so the reader can anticipate the
correction before it works.

From pieces already on the page—the plank, Pru's fold, the old word *crossing*,
Pike's seven columns, and the rule *mine before yours*—the four begins assembling
the **handed crossing**: catch, carry, and plant performed by three people with
unlike doors, called by somebody who understands both gaps. It costs little ash
and a great deal of declared trust. It may work in a controlled yard here; it
does not receive its decisive live proof until the Longshore later in the book.

## Consequences to land

- **Coombe arrives and teaches on the page.** Her competence should be seen in
  diagnosis, drill design, and what she refuses to let students pretend.
- **The hand-to-Ember gap is entered under Aske's card.** The earlier plan said
  six crossings during this movement, but the calendar permits at most five in
  the thirty-day second month under the canon one-in-seven-days limit. The power
  limit governs. Do not manufacture a sixth. Let certification progress honestly;
  defer an unfinished sixth requirement if necessary and record the surviving
  count exactly.
- **Two second-month van nights occur** with Pike as voice and Varley at the
  cloth. Carry the injury and the altered crew mechanics. At least one night
  should test a behavior Coombe is teaching, without making the professional
  call merely another student floor.
- **A developed learning fight against Halden License** puts Kellan opposite
  Seven. Establish terrain, starting positions, reach, declared doors, objectives,
  and limits. Both sides adapt. Kellan is neither villain nor traitor. The fight
  leaves a correction Seven can deliberately attempt again.
- **Seven earns its Flask license in its own right** through shown competence,
  not a paperwork gift. The license changes what they may risk and whose name
  stands over the risk.
- **Seven's first licensed hunt in the hills around the Sag** is a major set
  piece. They go looking for something quiet enough to refill the sealed medical
  reserve. Render terrain, signs, pursuit, the team plan, the spawn's changing
  decisions, ash grading, and the return. They kill well. Nothing comes up plain.
  The disappointment matters because the fight itself was a real success.
- **The ash ledger reconciles to the hundredth.** Every floor, crossing, call,
  yield, grade, share, and refused purchase is entered. No free practice fuel.
- **The Order of the Even Hand becomes the only known source of plain ash on the
  road south.** Its door has been on the latch since summer. The team reaches it
  or makes the binding choice that takes them through it on the road. Kiva meets
  Clemency Thrale, a Glory master with one door for life. Thrale is not an oracle
  and the Order is not a villain.
- **Kiva stands near a Glory master for the first time.** The dream returns with
  a fever on the ferry. Preserve uncertainty: do not explain the filter, the
  pressure, the count, or what the dream means.
- **Exit:** Seven is on the road south with its Flask license, a rehearsed method,
  an exact depleted ledger, and a problem the hills did not solve. The next
  movement begins at Saltmere on Tuesday the 1st of the third month.

## Character and viewpoint work

Kiva remains the dominant lens. Aim near or slightly above 87% Kiva in this
movement so the cumulative book moves toward the formula after Movement One's
86.2%. Use only the five established secondary lenses—Anwen, Kellan, Mara, Orla,
and Rhea—and only where Kiva cannot witness an essential choice. Rhea already
carried the largest secondary share in Movement One; do not give her another
large cutaway merely because the van is present. Perrin, Pru, Jude, Pike, Coombe,
Aske, and Thrale develop through what Kiva sees.

Give independent choices or realizations to the team, not only Kiva:

- Perrin learns to captain before he learns to enjoy the word.
- Anwen's hold is not simply support for Kiva; it is a field skill that can lead.
- Jude's kill work and his question about what ash is for both matter to the hunt.
- Pru is a catcher with her own sheet, not Kiva's cord.
- Kellan's opposing method should be credible enough to teach Seven something.
- Pike must remain useful without his hands and must pay an emotional price for
  letting another person perform what his body used to know.

Keep the established humor and warmth. Do not ration jokes per chapter. Do not
let catchphrases replace fresh behavior.
