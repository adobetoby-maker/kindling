## 1. Unscored reader response

**Where interest caught.** The first page works. A girl cannot press a wax seal and says so, and
the mate asks the fairest question of her week: *"What does a catcher do with a hand like that?"*
That question carries the movement. The next hook is the drum: two people, a needle and a cork,
saying *now* together "like two people saying grace." It is the best small idea in the movement,
and its plain, wordless proof is very satisfying. Interest catches again at Jude's question in
the cold Low Lane (ch. 2), at Pike's thumb (ch. 6), and fully at the coal wharf (ch. 7). In the
wharf chapter the laundry from chapter 3 comes back as a lethal mechanism. That is the movement's
best payoff, and a reader feels it land.

**Where it slipped.** The Charter Board notice (ch. 2, first section) gives about 1,300 words to
rules: four boards, the roll, steps and licences, Handful and Flask, and clean yield. Chapter 3's
long section of Kiva rereading her own book is quiet in a different way: it is reflective, it
quotes earlier entries, and the stakes are internal. The finger proof (ch. 4) is clever but runs
twenty trials before the result is read out. The Rowan kitchen sum (ch. 8) is the third time the
reader is given the warrant arithmetic. In the opening half, the hand injury is the only danger.
A physical threat first arrives at the kiln (ch. 5 §4), about 54% of the way through by word count.

**Who matters.** Kiva, clearly. After her, **Pike**: his habits, his thumb, his reach and his
"Don't make a wound of it" make him the movement's second heart. Pru comes third, and Perrin is
the warmth.

**What I expect next.** Coombe's floors, and the bedsheet drill tested against something real.
Hart's answer about his own hand. Kiva's six hand-to-Ember crossings against a book that cannot
pay for them. Pike riding as a voice on the February nights. Kellan across a lane in black and
gold.

**Would I continue?** Yes. That holds for both lenses below, but the younger reader needs chapter 5
to come soon enough.

\*Narration is computed with quoted spans removed. Leftover tags ("said Kiva.") count as short
sentences, so the narration figures for short sentences are inflated and those for long sentences
are slightly deflated. 5,504 sentences in all; dialogue is 37.2% of words.

**Read.** The drift is not the uniform mid-length prose the formula warns about. It is the
opposite: the prose is **uniformly short**. Every chapter sits between 39% and 46% short sentences
and has a median of 6–7. The longest sentence in the movement is 63 words (ch. 4 §2), and only
about 28 sentences reach 40 words. The short beat is the default texture rather than an accent,
so the wharf's short beats have little contrast to land against.

**Formula conflict to bring to the owner.** A mean of 14.6 with a population SD of about 26 and a
27.7% short share needs a heavy tail of very long sentences. Typographic prose rarely produces
that. The source figures come from ASR segmentation, where the pause and punctuation choices are
Whisper's. The targets are left unchanged as instructed, but the SD target may not be reachable
without making the prose run on. Dialogue alone sits at 58% short. Hitting 27.7% across the whole
book would mean reshaping dialogue as well as narration. The owner should decide whether the
sentence targets govern dialogue.

## 5. Repair brief — three priorities, bounded

**P1 — Number, calendar and tally hygiene.**
- *Location:* the three real month names (ch. 4 §1, ch. 5 §5, ch. 8 §1); Mara's "five of them"
  (ch. 1 §5); the Low Lane count and entry (ch. 2 §2–3); Perrin's "four days" (ch. 1 §7);
  optionally the drum skip (ch. 1 or ch. 4) and the off-page cuts (ch. 2 entry).
- *Issue:* the arithmetic and calendar break inside a book that trains its reader to check sums.
- *Effect:* "That adds" is a running promise. A wrong sum or a "January" in a numbered-month world
  is exactly what this book's reader is primed to catch, and it costs trust in every later figure.
- *Scope:* about six to ten line edits. No scene changes.
- *Preserve:* the ledger device, Kellan's sums, and the exact dates. Fix the counts, not the habit.

**P2 — Restore long-sentence range in narration (formula drift, all chapters).**
- *Location:* the reflective and explanatory narration first: ch. 2 §4 (Orla), ch. 3 §2–3 (the copy;
  the page of the sixth), ch. 4 §2 (Mara), ch. 6 §1–2 and §6 (the columns; Kellan), and ch. 8 §1.
- *Issue:* mean 8.8 against 14.6, 43% short against 27.7%, 0.5% long against 3.3%. The texture is
  short everywhere.
- *Effect:* the quiet stretches read staccato aloud. The short beats lose their punch because
  nothing around them is long. Reading level also sits far off target (FK 1.6).
- *Scope:* a focused same-author pass on narration only. Join adjacent declaratives into
  compound-complex sentences where the thought is continuous, and allow one 40-plus-word sentence
  per scene where a thought earns it. Do **not** touch dialogue until the owner rules on whether the
  sentence targets govern dialogue (§4a). A reasonable interim aim is a movement mean of 11–12 and
  about 35% short, measured with the same script. That aim is an editorial suggestion, not a
  revised target.
- *Preserve:* the wharf's action rhythm (ch. 7 §2 to §4) and the short beats that end scenes ("It
  did not hold." / "Nobody had it."). Do not shorten any fight.

**P3 — Thin the rules and figures for the younger lens.**
- *Location:* ch. 2 §1 (the notice); ch. 6 §3 (the conditions); ch. 8 §2 (the sum) and §4 (the ash
  office entry). Spot clarity edits throughout for causal *since* where it could read as time
  ("since the cell was right there"), and the heaviest "-and-twenty" clusters.
- *Issue:* the warrant arithmetic is given in full three times. Steps against licences, and
  Handful against Flask, arrive as lecture.
- *Effect:* this is the single biggest drag on the 13-year-old's keep-reading and clarity scores,
  and it delays the movement's pressure.
- *Scope:* keep one full sum, where Kellan's motive is in the scene (ch. 8 §2), and reduce the
  others to a line each. In ch. 2 §1, move the Hunt's small print later, to the moment Jude reacts
  to it. Replace roughly a third of causal *since* with *because* or *as* where it is ambiguous.
  This does not cut any action.
- *Preserve:* Kellan's numbers as character ("He had been waiting… so that he could be in the
  room"). Keep the register, including *since*, wherever it is unambiguous.

**Not prioritised, for the owner:** Rhea's lens share (§4f); progression front-loading against

## 6. Uncertainty and limits

- The syllable estimator and sentence splitter are heuristics. They are stable for comparing runs
  but are not ground truth. Comparisons with the ASR-derived source are directional.
- The POV, development and supporting-beat counts are manual judgments by the reviewer.
- This is one movement of a planned six. Book-level targets (front-loading, POV allocation, the
  development spread) are provisional until later movements exist.
- A same-model review shares the drafter's blind spots. A human cold read of chapters 2–4, by a
  reader near thirteen, would test this review's main claim about pacing better than any score
  here.

## 7. Method — the full script used for §4

POV segmentation used by the script: ch. 2 section index 3 (Orla), ch. 4 index 1 (Mara), ch. 6
index 5 (Kellan), ch. 7 indices 2 and 5 (Rhea). Indices are 0-based, split on `---` lines, so
index 3 is the fourth section. The rest is Kiva. Literal-door adjustment: a phrase grep of
"(by the|open|green|house|through the|at the|to the|oven|shed|colleague's|laundry|side|every)
door(s)". Narration and dialogue split: quoted spans removed with `"[^"]*"`. Dialogue tags:
`["”]\s+(said|asked|answered|replied|called|shouted|added|told|whispered|murmured|cried)\b`.

```python
#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""Movement One numerical pass. Pure stdlib. Usage: python3 -I m1_metrics.py <manuscript_dir>"""
import re, sys, statistics as st, json, collections, os

MD = sys.argv[1]
FILES = [os.path.join(MD, f"chapter-0{i}.md") for i in range(1, 9)]

# POV segments: (chapter, 0-based section index) -> lens. Sections split on '---' lines; section 0 includes the heading.
POV = {(2, 3): "Orla", (4, 1): "Mara", (6, 5): "Kellan", (7, 2): "Rhea", (7, 5): "Rhea"}

ABBR = re.compile(r"\b(Mrs|Mr|Dr|St|Mt)\.")
INIT = re.compile(r"\b([A-Z])\.(?=\s*[A-Z])")  # K. Rowan, J.H., M.R.

def clean(t):
    t = t.replace("—", " — ")
    t = re.sub(r"[*_]", "", t)
    return t

def sentences(par):
    p = ABBR.sub(lambda m: m.group(1) + "<DOT>", par)
    p = INIT.sub(lambda m: m.group(1) + "<DOT>", p)
    parts = re.split(r"(?<=[.!?])[\"”’')]*\s+(?=[\"“‘(]?[A-Z0-9])", p)
    out = []
    for s in parts:
        s = s.replace("<DOT>", ".").strip()
        if words(s):
            out.append(s)
    return out

WORD = re.compile(r"[A-Za-z0-9]+(?:['’\-][A-Za-z0-9]+)*")
def words(s):
    return WORD.findall(s)

def syllables(w):
    w = w.lower()
    if w.isdigit():
        return max(1, len(w))  # digits rare; crude
    w = re.sub(r"[^a-z]", "", w)
    if not w:
        return 0
    if len(w) <= 3:
        return 1
    w2 = re.sub(r"(?:[^laeiouy]es|ed|[^laeiouy]e)$", "", w)
    w2 = re.sub(r"^y", "", w2)
    n = len(re.findall(r"[aeiouy]{1,2}", w2))
    return max(1, n)

chapters = []
for ci, path in enumerate(FILES, 1):
    raw = open(path, encoding="utf-8").read()
    secs = re.split(r"\n\s*---\s*\n", raw)
    chapters.append(secs)

all_sents, all_pars, sec_rows = [], [], []
corpus_words = []
pov_words = collections.Counter()
for ci, secs in enumerate(chapters, 1):
    for si, sec in enumerate(secs):
        lens = POV.get((ci, si), "Kiva")
        pars = [p.strip() for p in re.split(r"\n\s*\n", sec) if p.strip()]
        sec_w = 0
        for p in pars:
            if p.startswith("#"):
                continue
            c = clean(p)
            w = words(c)
            all_pars.append(len(w))
            sec_w += len(w)
            corpus_words.extend(w)
            for s in sentences(c):
                all_sents.append((ci, si, len(words(s)), s))
        pov_words[lens] += sec_w
        sec_rows.append((ci, si, lens, sec_w))

N = len(corpus_words)
sl = [x[2] for x in all_sents]
res = {}
res["words_body"] = N
res["sentences"] = len(sl)
res["sent_mean"] = round(st.mean(sl), 2)
res["sent_median"] = st.median(sl)
res["sent_pstdev"] = round(st.pstdev(sl), 2)
res["share_le5"] = round(100 * sum(1 for x in sl if x <= 5) / len(sl), 1)
res["share_ge40"] = round(100 * sum(1 for x in sl if x >= 40) / len(sl), 1)
res["max_sentence"] = max(sl)
res["paragraphs"] = len(all_pars)
res["par_mean"] = round(st.mean(all_pars), 1)
res["par_median"] = st.median(all_pars)
res["par_share_over_60"] = round(100 * sum(1 for x in all_pars if x > 60) / len(all_pars), 1)
res["sections"] = len(sec_rows)
res["sec_mean"] = round(N / len(sec_rows))
res["breaks_per_10k_incl_chapter_starts"] = round(len(sec_rows) / N * 10000, 2)
res["breaks_per_10k_internal_only"] = round((len(sec_rows) - 8) / N * 10000, 2)
syl = sum(syllables(w) for w in corpus_words)
wps = N / len(sl)
spw = syl / N
res["flesch_reading_ease"] = round(206.835 - 1.015 * wps - 84.6 * spw, 1)
res["fk_grade"] = round(0.39 * wps + 11.8 * spw - 15.59, 1)
res["syll_per_word"] = round(spw, 3)

# per chapter sentence stats
perch = {}
for ci in range(1, 9):
    s = [x[2] for x in all_sents if x[0] == ci]
    cw = sum(r[3] for r in sec_rows if r[0] == ci)
    perch[ci] = dict(words=cw, sents=len(s), mean=round(st.mean(s), 1), median=st.median(s),
                     le5=round(100 * sum(1 for x in s if x <= 5) / len(s), 1),
                     ge40=round(100 * sum(1 for x in s if x >= 40) / len(s), 1),
                     sections=sum(1 for r in sec_rows if r[0] == ci))
res["per_chapter"] = perch
res["pov_words"] = dict(pov_words)
res["pov_pct"] = {k: round(100 * v / N, 1) for k, v in pov_words.items()}
res["sections_rows"] = sec_rows

text = " ".join(clean(open(p, encoding="utf-8").read()) for p in FILES)
tl = text.lower()

def per10k(n): return round(n / N * 10000, 1)

# Progression lexicon (project-specific; case-sensitive where the stat name collides with ordinary English)
PROG_CS = [r"\bDraw\b", r"\bHold\b", r"\bEdge\b", r"\bStance\b", r"\bWard\b", r"\bRank\b", r"\bFlame\b", r"\bFire\b",
           r"\bTorch\b", r"\bBlaze\b", r"\bGlory\b", r"\bKindled\b", r"\bEmber\b", r"\bHandful\b", r"\bFlask\b", r"\bBarrel\b", r"\bWell\b(?= class)"]
PROG_CI = [r"\brank\b", r"\branks\b", r"\bdoors?\b", r"\bcrossings?\b", r"\bcrossed\b", r"\bstack(s|ed)?\b", r"\bcertif\w*", r"\bclassif\w*",
           r"\bunclassified\b", r"\blicen[cs]e[sd]?\b", r"\bsecond step\b", r"\bfirst step\b", r"\bthimble\b", r"\bre-reading\b",
           r"\bsanctioned mast\b", r"\bcliff\b", r"\bseated\b", r"\bnational license roll\b", r"\bcolumn for\b"]
prog_hits = collections.Counter()
for pat in PROG_CS:
    prog_hits[pat] = len(re.findall(pat, text))
for pat in PROG_CI:
    # avoid double count of 'rank' already counted case-sensitive 'Rank'
    prog_hits[pat] = len(re.findall(pat, text if pat == r"\brank\b" else tl))
# 'Rank' CS and 'rank' CS are disjoint because CI pattern above for rank uses original case text (lowercase only)
prog_total = sum(prog_hits.values())
res["prog_hits"] = {k: v for k, v in prog_hits.items() if v}
res["prog_total"] = prog_total
res["prog_per10k"] = per10k(prog_total)
# by movement thirds (equal word count)
third = N // 3
idx_words = WORD.findall(text)
bounds = [0, third, 2 * third, len(idx_words)]
def prog_in(span_words):
    t = " ".join(span_words)
    n = sum(len(re.findall(p, t)) for p in PROG_CS)
    n += sum(len(re.findall(p, t if p == r"\brank\b" else t.lower())) for p in PROG_CI)
    return n
res["prog_by_movement_third"] = [prog_in(idx_words[bounds[i]:bounds[i + 1]]) for i in range(3)]
# broad tier: ash economy
ECON = [r"\bdrams?\b", r"\bash\b", r"\bheld\b(?=:)", r"\bwarrant\b", r"\bhundredths\b", r"\btenths\b"]
econ = {p: len(re.findall(p, tl)) for p in ECON}
res["econ_hits"] = econ
res["econ_per10k"] = per10k(sum(econ.values()))

# -ly words
ly = re.findall(r"\b[a-z]+ly\b", tl)
LY_EXCL = {"only", "early", "family", "likely", "ugly", "holy", "july", "reply", "apply", "supply", "fly", "belly", "jelly",
           "rally", "ally", "lily", "silly", "bully", "folly", "hilly", "curly", "oily", "lonely", "lovely", "friendly", "elderly",
           "orderly", "kindly_adj", "daily", "weekly", "monthly", "yearly", "nightly", "costly", "deadly", "lively", "comely",
           "unlikely", "wily", "jolly", "woolly", "assembly", "anomaly", "italy", "sly", "ply", "dolly", "gully", "sully", "tally",
           "rely", "imply", "comply", "multiply", "underbelly", "manly", "womanly", "homely", "surly", "burly", "chilly", "grisly",
           "measly", "steely", "princely", "stately", "unruly", "beastly", "ghostly", "leisurely", "cowardly", "scholarly",
           "kelly", "ely", "billy", "bully", "fully_never"}
ly_raw = len(ly)
ly_f = [w for w in ly if w not in LY_EXCL]
res["ly_raw_per10k"] = per10k(ly_raw)
res["ly_filtered_per10k"] = per10k(len(ly_f))
res["ly_top"] = collections.Counter(ly_f).most_common(15)

REPORT = r"\b(said|says|asked|replied|answered|shouted|whispered|muttered|murmured|added|cried|called|told)\b"
rep = re.findall(REPORT, tl)
res["reporting_per10k"] = per10k(len(rep))
res["said_per10k"] = per10k(tl.split().count("said") + len(re.findall(r"\bsaid\b", tl)) - tl.split().count("said"))
res["reporting_top"] = collections.Counter(rep).most_common(10)

COMBAT = r"\b(blade|blades|cut|cuts|cutting|strike|struck|hit|hits|kill|kills|killed|fight|fought|fighting|block|blocked|dodge|dodged|attack|attacked|lunge|swing|swung|slash|wound|wounds|hurt)\b"
cb = re.findall(COMBAT, tl)
res["combat_per10k"] = per10k(len(cb))
res["combat_top"] = collections.Counter(cb).most_common(12)

FILL = ["that", "just", "almost", "felt", "seemed", "since", "rather", "quite", "one", "a good deal"]
res["fillers_per10k"] = {f: per10k(len(re.findall(r"\b" + f + r"\b", tl))) for f in FILL}

TICS = {"I'd like it known": r"i'd like it known", "I want it said": r"i want it said", "That adds": r"that adds",
        "nearly smiled": r"nearly smiled", "scratched his jaw": r"scratched his jaw", "went pink": r"went pink",
        "went scarlet/red": r"went (scarlet|red)", "ears (went|had gone) white": r"ears (went|had gone) white",
        "a good deal": r"a good deal", "four-and-twenty style numerals": r"\b(\w+)-and-(twenty|thirty|forty|fifty)\b",
        "as one ...s (generic 'one')": r"\bas one \w+s\b", "with care": r"with care",
        "for some time": r"for some time", "said nothing": r"said nothing", "I'd sooner": r"i'd sooner"}
res["tics"] = {k: len(re.findall(v, tl)) for k, v in TICS.items()}

# 3-grams
toks = [w.lower() for w in idx_words]
tg = collections.Counter(zip(toks, toks[1:], toks[2:]))
res["top_trigrams_per10k"] = [(" ".join(k), v, per10k(v)) for k, v in tg.most_common(15)]
for ph in [("out", "of", "the"), ("one", "of", "the"), ("for", "a", "moment")]:
    res.setdefault("ref_trigrams", {})[" ".join(ph)] = per10k(tg[ph])

# development proxy (regex): Kiva/she within 120 chars of marker
MARK = r"(realized|realised|decided|for the first time|swore|no longer|found that|understood|made herself|saw that|knew that|had not known|had thought)"
prox = 0
for m in re.finditer(MARK, text):
    win = text[max(0, m.start() - 120): m.end() + 120]
    if re.search(r"\b(Kiva|she|She|her)\b", win):
        prox += 1
res["dev_proxy_markers_total"] = len(re.findall(MARK, text))
res["dev_proxy_kiva_window"] = prox
res["dev_proxy_per10k"] = per10k(prox)

# longest sentences
res["longest"] = [(c, s, n, t[:160]) for c, s, n, t in sorted(all_sents, key=lambda x: -x[2])[:6]]
# month names
res["month_names"] = re.findall(r"\b(January|February|March|April|June|July|August|September|October|November|December)\b", text)
res["reserved_terms"] = {t: len(re.findall(t, text)) for t in ["Unroofed", "Breach", "handed crossing"]}

json.dump(res, sys.stdout, indent=1, default=str)
```
