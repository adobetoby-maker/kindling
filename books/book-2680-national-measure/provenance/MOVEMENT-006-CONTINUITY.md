## 2. Rehearsal and the Slack

**The one idea (Mara, Monday):** "It's a bucket with no bottom that holds water anyway, because the water's keeping the
town's time. If it spills in the harbour, the time goes back into the harbour, and the bank can't come out. If we cut it
where it stands, there's a Barrel of open ash in the water the pumps draw from, and that can cook a bank. So we pick it up
full, and we carry it out of the harbour without spilling it, and we empty it in the sink. The sink's the Graving."
**Brask's naming:** a cask with staves and a chime and no head; "the water's its head. And the head's keeping time."
**Mara's winter finding:** the six cells that went back in during the winter trial went back at the end of the slack,
because the ring lay down and gave back what it held.

**Bearings (on the page):** pump house at the basin's head; six pumps; thirty-six cells on two galleries; iron landing and
grating; the ring ten paces from the outfalls (a circle of Hart's stakes marks its rim). The Graving lies along the basin's

ring; **M1**, a stake 25 paces west; **M2**, the stone sill of the dock mouth, 25 more; **M3**, Noa's chalk line **45 paces

## 4. The sequence asked and refused

**At 5.40 on the Slack's morning the Proprietor asks Kiva** (from his chair at the Long Quay's end, thirty feet): "If two of
your links fail… and a third is failing… Will you do the three?" **"No."** Why: "if this morning needs my three, it's not a
method. It's me… The bank has to come out on things with handles… I don't know what a fourth costs… I'll not spend a thing I
can't price." He: "That was a girl saying no to a rich man. This is a catcher saying no to a method… I'll not ask again."
(The first refusal was the third movement's, on a Halden mast.) **Three clean, unchanged. No third-door attempt. No
fourth.**

## 9. The grid order (Friday 25th, the Salt Hall, the Clerk, eleven minutes)

Every municipal bank in the country (pump house, lamp office, drum, tenement box, tram shed) on its own count, no two alike
in any house, each pump on its own cells, **kept there**; no municipal bank stepped from this day nor joined by water or
iron to a stepped one; any fallen in to be taken out by the Board's officers as plain ash can be found; **every jar and tin
of plain declared to the Board within the year (declared, not taken), allotted to banks first and hands with them, and no
bank before a hand**; a **travelling commission under D. Coombe**, with readers and officers lent, to every municipal bank on
the roll within two years, writing in seven columns what was got wrong first.
**Price (Kellan):** about **a tenth of the light** (Ferren lost a ninth since the autumn; Saltmere the same since
Wednesday); **a third again in fitters' wages** (Peverell's book), the towns' to pay; **the foundation's municipal licences:
a hundred and twelve works stepped under licence since 2391**, its oldest income. ("Its oldest property was a cell under a
glass… It's not in the Board's order.") Counsel: the foundation will not contest; its own yards stay stepped. The
Proprietor's slip: *I am not certain I was wrong… On Wednesday they did neither.* The Clerk: "The Board doesn't say he was
wrong… a fitter's rule is all it's got." **Not settled whether he was right.**

## 10. The 30th and the commission

Rhea asked whether Kiva would want her to ask Graywater for her as Pike's second next winter: **Kiva: "No"** (she would stay;

You're not."). The sheet (Rhea's hand, under Coombe's): *Rowan, K. Catcher. Caller of the pass in three… One door declared at
every response. Two crossings certified… one in fourteen days, by her healer's card; the three doors not to be asked. Dark
hands till her healer's card is up, from the 15th of the fourth month. Under D. Coombe's hand. Until the last of the ninth
month. Then we look again, and either of us may say no.* **Kiva's two conditions, said first, written by Coombe:** (1) at
every bank she teaches the call to two who are not her and leaves it in seven columns in that house's book, and the next

thing on a table herself if there is a thing; nobody asks. *Accepted. D. Coombe, Hunt Marshal.* Signed K. Rowan and M.
Rowan (guardian). Rhea's name on it nowhere.

## 11. The last image (Thursday the 1st, about 6.55 p.m., the end of the Mole; Jude and Noa present)

The coast's lamp offices from Saltmere to Westerley, and ships in the roads, reported the same nine seconds (the previous
Thursday and Friday; in Noa's column). New wires from over the water via the cable office at Westerley: **three ports** (two
quoted; one in another tongue, translated) logged every lamp to a point at **6.22 Saltmere time on the 23rd, nine seconds**;
the third, the ship's engineer's port: *OUR HARBOUR BANK FELL INTO STEP 17TH NINTH MONTH LAST AT AN HOUR WHICH RECKONED TO
SALTMERE TIME IS TWENTY PAST TWO AFTERNOON. AS OUR ENGINEER REPORTED. VOUCHED.* Noa struck *not entered as fact*, neatly.
**A hull standing in from the south** on the evening flood, a light on its bow like no lamp in Saltmere (not white, not
yellow). Kiva's thought, unspoken: somebody aboard dreamed, saw the lamps go to a point, and came to see who answered. **Not
met, named or explained. The disk is cold. She writes it** in the fourth column, right-handed.
