## Lane rules used on the page

Pen 40 paces x 20 (Graving). Hurdles hip-high hazel at each end; chalk squares (no still door plants in a
square; a foot planted in a square is a down). Litter = the Commodore. Three for the litter over your own
hurdle (passed two to two, top to top); a down (flat of an edge on body or head) is one; a touch on a limb is a
half, two halves on one fighter in one carry make a down; a downed fighter kneels a count of ten, lets go of the
litter, and nobody touches them. If the glass runs, a point to the half the litter lies in. Three carries. A
change of door must be declared to a count by the captain's voice ("Rowan, changing on three"); the marshal's
flag goes up; the change is made on three exactly. Candidates under Dray's rule read from the Board's lane mast
on the Graving floor (Noa, Polglase).

Doors in a lane: edge downs; ward walls (Anwen) or a catcher's tray (Pru, Treen); stance plants (cannot be
shifted; holds the hurdle); Kiva's Ember gives a low bound of a few paces.
