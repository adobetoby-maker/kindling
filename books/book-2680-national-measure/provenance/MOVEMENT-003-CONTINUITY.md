## 5. Saltmere, as on the page

- **The Mole** (a mile of curved sea wall); **the sea gate** (black iron on chains in a stone arch, ten feet
  above the water; down only in gales; not since the winter before last); **the low town** below high water,
  kept dry by the pumps; drains with iron gratings that rattle on the count; **the high town** on a ridge.
- **The harbour pump house** (*Built 2398. Re-celled 2611.*): thirty-six cells on two galleries, eighteen a
  side; **six pumps** on one stroke; keeper's desk and **press** of books back to 2398; **outfall gallery**,
  iron landing, ladder, **grating** over a culvert; **the basin's tide staff** (0 at the mud to 24 ft).
- **The basin's slack lags the sea gate's table by three-and-twenty minutes** (Mara found it Wed 2nd night).
- **The Regiment:** the bank's name on the bills. **Fifty-seven to the minute** (Adlam, timed Sat 5th).
- **The Ropewalk:** a quarter-mile tarred shed on the ridge; pen at the near end twelve paces wide; the
  **Commodore** (canvas dummy, tarred hat, painted moustache). Up **Tar Lane**.
- **The Graving:** a dry dock three hundred paces long, as deep as a church, tiers roofed with iron and
  glass, a floating gate at the seaward end; floor eighty feet down. The Board's **gate** is in the old
  **timber pond** beside it, half boarded; the **gate mast** (forty feet, eight pens).
- **The Long Quay**, **Quay Street**, **Quiller's** chandlery (Meridian's loft), the **Company's van yard**,
  the **Counting House** (Halden's house on the Long Quay; white unflickering lamps; the long room).
- **The Longshore:** flats east; **Gannet Creek**; an old stone causeway ("the hard").
- **Coast craft (as on the page):** cut only at the **slack**; ward-walls laid **flat on water** as a boom;
  **purses** — sheets on iron rings with drawstrings, set at low water, hauled from the bank; "a shut purse
  is a sealed bag" on this coast; Penhale's count "one fathom, two fathom."
- **A kiddle:** seven grey posts across a creek mouth, skins between them like nets; "works" the flood and
  the ebb (leans with the water; water through the skin comes out frosted); **stands upright at slack**; can
  **shed a rung uncut** at the top of a surge; a cut post's skin **folds from its far edge** toward the
  nearest line. Tear in the bed under the middle post, closed at low water by a licensed crew.

## 6. The Hoop and the prediction

- **The Hoop** (low-town children's name; Peverell's "the ring at the outfall"): a grey ring, frost on its lip,
  "as wide as a cart is long," rising from the mud ten paces out from the grating **at the start of a spring
  low-water slack** and lying down at its end. Inside it, harbour water **held level with its lip**, above the
  basin, **trembling four and four at the bank's count**. While it stands, **Hart's hand on the grating is
  quiet** (nothing in the basin keeps time) though the pumps run. It lies down and the held water spills in a
  white sheet. First noted the **26th of the ninth month, a hand high**; **4 ft 1 in** at the springs a
  fortnight before the 8th; **4 ft 6½ in** on the 8th and the 9th. Stood 6.12–6.33 (8th, 21 minutes)
  *(repair; was 6.14–6.35)*, 6.13–6.33 (9th, 20 minutes).
- **Readings voiced (not established):** Hart, to Mara on the 8th, aloud for the first time in six months:
  "as if the count goes out of the harbour at the slack and into that." Mara asked only "How". Kiva on the
  9th said it unprompted ("It's gone out of the harbour, into that") and added "taking it in. Holding it."
  Hart: "Somebody else got there by herself." *(repair; was "Mara (the 8th) and Kiva (the 9th) said the
  same")* Jude: "twenty minutes when you can see what's
  keeping time." Mara: "the harbour was still at the slack. All of it, except one thing."
- **Seen by:** Hart, Peverell, Yeo, Rusk, Mara (8th); Hart, Kiva, Jude, Mara, Peverell (9th); the low town's
  children at the springs.
- **The prediction**, written by Mara, sealed by Peverell with the pump house seal, received at the Board's

## 8. The Proprietor (Ambrose Halden) as on the page

- Looks about fifty-five; dark hair grey at the temples; lean; plain dark coat. **States he is a hundred and
  three**; was **twenty-six** and **a bench clerk in the Counting House** in 2604.
- **His limit:** his **left hand lies on a glass case over the foundation cell** (the first stepped, 2391,
  "by a man whose name nobody remembers"). **He has not lifted it for more than a minute since 2611**, when
  the holder before him died with his hand on the glass. He eats with his right; a servant cuts his meat;
  he sleeps with the case by the bed. He cannot rise to greet. "I don't know what would happen if I let go…
  I'm not brave enough."
- **Effect:** people near him "fall in with it" — pulse slows to his count (Kellan felt it; Kiva to 57);
