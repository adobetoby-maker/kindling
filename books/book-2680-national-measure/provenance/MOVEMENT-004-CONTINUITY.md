## 4. Mallow Creek

- **Terrain:** a creek running south to the sea, thirty paces wide at the top; the **spur**, an old stone cart track
  off the causeway, along the east bank; marsh to the west. Two **stows**, sixty paces apart: the upper (Seven's),
  the lower (the Tarn's) in the bend, where the channel is deeper on the outside, so its far post (*(repair)* on
  the west side of the bend; its sleeve lay downstream on the west mud and swung on that post from downstream to
  inland over the west mud, closed end last) stood in a yard of
  water at low slack and needed the punt. A **tear** in the bed under each stow's mouth.
- **The stow** (coast name, after stow-nets): two grey posts eight paces apart with the mouth of a long grey skin
  hung between them; behind it a sleeve ten or twelve paces long, closed at the far end, streaming with the tide;
  works the ebb (water out through its sides frosted); swings on its posts like a vane to work the flood; at slack
  the posts stand and the sleeve lies down on the mud. Sheds rungs uncut on a surge. **With one post cut, the
  freed side of the mouth falls and the sleeve's far end lifts and folds toward the nearest line. With both cut,
  the stow dies at once and goes to a film of ash.** On a standing post it can swing.
- **Evidence:** the upper stow leaned *before* the 10.50 gust reached it (Kiva, Pru). The lower stow turned at
  **12.44** on its standing post with the slack holding (fingers silent; grass upright; Perrin's watch); the water
  turned at **12.51**. Penhale: "Things like that don't hear."
- **Crew:** Yeo (officer; green notebook); **Penhale** (officer's reserve; four Hunts, never on the mud); **Ivey**
  (catcher to the closing crew); the closing crew of four and their own officer (short grey man, unnamed); a
  boatman with two punts.
- **Seven:** watched the ebb two hours. 10.50: the upper stow's near post shed a yard on a surge; **Anwen laid a
  wall flat on the water** on Yeo's word (first live boom; Kiva: "Down a hand"); netted by Ivey; 9.1 drams in the
  net's tin, red; remark the creek's. 11.30 purses set with Penhale ("one fathom, two fathom, three"; his brother
  went to sea). Penhale asked what *nobody* was and laughed. 12.12 slack. Near post cut 12.14 on Kiva's call off
  Jude's heel; Kiva hauled, shut; **Pru called *far edge* once** as the sleeve's far end lifted; Kiva came off the
  line; the fold lay down where she had been. Far post cut 12.24; Pru hauled, shut; stow dead 12.29; upper tear
  closed by 12.40.
- **The Tarn:** near post 12.35 (red-haired blade from the mud; Bridie called and hauled with Penhale's count).
  The far post needed the punt. The second line caught; **Bridie asked Yeo whether the lying sleeve was ground;
  Yeo answered by the book**; Bridie walked out on it and freed the line. 12.44 the stow turned; the sleeve came
  over Bridie. The corner-hand (thin Tarn boy) had the far edge by the book and was hauling. Kiva shouted *far
  edge* from sixty paces; nobody on that half was listening for it. **Penhale went to the near edge by the book**,
  ward under, lifted, took Bridie's arm; **the sleeve's far end came round over his back.**
- **The pass in three (live, first time):** Pru: "If you cut the post, the skin folds… They're the nearest" (Yeo
  holds the cut). Perrin proposes the pass; Trewin: "I'll plant"; **Yeo: "It's mine. On my word. Do it."** Kiva's
  change from rehearsal: Pru's ward is not put under anything; it is set a pace off as **the nearest thing**, and the
  sleeve's far end folds onto it; Pru finishes the fold to a loaf-sized lump; the whole sleeve comes up off the two
  people after it. *Nobody.* Kiva's Ember (declared, single) carries it **six paces low** across the thread to the
  east mud; it begins to wrap her right fist; *nobody* from the air; **Trewin opens on the word**, plants it; it
  writhes once and lies still. Yeo: "Cut." The far post cut from the punt; the stow dies; the sleeve lies across the
  channel as ash. **It held. Penhale was dead under it already.** Under his grey ward, Bridie alive in an inch of air;
  his hand round her left wrist; he had been counting fathoms to keep her still and "stopped at three."
- Ivey opened his fingers and turned him; "He's gone." Carried up the east bank on the punt's floorboards
  (Ivey, Trewin, Jude, the boatman, Kiva left-handed); **Perrin lifted the head of the boards at the spur's lip
  against Aske's card.** Yeo laid her coat over him and closed his eyes.
- **Yeo's slate (read aloud):** closed at one, on the flood; *Lives: one. D. Penhale… went to the near edge by
  the book and was taken under the far end. The girl lived under his ward.* Hurt as §7. *The skin was taken off
  them, on the officer's word, by a pass in three… Called. Nobody holding it twice.* **Remarks: none, to any
  four. "The remark's the book's. And mine. I said it was ground."**
- Coombe (1.10): kneels with Yeo; to Kiva: "It held… Write it… And his. In the first column. Before."
- Bags: Seven's two dragged up the east bank before the flood (Jude and the boatman); the Tarn's first likewise;
  the Tarn's second (under the far post) unhauled, the creek's. Lower tear under the flood; to be closed near one in the
