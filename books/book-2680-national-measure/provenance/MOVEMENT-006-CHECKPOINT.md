## The one idea

A bucket with no bottom that holds water anyway, because the water is keeping the town's time. Carry it out of the
harbour full, without spilling, and empty it in the sink. The sink is the Graving. Spilled in the basin, its count goes
back into the bank's water and the bank cannot come out (the winter trial's late fall-backs); cut in the basin, a Barrel of
open stepped ash goes into the pumps' water. So: lift it whole, carry it in three passes, plant it in the dock, shut the
dock, cut it there.
