## 2. Character state at exit

## 5. The purse string (shown, not explained)

1. A string of four on end, skinned to the knee, with a white patch three paces by two lying
   flat behind its heels. It slides when the string walks. It is hard and cold under a boot at
   its near end and does not answer a boot.
2. The book calls a lying hem ground. It is the same stuff as the skin (Pike). Its ash reads
   ward, full, and stepped "to the top of the reed."
3. The string does not go to the lit end of the gate after a cut; it stands at the head of
   the cloth.
4. A rung lying on it does not lean; the heap sinks "straight down."
5. At about the twelfth count it lifts from its far edge, by both far corners at once, comes
   over whatever lies on it, is laid on its near edge, and draws back to the string's feet.
   It lets go of everything that is not ash. The knot above takes the ash in and is larger.
6. Dead weight along the far edge (sacks of coal, a hundredweight each) frosts, creaks, and
   holds; sacks split and must be renewed. With the far edge held it stirs and does not fold.
   Stepping on its far edge was not tried by a person.
7. When the last knot is cut it thins to a film of ash.
8. Nobody says why. Ruddock names it; the crew keeps the name.

## 6. The two fingers

Proof of Saturday the 4th of the twelfth month, on a foot-square iron surface plate in a vice,
the two fingers only, Mara's thumb behind a screen, Anwen's tally, Pru on Kiva's pulse. Twenty
tries: counted, seven (six called); steady, seven (four called; two called counted, one called
nothing); blank, six (three called; three called counted). Of five wrong calls of *counted,*
four were at a pulse over ninety. What count: asked seven times, right none. **Finding: through
something solid, at a light touch, they can say that a thing keeps time; never what time; and
they read her own pulse if she leans or is frightened.** "A needle with no scale on it." "A
reason to look." Not healing; not a sense. On the wharf they spoke, she could not tell them
from her own heart, and she did not act on them.

## 9. Departures from the packet and `BOOK_MAP.md`, and items for attention

1. **The book after the warrant is 9.64, not 10.53,** since two practice floors (0.89) were
   entered. Uncommitted is 3.84, not 4.73. The warrant was paid, not only posted.
2. **Jude Talley goes on the team book.** The plan did not ask for it. It follows from the
   entry needing a Kill who is not Kellan.
3. **The Chair's conditions for 2681 are written,** including the incident line re-entered.
   The contest was a yard contest and did not require them; the Chair promised them at the
   re-reading and posts them with the entry. Owner may prefer the line left open.
4. **The first notice's Hunt rule** ("a four holding a Flask license in its own right hunts on
   its own entry; a four without one hunts attached") and **the captain rule** are the
   author's, written to give Movement Two's licensed hunt and Perrin's captaincy a stake.
5. **Hart is the fitter with the watch** at the Saltmere harbour pump house, by his hand on
   the list. Mara and Kiva both see it. This plants Movement Three and does not meet him.
6. **Kiva asks Hart one question** ("What does yours tell you?"). It is about a hand. She
   decides on the page that no question about a fever may be asked, since any answer would no
   longer be independent of her; Mara agrees. **Nobody new learns that she dreams.** Orla has
   read the entry of the 6th in the book and the cover card's "one line is left out"; neither
   tells her anything.
7. **Mara now knows about Hart** (from Kiva, by Kiva's choice). The page does not say what
   Anwen and Pru were told about why the finger proof was run.
8. **One crossing only on the page.** Aske's card makes one in seven days a ceiling. The
   weekly go was not spent on the 14th, 21st or 28th.
9. **What Kiva did at the rule's edge:** she kept silent for one count, then said *Pike. Far
   edge.* before any bag was sealed. It gave Pike time to clear his corner-hands and time to
