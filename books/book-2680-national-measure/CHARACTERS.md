## Places and bodies new to Book Two (planned names)

- **The Ropewalk School**, Saltmere: the host circuit's leading school. Teaches two doors
  **laid** together. Its circuit has a column for it.
- **The Graving:** Saltmere's national floor, a dry dock turned arena, with a gate that keeps
  the harbour out.
- **The Longshore:** the tidal flats where the Hunt is held.
- **The Saltmere Company** (green; founded before 2362) and **Graywater** (slate blue) are
  Book One's. **The Charter Board** is Book One's.

## Viewpoint

About 87% Kiva. Five established lenses share about 13%: **Anwen, Kellan, Mara, Orla, Rhea.**
Each cutaway shows an action Kiva cannot see. Perrin, who had a lens in Book One, is seen
through Kiva in Book Two; his board is hers to watch.

## Development budget

The lead has a steady realization or decision about every 2,200 words. Small, independent
beats are spread across **Anwen, Perrin, Kellan, Pru, Jude, Noa, Orla, Mara, Rhea, and Pike,**
with Dray, Hart, and Carrow as the rivals and mirror who adapt. No single friend or mentor
takes the secondary story.

## Voices to keep

Anwen owns "I want it said" and "What's behind it?" Perrin minds at length and is funny about
it. Kellan does sums aloud and says "I'd expect nothing else." Pru says a big thing and goes
scarlet. Jude scratches his jaw and asks what ash is for. Mara is dry and exact. Orla is rough
and brief. Rhea allows herself one piece of advice a season. Pike nearly smiles. Dray's face
is a shut door. Aske counts so that others need not.
