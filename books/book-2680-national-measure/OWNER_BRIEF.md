## Book shape

- Six open-range movements of eight chapters each, chapters 1–48, approximately
  285,000–315,000 words unless the scenes earn more.
- Approximately 87% Kiva viewpoint/focus, with the remaining purposeful cutaways spread
  across four or five established or newly necessary characters.
- Begin with action, work, or a consequential choice rather than a recap lecture. Reorient a
  returning reader through what the cast does.
- Movement One should use the winter preparation and Rhea's night-van arrangement to teach
  the professional-rift ceiling, test Kiva's damaged hand under new constraints, and put the
  National Measure in immediate practical view. Its eight chapters must form one continuous
  creative run with substantial scenes, not eight summaries.
- Keep the familiar cast central while adding only the Saltmere competitors, officials,
  professionals, industry figures, and Glory witnesses the book actually needs. New names
  must avoid existing series collisions.
