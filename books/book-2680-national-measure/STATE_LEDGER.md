## 1. Calendar

Every month has thirty days and twelve months make the year (Aske's "eleven weeks and four
days" from Monday the 10th of the tenth month to the new year confirms that no day stands
between the twelfth month and the first). A month therefore begins two weekdays after the
one before it.

| Month | 1st falls on | 30th falls on | Fixed points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tenth, 2680 | Saturday | Sunday | Book One ends Sunday the 16th. First free day of Book Two: **Monday the 17th.** |
| Eleventh, 2680 | Monday | Tuesday | **The 1st:** Rhea Sorn's card goes back to her, signed or torn. Mara's hand is on a card "until the eleventh month." |
| Twelfth, 2680 | Wednesday | Thursday | Nothing fixed. |
| First, 2681 | **Friday** | Saturday | **The 1st:** Aske's ban on anything lit ends, if he signs the card up. The night van offer begins. Perrin joins his Hall's second team. |

| Third, 2681 | **Tuesday** | **Wednesday** | **The National Measure, at Saltmere.** Selka Dray "on her feet by the third month." Rhea Sorn's card ends on the 30th. The 11th (a Friday) is seventy-seven years to the day after the Ferren keeper's fever entry of 2604. |
| Fourth, 2681 | Thursday | Friday | Nothing fixed. |

Earlier anchors that prove the chain: the fifth month's 29th was a Wednesday (the Pit); the
sixth began on a Friday; the seventh on a Sunday; the ninth on a Thursday (the Regional, Friday
the 16th and Saturday the 17th); the tenth on a Saturday.

## 2. Kiva Rowan

- **Age and household.** Seventeen. Lives over Rowan Instruments with her aunt and guardian,
  Mara Rowan. A guardian's hand is still wanted beside her own wherever a rule says "under
  eighteen." No birthday is fixed in the supplied record; Book Two does not move her age
  unless the Book One manuscript gives a date.
- **Place.** *Rowan, K., Third Division, the Crooked Line: seated. No longer provisional.
  Catcher.* (the Chair, the 14th of the tenth month). Crooked Line third team; Team Seven.
  "Her sponsor's line and every condition on her certificate stand as written. She is not
  thereby classified." There is still no column for her; there is a page headed *Witnessed*
  with two lines on it.
- **The Home Line.** *Rowan, K. Entered on the Line's roll as catcher, autumn 2680. Hand as
  before.* Not a seat. No wage but a line-hand's day rate when called. Called by a Line
  foreman, with her sponsor's leave while she has a sponsor, "to set a mark, to read ground,
  to call a count that is hers and name the ones that are not." She has not yet been called.
- **Certificate, as it stands.**
  - Orla Dane's line: recovery only, dark hands; **and one crossing, Ember to still, low, to a
    mark, declared to a count, on a sanctioned mast read by the Chair's reader, one in seven
    days.** Not certified: any stack, any third door, anything Orla has not seen six times.
  - The Chair's conditions of the spring: one door declared to the referee before every
    sanctioned event, and any change declared; her book to her sponsor weekly; no live-cell
    events until her sponsor certifies in writing; one sanctioned-event incident ends her
    season. (The 2680 season is read. Whether that last line is re-issued for 2681 is the
    Chair's to say and is not on any page.)
  - The Chair's conditions of the 2nd of the seventh month: no second door on any Circuit
    floor except a crossing declared to a count and read at the time from a sanctioned mast
    by the office's reader; a stack so read is an incident; not at the Bench; recovery only on
    any floor with a contained rift. No crossing counts unless the tape shows the cliff. Noa
    Bexley is reader to the office.
  - **Selka Dray's mast rule** (any candidate with more than one door is read from a
    sanctioned mast by a reader of the Meridian office) has gone to Saltmere under seal.
- **Crossings on the record.** Ember to still: one under a field direction (the 29th of the
  fifth month), then Orla's six on sanctioned masts, and one begun and not landed in a lane
  at the Regional. Hand to Ember: landed once (the eighth month, 0.30). A crossing costs
  about 0.30 to 0.31 of a dram, of which about 0.26 is the gap. An unlanded crossing still
  spends the hand and the week's go. A stack is a miss, forbidden, and an incident if read.
- **Three doors.** Order: hand, Ember, still ("catch, carry, plant"); 0.59 whole. **Three
  clean in all: a yard (the 29th of the eighth month), the Pit (the 17th of the ninth), the
  Old Yard (the 5th of the tenth).** Uncertified, unclassified, and by the Chair's written
  entry "not to be asked of her." The last two were done under a recorded Home Line direction
  and an officer's hand, with the price said first by her healer; the last was offered and not
  asked. Never twice inside a fortnight. Aske does not know that the arm would come back a
  fourth time.
- **Readings.** Nothing has been measured since Classification Day, 2680: Draw 3.1; Hold,
  Edge 3.0, Ward 2.6, Stance 6.2; rank unsettled (the frame flickers); first door, none; the
  Call column dark. **No rank, Hold, Draw or bench figure has been re-measured.**
- **Body.**
  - **The first two fingers of the left hand have permanently lost fine sensation.** They
    bend, grip, and report pressure ("something's pushing"). They do not tell edge from flat,
    hot from cold, or one thread from two. Ward-trace ash and rest are ruled out as remedies.
  - The left arm lost feeling to a hand above the elbow on the 5th of the tenth month and came
    back over four days "to where it was, and not a hair less."
  - **Aske's card of the 10th: nothing lit until the new year. "Not a step. Not a ward for a
    tap." Dark hands and her voice.** His standing rule before that: one crossing in seven
    days, with the hand seen the next morning; the three-door is not on his card at all.
  - The left knee is still strapped and complained once, running. The three heat-lines run
    from the breastbone over the collarbone toward the left shoulder and upper arm.
- **Handoffs she names** (the list of the 30th of the ninth month): the cord, all three turns
  (Pru Finch); the slack of a hem on her left side (she takes a right-hand corner or is called
  to); tin lids with a lip; the pin of a seal and whether wax has gone off (Anwen has the
  wax); a button and a cuff; **the count of a thing that keeps time (somebody else's, named
  before).**
- **The disk.** Cold. It warmed once, on the 29th of the fifth month, after her first correct
  deliberate crossing, and has been cold through every crossing and all three three-door
  sequences since. Recognized by the Compact Library's Reader as the plate of nine rings
  carried by the first teachers and *released* by appointment. Held of the Line; borne by K.
  Rowan; not equipment, an instrument, or evidence; no hand but the bearer's without a vote of
  the watch; the bearer writes in the fourth column within the week after any crossing. What
  it is for is unknown. The first bearer's book is lost.
- **The dream.** No recurrence since Classification Day, 2680. No fever on any night of the
  autumn. She has Annick's phrase *the sky unroofed* and the Ferren keeper's line of 2604.
  **The name "the Unroofed Sky" has never appeared on the page.**
- **Her book.** Green cover, soft at the corners; a spring, a summer, and an autumn; no page
  ever torn out. It goes to Orla weekly and lies on the kitchen table nightly for Mara.

  tells him afterward what he did that he did not say aloud. She is not to open her mouth

- The card is to go back to Rhea on **Monday the 1st of the eleventh month.**
- Rhea's slate has six columns: *Before. Closed. Lives. Works. Tinned. Remarks.*

## 6. The grid and the rifts

- **The Ferren bank** (twenty-four cells, the Municipal Works') **is out of step.** The method:
  nothing in the water that keeps time; pumps stopped; every cell quenched on one word; fresh
  cans, each salted with a pinch of plain ash that "has never heard a beat"; a card with its
  own count under each handle, "not two alike in this room"; a reader between each cell and
  the next; each pump restarted on its own eight cells. It needs the main to be **still.**
- **Seven drums remain in step** on the Brickfields trunk: **19, 20, 21, 25, 26, 27, 28,** each
  alone on an open link, to be done one a day with the wardens' old winter stock. None has been
  done on the page.
- **Drum twenty-nine is fouled** (about thirty drams through a split plank). Its four streets
  are on boxes for about a month from the 6th of the tenth month.
- The Stopgate sluice works. The Crooked Line School's core house never fell in.
- **Saltmere's harbour bank, thirty-six cells, went into step at twenty past two on the 17th of
  the ninth month and has not come out.** Its pumps have not dropped a stroke. Nobody at
  Saltmere knows how to take a bank out.
- **Nobody has said** that water can step a cell, why a tear throws stepped ash, why husks
  stood a hold, or why the bank fell in on the 17th.
- The Home Line's suspension of held tears has not been lifted on the page.
- A can of civic stock in the Works' own store was cut with soot. Noticed; nobody has taken
  it up.
- **Evidence.** The notch on Kiva's pen falls eleven seconds after her second cliff (three
  occasions). The lamps' T-0 falls when a hold comes down. They are two things. T-0 entries
  on record: 2446, 2604, 2676, and three in 2680 (the Pit twice, Stopgate once). Noa's three
  columns (people; rifts and spawn; Saltmere) all rise, faster since the fifth month. One
  unvouched line says an overseas port's bank fell in at the same hour.

## 11. Reconciled for Book Two

1. **Hart's hand.** Orla says in chapter 8 that he "lost a hand" and left "with one hand."
   His card says "the hand, the bad one." Both stand: Orla never saw him afterward. He has one
   working hand and one ruined one that is still on his arm.
2. **Who dreams.** The series bible says that "a small number of high-rank practitioners"
   record the fragments. Book One's second account is a pump keeper's. The accepted book
   governs: the fever reaches ordinary people who sit beside banks and lamps; high-rank
   accounts are fuller. Book Two's plan builds on both.
3. **The notch.** The Movement Three block's "about forty seconds" was the keeper's guess and
   is superseded: eleven seconds.
4. **Kellan's "five heads."** Left open by Book One and settled inside Book Two (see
   `BOOK_MAP.md`).
5. **The seven drums** are 19, 20, 21, 25, 26, 27, and 28. Twenty-nine is fouled, not stepped.
