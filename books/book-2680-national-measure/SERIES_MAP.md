## Series promise (unchanged)

A generation born into mature door schools finds that its institutions kept the founders'
methods and simplified away the reasons. A three-door catcher has to rebuild connection
between divided traditions before a failing filter makes that division fatal. The ladder runs
from collegiate competition into the professional rift companies that defend and power the
rebuilt world.

## Repeating pleasures (unchanged, with Book Two's emphasis)

- Visible practice, measurable correction, rematches, standings, recruitment, and earned
  change of place.
- Schools that solve one obstacle in ways that look incompatible. **Book Two adds a school
  that teaches the forbidden thing well.**
- The earned-ash loop: train, fight, kill, contain, recover, grade, divide, invest. **Book Two
  turns it over once: the scarce ash is the plain kind.**
- Rift set pieces where teamwork and ground matter more than output.
- Records that change a present choice.
- Friends, rivals, and mentors with stakes of their own.
- The Unroofed Sky, as fallible human testimony and never as instruction.
- *Why the post is crooked.* **Book Two's companion question: what is being measured, and
  who keeps the count?**

## NEXT — Book Two, *The National Measure*

**Scale:** national. Saltmere, six circuits, twenty-four fours, licensed hunts, draft boards,
an industrial Glory house, and a school order, with the Charter Board over all of them.

**Promise.** Kiva earns a place among the country's best on a method other people can use.
The competition is read to its end. A scheduled national operation takes a harbour bank out
of step on tidal water, and the country chooses a slower, uneven grid.

**Irreversible changes by its end:**

1. The Unroofed Sky is named in public, attested by a sealed page, four records, and two
   living Glory masters, who read it in opposite ways.
2. The Step is understood as the mark that rising pressure leaves in ash, and as the thing
   that tears come to. Human and spawn escalation are shown to be answers to one pressure.
   The filter trend is established as a trend. Its cause is not.
3. The Halden foundation gives up its claim on the Step. Municipal banks go onto their own
   counts by order.
4. A mixed cohort exists: students of four circuits and three companies who have done one
   hard thing together by a written method. It is the seed of a national team.
5. Kiva holds two certified crossings and a slower card: one in fourteen days. She is on the
   national license roll with her four, and under a Charter Board commission to carry the
   method.
6. Team Seven's four choose four different futures.
7. Ports over the water saw the same light in the same minute, and somebody has sailed
   toward it.

## HORIZON — Book Three (not planned in detail)

**Scale:** international. National teams and companies, cross-border grids, rival doctrines,
Glory masters, apex rifts.

Fixed by the owner: the planetary filter is completely lost; pure Glory presence becomes the
world's permanent condition; the survivors begin a shared next-ladder method, with no single
ruler. The dream does not fix who gathers, who cooperates, what survives, or who leads.

Book Two hands Book Three these live questions and answers none of them:

- Was the Proprietor right that stepped lamps go white and plain ones go out? The country has
  just bet against him.
- Who was on the hull, and what did they see in their own fever?
- Can a handed crossing be called across languages and doctrines that share no count?
- What does a card of one crossing in fourteen days allow, when the pressure doubles?
- Three doors, three clean, a fourth unspent.
- The plate: cold beside two Glory masters. The first book: still lost.
- Hesper Annick walked out in 2461 and the Order wrote her down as dead.

## Deep mystery budget

Book One identified the plate's bearer line. **Book Two may establish the filter trend, name
the Step as its mark, and make the dream a public, shared, and disputed fact.** It may not
remove the filter, deliver Glory presence, explain the Fall, give the disk an origin or use,
identify the Makers, or crown one doctrine. Book Three may cross the threshold while leaving
ultimate cosmology open.

## Series checks at this turn

- **Power inflation.** Kiva ends Book Two with no new door, no fourth sequence, and a slower
  card. Her gain is a second certified crossing and a method that makes other people stronger.
- **Repeated plot.** Book One struck its tournament and then met a surprise at home. Book Two
  reads its tournament to the end and then runs a *scheduled* operation, in daylight, that
  everybody has argued about for three weeks.
- **Cross-series consequence.** The first trilogy's records still govern. Nothing in this
  plan needs a founder to have been wrong.
