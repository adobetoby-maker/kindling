## MOVEMENT 5 CLOSE — Chapters 19–23, drafted. BOOK 1's COSTLIEST LOSS.

**Milo Faust is dead.** Killed by a rifle shot at ~110 feet, across the river, from
a Cinder Company member never seen or named to Toren, a little before 4:00am on
the south apron of the old weir (Ch. 21–22). He was thirteen years and six months
old. Buried at noon the next day in the field above the Greenmill mill ruin;
Toren cuts MILO FAUST into the sluice-house lintel by hand that afternoon — the
first named, permanent grave marker in the book (contrast: three unmarked graves
at Greenmill, three unmarked at the Movement 1 roadside). Party is now four
(Toren, Dessa, Wyck) plus Rook and Senna.

**Why Milo, not Wyck (the original working assumption) or Dessa:** Wyck's
resentment-to-loyalty arc had already fully resolved through non-lethal means by
Ch. 18 — killing him now would have spent an arc already paid off rather than
complete one still owed. Milo's death is caused by his own specific growth (the
unattended-light skill learned Ch. 13 is what draws Cinder Company to ground Rook
chose), pays off the bread-ritual plant running since Ch. 4 (he never learns what
it means — his death lets that plant land on Rook, Toren, and Milo simultaneously,
which only works if Milo is the one who dies), and costs the book its lightness
rather than a logistical hole (Dessa's death would have stranded Senna and cut a
still-owed Movement 6 beat). Full reasoning in the drafting agent's report,
verified sound on review.

**Rook's real name — Elias Marsh — lands, in two stages:**
1. **Taken, not given:** Anse Whitlow (Cinder Company, 61, was on the ridge at
   Homura, read Rook's name off a subject list nine years ago) says "Marsh," then
   "Elias Marsh," on the walkway at the weir (Ch. 23) — explicitly not as
   leverage; he's carried it nine years, never told his own captain, gives it
   because "it's the only thing I've got that's worth anything."
2. **Given, freely:** hours later at the fire, Rook says it himself to Dessa,
   Wyck, and Senna — "My name's Elias Marsh" — the moment he realizes Toren told
   them the full Homura account without being asked to. Senna: "That's a name.
   The other one isn't." / Rook: "No. It isn't." / Senna: "You can have both.
   Most people only get the one."

**The turn:** Rook is offered the whole of Cinder Company inside nine days —
five survivors, no captain, pinned to a four-day contract in a quarry cut four
miles up the valley from Greenmill, then five more days to Undern before the
company dissolves at the frost. He has wanted this for five years. He refuses it
out loud, to the group, with the reason stated plainly: going means leaving three
children, a lame boy, and a dying woman alone for a week on an apron four miles
from an open tear. "I've stood in that exact place once already. I'm not doing it
twice." Who ordered the original Homura hit stays explicitly, permanently
unknown — Rook refuses to extract a name from Whitlow even though he could:
"a frightened man hands you a name to keep his teeth... I'm not going to [know].
That's not me being noble. That's me knowing exactly what I'm like."

**Combat/canon locked this movement:** First confirmation that firearms exist in
this world and are a real, undefended threat — "there's no guard, there's no
light" against a rifle. The diamond formation (the party's core drilled tactic
since Movement 1) is explicitly named as *wrong* against human opponents — Rook
teaches a four-part human-vs-Riftspawn distinction in Ch. 20 specifically because
the diamond's central-protection logic is what put Milo in the open when it
mattered. Cinder Company is confirmed not to be monstrous — "men doing a job,"
Hale Orsk (the detachment's Torch-rank captain, killed by Rook at the walkway)
explicitly comes to talk first and gives Rook a clean way out, which Rook refuses
on principle, not because Orsk was unreasonable.

**Physical/progression:** Toren crosses to **Flame** — confirmed by Rook the
same morning ("You had two feet of it... the whole way through... That's
Flame"), held two feet of Edge continuously through the entire post-death
Riftspawn engagement without losing it once, moving and shouting throughout. It

**Knowledge:** Toren tells Wyck and Dessa the complete, unredacted Homura account

**New named entities:** Greenmill (mill hamlet on the Reave — first living
