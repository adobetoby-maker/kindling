Toren's specific disk is "his Satori" or "the Satori." ## Retired

- **"a Brand"** — earlier proposed term for power-classification, deliberately avoiding
  "Path" to prevent cross-series collision. Retired: the owner explicitly chose to
  keep "the Path" as a deliberate echo rather than avoid it, so the collision concern
  that motivated "Brand" no longer applies. "Brand" may still be useful vocabulary for
  something else later, but is not currently load-bearing.
