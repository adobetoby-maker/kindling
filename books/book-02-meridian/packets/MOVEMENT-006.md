- Intended movement scale: approximately **36,000–42,000 words**. Book Two is

Open at the cut on the west side of the collapsed wash crown, late D+49 or first light
D+50. Do not skip the crossing already priced and planned. The cart, horse, two live
wheel boxes, people, ash, and injuries must cross a road that no longer exists.

Read Chapters 33–40 together before writing. Treat
`provenance/MOVEMENT-005-CONTINUITY.md` as the binding entry ledger. Carry:

- **Party:** Pell, Dee, Rook, Jab, Callie and Toren; Duchess and the damaged cart.
- **Bodies:** Pell's broken right ankle; Rook's infected/reopened back and one fever
  paper left; Callie's two newly cracked left ribs and old bruising; Jab's white palm
  mark and blister; Dee a finger over her floor after reaching it once; Toren's left
  forearm still cuffed and his personal reserve recovering.
- **Cargo:** two live yellow wheel boxes in the lead carrier; the carrier on the cart;
  three untouched home Flasks (24); fence ash 13; lifter ash 20½ clean and 4 dirty;
  five vent jars left behind (three under the room-one racks, two by the pump-house
  stone); counter, glass, ledger paper, tongs and contaminated gear as recorded.
- **Crossing price:** about 10–12 clean Handfuls from the lifter's recovery for an hour
  at approximately 80, all six people and Duchess, plus Toren's settled strut-steps.
  The cart crosses in pieces: wheels, axle, bed, shafts, carrier board, Pell on a canvas

- **Clock:** planned crossing D+50 if wind is down; five road days home after it; best

- **Breach:** D+48's deliberate three-Handful line crossing, `once`, is written for the
  Director with Toren's and Dee's names. The abort rule itself remains in force.
- **Rip:** the Caul Hill sky lost its ten-and-three count after the lifter died. Falls
  continue without a regular rhythm. Causality is unknown.
- **Home:** the reviews were held and their clocks stopped at the D+41 gate. Dessa is
  home command. Katori, Ines, Otto, Wyck, the unnamed weighbridge girl and yard crew
  hold the Sag. Tonk, Tilda and Senna must be met as bodies whose conditions continued
  while the party was gone, not as paused rewards.

## What this movement must accomplish

This is the return, consequence and book-resolution movement—not a second larger
climax. Deliver all of the following as connected story:

1. Cross the ruined wash with the planned cart-in-pieces method, finite ash and injured
   bodies. Prove the Caul Hill victory did not make the road easy.
2. Bring the party home through several days of consequence, compressed only where
   repetition is truly repetition. Let task-bound warmth, disagreement and care show
   what company they have become.
3. Stage a shorter **return hold** at the Sag as a delayed surge reaches Meridian. It
   may plausibly follow the distant Caul Hill disturbance, but nobody can prove the
   cause on the page. The home cohort and returning party solve it together.
4. Outside the primary climax, prove each protagonist's new capability:
   - Callie holds or routes a shared physical line for others without treating herself
     as the whole solution. Ward moves matter and bodies, never radiation.
   - Jab reads active burden, performs a bounded recall/rotation, stops before
     self-erasure, vents, and returns by choice. He is a healer and still a brother.
   - Toren uses Guard deliberately and leads through assignments and handoffs. He may
     use Guard and Edge in sequence only; below unbound, never simultaneously.
5. Install and test a recovered wheel box in the treatment machine. The machine may
   restore its calibration function; it does not cure Tilda, Tonk, Senna, Rook, Pell,
   Callie or any fixed damage.
6. Put the ash, injuries, line breach, expedition record, recruitment terms and held
   household reviews before Meridian. Demonstrated work creates leverage, but the
   accord is specific and incomplete rather than utopian.
7. Show concrete on-page medical states for Tonk, Tilda and Senna, plus the returning
   injured adults. Give each at least one action or limit, not a summary paragraph.
8. Leave the north-yard ledger and three practice lanes functioning as the first seed
   of the future schools. The three protagonists remain together for Book Three.
9. Let the company take a page-earned name. The author may choose the exact name from
   language already earned in this book; it must not sound like a modern brand or a
   pre-existing institution. Record it in the final checkpoint.
10. Land one disk/Satori clue and one off-page Cinder-remnant trace as horizon threads,
    not explanations or new plot engines.

## Recommended eight-chapter dramatic shape

This is sequencing guidance, not compulsory scene cards. Keep 6–8-chapter-run
creativity and alter chapter boundaries if continuity improves.

### Chapter 41 — Jab: the wash in pieces

- Settle, measure and cross at first light if the wind permits.
- Jab and Dee own the body/ash rotation; Pell directs despite the litter; Callie reads
  the banks; Toren builds only the strut-steps needed; Rook obeys `off`.
- Make the 10–12 Handful price legible. No home Flask is casually opened.
- A moment involving Little, Tonk's release, or Rook's fever should make `home` a
  person rather than a compass direction.

### Chapter 42 — Toren: what a road costs backward

- Reassemble the cart and leave the cut. Account for tools, wheel alignment, horse,
  contaminated cargo and everyone who cannot walk normally.
- Carry the machine's missed D+54 cycle as an active clock.
- Let Toren lead without doing every task himself. A correct handoff matters more than
  another display of force.

### Chapter 43 — Callie: five days and the count

- Develop enough of D+51–D+54 to make return distance felt: weather, food/water, wound
  care, cart trouble, ash arithmetic and one human disagreement or laugh.
- Let Callie read changing ground/ash behavior that points toward Meridian without
  announcing a new catastrophe too early.
- Her cracked ribs alter what she can carry and how long she can hold.

### Chapter 44 — Jab: the wrong movement in the Sag

- Near Meridian, Jab or the party detects the surge before it becomes a surprise cut.
- The surge should be a consequence/public-proof action, materially different and
  shorter than Caul Hill: a line and rotation problem, not another boss monster.
- Establish the home cohort's positions and what has changed during the absence.

### Chapter 45 — Toren: everybody back

- The returning party and home cohort jointly contain the surge.
- Toren assigns, listens and preserves the team. Callie holds others; Jab rotates;
  Dessa's command, Katori's field judgment, Wyck's body lane and the existing yard
  training all matter.
- Pay off the phrase `Everybody back` as return and tactical doctrine. Do not erase
  Rook/Pell's injuries to give them hero beats.

### Chapter 46 — Callie: the wheel in the machine

- Enter Meridian and make arrival physical. Do not stack all reunions into one queue.
- Let the unnamed technician and existing medical staff install/test one wheel box;
  the second remains reserve/evidence unless the mechanism credibly needs both.
- Show Tilda's current speech, right side and medicine response; Senna's transfer and
  cycle; the machine's repaired function. Keep every incurable fact true.
- Use the recovered box/plate/archive relation for one restrained Satori/disk clue:
  another concrete record, fit, serial, or phrase that deepens `released to bearer`
  without naming origin, Maker or final meaning.

### Chapter 47 — Jab: the terms he chooses

- Show Tonk's present infection/frame/mobility state and reunion through action.
- Put Jab's medical-register offer and weekly teaching promise back before him. He
  chooses service terms; he is not assigned as station property. Tonk's stability is
  not bargaining collateral.
- Stage the ash and breach accounting. The Director may censure, restrict, require a
  record, or impose recovery, but must distinguish an owned exception from a secretly
  abandoned rule.
- Resolve the held household reviews into a concrete, partial improvement. Do not fix
  Meridian's whole labor/residency system.

### Chapter 48 — Toren: the first page after

- Apportion clean, dirty, store, medical, road and training ash. Return the three home
  Flasks visibly untouched if they remain so.
- The company takes its earned name. Callie, Jab and Toren choose to remain together.
- Dessa's append-only ledger, Senna's reasons, and the three marked north-yard lanes
  remain in daily use: seeds, not a chartered academy.
- Confirm only a trace of Cinder's remnant off-page—changed badge, rumor, recovered
  mark, or report that Tull's people are gathering. Do not reveal its new name/shape
  or stage a confrontation.
- End on forward pressure and belonging, not a policy speech. Book Two must feel
  complete while Book Three has a horizon.

## The return hold

Keep this action shorter and simpler than Caul Hill. Its pleasure is recognition:
techniques laboriously learned now work in public and in combination.

- The surge may carry irregular ash/fog and lesser Riftspawn or a failing held line.
  It does not need a named new creature or a larger Yield.
- Dessa is home command and must not be displaced merely because Toren returned.
- Katori's blade, Ines's bow, Otto, Wyck, the weighbridge girl and the yard workers may
  perform already established roles. Do not invent a named continuing trainee.
- Callie may widen/route a Ward around multiple people or a transfer lane, priced and
  limited by ribs and reserve. This is the clearest place to prove `holds others`
  without rewriting M5 into retroactive Ward-at-scale.
- Jab must use Sustain with a watch and a rotation. He may draw active burden, not
  fixed wounds, and must vent. His stop is part of the victory.
- Toren's Guard must have a physical load and breath cost. Any Edge use requires a
  clear release/handoff and darkening before Guard returns.
- Adults contribute real work but do not steal the three-lead solution.
- No death is required. Cost may be reserve, contamination, delayed treatment,
  worsened existing injury or a bounded new wound.

## Patient and relationship obligations

### Tonk

- Infection was clear; the frame remains until spring; growth plate damage remains.
- On D+41 he completed most of a chair transfer in nine minutes and released Jab,
  keeping both ends of the cord. Do not reset him to helpless or make him walk.
- Give him agency in the reunion and in Jab's chosen terms.

### Tilda

- D+40 fit lasted 131 seconds; right side and language worsened; Callie's name returned
  that evening. D+41 she walked on her stick with the right leg lagging and partly
  closed the right hand on a cup. Second medicine was raised.
- The machine does not remove the growth or restore her fully. Show whether the raised
  medicine changed fits/swelling/function through observed action and Sowerby's
  bounded judgment.

### Senna

- She cannot take the transfer step; two people lift her; hands and feet are numb.
- The missed/poorly improvised cycle has a cost. The repaired machine restores a
  reliable course going forward, not lost nerve or marrow damage already fixed.
- She remains route author, recorder and institutional memory, not only a patient.

### Rook, Pell, Dee and the three leads

- Rook's infection and reduced margin require ordinary medicine and rest.
- Pell's ankle remains broken and requires recovery.
- Dee reached her floor once and does not become a limitless healer on returning.
- Callie's ribs, Jab's palm/blister and Toren's forearm/reserve remain in the room when
  decisions are made.
- Reunions should be interleaved with tasks and consequences, not performed as one
  sentimental receiving line.

## The accord and its limits

The expedition gives leverage, not sovereignty. A credible specific result may
combine:

- the held Callie/Tilda and Jab/Tonk household reviews receiving longer, stable terms;
- Jab affirming the already agreed once-weekly teaching on the other side of Dee's
  table while declining automatic on-call ownership;
- the three young leads and cohort receiving provisional defender/company standing
  with written consent, adult support, floors and review rather than informal coercion;
- an explicit ash allocation to treatment, road defense and training, with the lent
  store inventory accounted for publicly;
- the D+48 breach recorded and judged as an owned exception, not praised as a model.

Do not abolish contracts, erase every debt, make the Director irrationally surrender,
or solve Meridian's whole system. The book's win is a door that now exists and terms
the protagonists helped write.

## Withheld truths and hard limits

- Public byline is Monroe Jackson. O'Connor remains internal.
- No new continuing named character. The owner names continuing cast.
- Do not reveal Dee's exact door count, Rook's third door, Katori's withheld words,
  Satori's origin, Makers, Cinder's new name/shape, or the full meaning of the disk.
- One-configuration limit is binding below unbound. No Guard+Edge blend.
- Ward turns matter/force, not radiation. Sustain removes active burden, not fixed
  injury. Guard costs by breath and load. Ash is finite and graded.
- No infinite Yield, soul theft, time reversal, universal cure or surprise extra door.
- No romance is required or assumed.
- Do not kill a patient or adult merely to manufacture ending weight.
- Preserve the owner-selected three protagonists. Dessa, Wyck and the adults remain
  essential support, not replacement leads.
