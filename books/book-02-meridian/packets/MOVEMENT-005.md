## Where we enter — the next moments

Open on the morning of D+47 at the roofless pump house on the east bank of the river,
outside the Caul Hill fence. Do not jump past the decision, Rook's wound check, Pell's
ankle, the floor readings, or the first look at the footbridge/east gate in daylight.

Read Chapters 25–32 together before writing. Treat
`provenance/MOVEMENT-004-CONTINUITY.md` as the binding entry ledger. Carry every
body, jar, object, promise and clock. The crucial entry state is:

- **Party:** Pell, Dee, Rook, Jab, Callie and Toren; Duchess and the damaged cart.
- **Inside-capable team:** Dee, Jab, Callie and Toren. Rook is off until Dee releases
  him; Pell cannot walk and was always meant to remain outside. Do not heal either
  injury away to restore the old five-person plan.
- **Ash:** five clean fence Flasks = 40 Handfuls. The abort line stays 16, leaving
  only 24 to work with against the original 48. Three home Flasks = 24, untouched.
  They remain route-home reserve and may not silently become fight fuel.
- **Healers:** Dee and Jab only. Rook is not a covert third rotation. Dee's word is
  the Director's on the road.
- **Bodies:** Rook's reopened, contaminated back declares itself on D+47; Pell's
  right ankle is broken and splinted; Callie's ribs remain bruised and her own
  reserve begins recovered only if sleep earned it; Toren's left forearm is jarred
  twice, cuffed and unusable as a lever; Jab's palms were warm and his floor was a
  finger under a Handful at the cut.
- **Objects:** second counter with Callie; brass glass/book with Dee; Toren's signed
  paper, copied plate and stone; Little buttoned in Jab's left coat; empty lead
  carrier, five vent jars and three home Flasks on the cart; five fence Flasks in
  Jab's frame; one broken-glass socket; Pell's knife lost; Dee's water skin empty;
  Callie's hook contaminated and not yet shown washed.
- **Access:** pump house on east bank; three-plank footbridge with one rail; chained
  iron-framed wire gate on west side; fence runs north. Senna's remembered route
  points toward the yellow stair and the door that opens toward the entrant: pull it.
- **Known phenomenon:** with no wind, loose ash moves uphill in a ten-breaths-on,
  three-off pulse. The counter and sky edge share it. Jab believes something under
  the wrong sky is lifting ash the way Sustain lifts, but cannot feel a person or
  know what it is.
- **Clock:** machine cycle around D+54. With the east road cut, earliest return was
  already D+55 before entering. The mission is now late even if it succeeds.

The first decision is not “do we disobey and enter the rip?” The Director authorized
the plant and boxes but prohibited entering the rip or hunting beneath it. The page
must establish whether the east gate, plant and needed rooms can be reached without
crossing the rip itself. The primary creature may make the approved salvage route
inseparable from the rip's tactical problem; the party must not rebel for spectacle.

## What this movement is for

Deliver the book's primary Caul Hill climax. The movement must turn the separately
learned doors into one comprehensible sequence under live failure:

- **Callie owns access and current control.** Ward cannot block ground radiation.
  It can stop/redirect particulate ash and physical bodies, shape a clean lane, hold
  a door or falling structure, and isolate a segment of the creature's loose-ash
  current. Her Edge opens/severs only where the viewpoint can understand the line.
- **Jab owns survival and pulse recognition.** Sustain identifies active burden,
  performs bounded recall and manages rotation. He must discover how to interrupt or
  mis-time the creature's recall pulse without becoming an infinite sink. He may
  catch, divert or vent only what the system and finite ash can pay for. He does not
  heal fixed damage or command the creature by sympathy.
- **Toren owns moving defense and physical severance.** Guard is now named. He uses
  set/step, strut and limited Edge to create stable openings, protect movement and
  sever physical routes the creature uses. Static holds cost by breath; his own
  reserve and left arm remain hard limits.
- **Dee owns the clean protocol.** She watches floors, exposure, vent and abort;
  she may heal, but cannot solve the three-lead tactical problem.
- **Rook and Pell own the outside line.** Their contribution must matter through
  observation, route, cart, extraction, warning, or a hard choice, without magically
  putting them back inside the fight.

The creature dies only after Callie, Toren and Jab land their answers in sequence.
Its released ash may replenish the exhausted Sustain rotation only **after** the
decisive exchange. No pre-death ash windfall may solve the fight.

## Entry, search and the boxes

Let Caul Hill be a place rather than a boss arena. Use ordinary industrial remains,
contamination practice, Senna's twenty-year-old memory and the current distortion.
Make the route understandable: fence, gate, yard/buildings, yellow stair, door,
rooms, exposure gradients, exits and the location of the rip relative to the needed
reference boxes.

Before the primary fight, the team should:

- establish a new four-person ash/exposure budget from the actual 40/16/24 state;
- decide who crosses, who remains, how the footbridge and gate stay usable, and what
  triggers immediate return;
- wash or isolate contaminated gear and solve Dee's empty-water problem with a
  credible source or constraint—never a convenient unlimited clean supply;
- use `pull the door` as earned practical knowledge;
- locate the live yellow reference boxes with the wheel: two required, three if
  physically possible, carried only in the lead carrier back to the cart;
- count the sky in an observable way and record it;
- reveal that the boxes and the ash current cannot be separated tactically.

Do not let a long search make the climax feel delayed. Entry scenes must deepen dread,
clarify the plant and spend or threaten a finite resource. The boxes may be reached
before the creature is killed but not safely extracted, or the creature may block
the route before they reach them. The author chooses the cleanest causal version.

## The Sustain-echo creature

This is a new Riftspawn expression, not a borrowed monster or a vague cloud.

Hard mechanic:

- it recalls **loose ash already present**—from old contamination, a leaking rip,
  damaged or killed spawn, and any jar spilled into the environment;
- it uses that recalled ash to restore damaged physical routes in itself and to
  reconstitute nearby lesser spawn after ordinary killing;
- it does not create ash, reach into a sealed jar without breach, steal settled ash
  directly from a soul, or retroactively heal fixed damage;
- its renewal has the observed ten-and-three rhythm. That rhythm is exploitable but
  not automatically easy: ten is the draw, three is its vent/recovery or comparable
  vulnerable transition;
- once the current is isolated and the pulse interrupted, ordinary damage can stay
  done long enough for the team to finish it.

The exact anatomy, movement and setting integration belong to the author. Privately
establish its starting location, reach, objective, ash routes, lesser-spawn inventory,
and physical links before drafting the fight. Give the reader an image they can
follow and a limitation they can anticipate.

Discovery should come through failed tactics:

1. a damaged or killed lesser spawn returns because loose ash flows back;
2. ordinary attrition visibly feeds the system rather than wearing it down;
3. the team recognizes the ten-and-three pulse and tests a wrong answer;
4. Callie learns what current she can isolate, Toren what physical route he can hold
   or cut, and Jab what moment he can interrupt without taking the whole burden;
5. the final coordinated sequence lands at real bodily and ash cost.

Do not repeat the wash fight's geometry. Caul Hill should pose a new problem: an
enemy for which successful hits are temporary until the renewal engine is broken.

## Ash, radiation and injury accounting

The count is part of the suspense. State the opening four-person budget, record each
opened Flask and major spend, and make the 16-Handful abort line meaningful. If the
party crosses the line, it must be a conscious emergency choice with consequences,
not an arithmetic accident.

- Five clean Flasks enter the decision. Three home Flasks remain outside and
  untouched unless survival on the road home truly requires them after the climax.
- Dirty/loose Caul Hill ash cannot be treated as clean jar ash without collection,
  separation and credible handling.
- Rook is off; only Dee and Jab rotate. If one is lost, the exposure window changes
  immediately.
- Jab must vent; Callie and Toren must be drawn or measured; Dee must also remain a
  body with a floor.
- The creature's death releases a large Yield, but collecting it costs time,
  containment and exposure. Preserve some ash for the road while accepting that the
  haul is not pure profit.

At least one new injury must be carried into Movement Six, and existing injuries must
remain. No injury is erased by recall. The party may win and still leave with less
capacity than it expected.

## Human pressure

- **Rook:** being left outside while the younger team enters is the consequence of
  his earlier overreach and the proof of trust. He may advise, warn or hold an exit,
  but Dee's `off` stands.
- **Pell:** a broken ankle does not remove judgment. Make his route/cart/part-handling
  knowledge decisive from outside.
- **Dee:** she has to trust Jab's felt sense and the three younger leads' decisions,
  even while she owns the abort line. Her protocol and Rook's relational method have
  become one practice.
- **Callie:** access is not the same as carrying everyone. Her success should require
  choosing a boundary and trusting others beyond it.
- **Jab:** interrupting the renewal pulse must test his fear of vanishing into a
  patient/problem. Little and Tonk's terms keep his return obligation present without
  making the fish magical.
- **Toren:** `Everybody back` may conflict with finishing the mission. Leadership is
  not denying danger; it is deciding whose risk is theirs to choose and preserving a
  viable return.

Use humor and warmth in task-bound moments; do not place banter over injury or turn
the climax into solemn instructions only.

## Where we leave pressure

End with the primary creature dead and its restoration stopped. The party has:

- two live yellow reference boxes with the wheel, three only if the carrying and
  lead-shield logic genuinely supports it;
- a large but costly ash recovery, counted and graded rather than called unlimited;
- proof that the rip is changing, recorded through the ten-and-three phenomenon and
  the creature's Sustain-like mechanism;
- injuries, dirty equipment and reduced margins that carry forward;
- Rook and Pell still injured;
- the road east still cut and the D+54 machine cycle already in danger.

They turn for home by a specific route decision. Do not resolve the return, machine
repair, residency accord, patient outcomes or north-yard school in this movement.
The final feeling is victory that creates a way home but does not make the journey
cheap: the team now possesses what Meridian needs, and has to get it back in time.
