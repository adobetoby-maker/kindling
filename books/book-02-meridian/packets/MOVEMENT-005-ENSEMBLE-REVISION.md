## Owner direction

Book Two currently continues to read as three prose strands after Callie, Jab and
Toren have physically become one working company. The owner wants a sustained
portion in which they are together and the narrator carries the company rather
than assigning every chapter to one of the three protagonists.

Rewrite Movement Five as that unified portion. The current chapters are the
binding story draft: preserve their events, dialogue worth keeping, physical
geography, injuries, ash accounting, power rules, discoveries, emotional costs,
fight causality and exit state. This is a camera-and-prose revision, not permission
to replace Caul Hill with a new plot.

## Explicit formula exception

The owner's ensemble instruction overrides O'Connor's normal single-lead POV
allocation for these eight chapters. Do not try to hide the old rotation under
neutral headings. The movement should genuinely feel as though the three stories
have joined.

This exception does not authorize loose omniscient head-hopping. Use an intentional
**company camera**:

- The primary narrator can follow the visible work of Callie, Jab, Toren, Dee,
  Rook and Pell as one causal unit.
- During shared action, privilege observable behavior, dialogue, spatial
  relationships, changing danger and what the company can collectively know.
- Private interiority may occur only after a clean paragraph or section settle
  into one person. Make the transition legible, remain there long enough for the
  beat to matter, and return cleanly to the company camera.
- Never move through multiple private thoughts inside one paragraph.
- Do not use an essaying or historical omniscient voice that explains the world
  from above. The narrator stays present with the bodies at Caul Hill.
- Preserve the accessible, read-aloud Monroe cadence: clear subjects and actions,
  purposeful punctuation, short paragraphs, and legible action geography.

## What must improve

1. **One continuous mission.** The reader should no longer feel the camera restart
   every time another protagonist becomes important. Decisions and consequences
   pass directly among Callie, Jab and Toren.
2. **Three indispensable competencies.** Callie owns access/current control,
   Jab owns survival/pulse recognition, and Toren owns moving defense/physical
   severance. The prose should make their work interdependent rather than serial.
3. **Adults inside the same field.** Dee, Rook and Pell remain active people with
   authority, injury and judgment. They must not become scenery when the camera
   widens, and they must not steal the three-lead solution.
4. **Legible fight geometry.** The team fight must remain a developed set piece.
   Track the gate, yard, yellow stair, door, rooms, rip, creature, boxes, exits,
   carried ash and injured bodies. Widening the camera should improve clarity.
5. **A real collective identity.** Task-bound humor, fear, disagreement, handoffs,
   watching one another's limits and accepting another person's risk should make
   the company real before it receives a formal name later.
6. **Audio clarity.** Remove ambiguous pronoun chains, unclear speaker changes,
   comma splices and sentences whose meaning only resolves at the end. Preserve
   purposeful long sentences when their hierarchy is audible.

## Revision method and deliverables

Work through all eight chapters as one connected author session. Do not perform
eight independent line edits. First understand the full movement; then rewrite in
reading order so entrances, handoffs and consequences carry across chapter seams.

Write only to the staging paths under
`revisions/ensemble-2026-09-29/manuscript/`. Do not overwrite canonical manuscript
files in this author run. Also write:

- `revisions/ensemble-2026-09-29/CONTINUITY.md` — day/scene map, camera settles,
  ash/injury/object ledger, preserved facts, changed joins and exact Chapter 41
  exit state;
- `revisions/ensemble-2026-09-29/AUTHOR-REPORT.md` — actual model, files read,
  chapter word counts, material departures from the source, and anything needing
  editorial judgment.

After drafting, read the eight revised chapters together once and repair only
blocking contradiction, unclear camera transitions, broken audio syntax or a lost
source payoff. Stop for the orchestrated cold read and continuity review. Do not
promote the staging files to canon, commit, push or alter unrelated files.
