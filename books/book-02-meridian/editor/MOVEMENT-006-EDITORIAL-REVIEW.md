- **Reader type:** a **same-model** editorial read in a fresh session. It is **not** independent testing and **not** a human read.
  - The reviewer is Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`). That is the same model that drafted Movement Six (per `provenance/MOVEMENT-006-CONTINUITY.md`).
  - This session did not have the drafting transcript. However, the session's startup context injected **memory summaries of the drafting run**: one-line titles such as "Chapter 43 continuity correction: 'First one full' attribution to Dee" and "Chapter 46 rack row correction". So I knew in outline where the author had already made fixes. The read is fresh-context in practice, but it is not blind.
  - Shared model habits are the main blind spot. A same-model reader tends to find the author's rhythms natural, and it can miss the same things the author missed. Treat the reader scores as weaker evidence than the counts.

## 1. Reader response (unscored), then two lenses

**Where interest caught.**
- The movement opens on a problem you can see: a four-pace gap, a post lying across a stream, a cart that has to cross in pieces.
- The second wheel's bounce into the drift (ch 41) is the best small crisis in the book. It is trackable, it has a price, and it ends on Toren asking "*Who goes in?*" instead of going.
- The meal sack, the singing wheel and the rising culvert counts give the road home three separate clocks.
- The Sag seen from the north lip (ch 44) is a genuine reveal: the fog is grey where it should be white, and the home line is working with Toren's call.

**Where it slipped.**
- The rain half-day in ch 43 idles until Callie's night request pays it off.
- Ch 47's opening ash recital covers ground the reader has heard at every halt.
- The last three chapters are mostly aftermath. That is the right shape for a book's close, but chs 46–48 lean on the same pause vocabulary ("for a long time", "did not say anything").

**Who matters most.**
- Jab, by the end of ch 47. His *off* at the table, then his supper term, carry the book's thesis in one boy.
- Callie's "Because I can't count it from here" is the movement's best single line.

**What I expect next.**
- Rook goes to the south huts with the brass.
- Somebody goes back to Caul Hill in the spring.
- Toren's disk meets the socket.

**Would I continue?** Yes, to Book Three, on the brass and the disk.

**Fluent thirteen-year-old (a lens, not research).**
- Will love: the cart in pieces, Pell's "She's a horse", Tonk's chair, "Then you're late", the three hounds in the lane.
- May skim: the Director's residency terms and the triple ledger (chs 46, 47, 48).
- May be briefly lost: the lane geometry at the top of ch 45's lane section ("third lamp… fourth… a pace"). It resolves on the next page.

**Adult genre reader.**
- Gets a satisfying return shape: one set piece shorter than Caul Hill (as the map asks), then consequences that cost something. The residency accord "buys two households, not forty", and the breach is censured, not praised.
- May want one *seen* beat of Callie's road hold in the big push. Toren hears it from the lane, by design.
- May notice the long tail after the climax.

### Verified consistent with the supplied evidence

  - Tilda's swelling is down, the growth is unchanged, and the number stays hers.
  - Tonk does a 6-minute transfer with no hand on him, against 11 and 9 minutes in M3. His pin site weeps (skin only).
  - Jab's palm mark is a fixed burn.
  - The technician's hand is due for Sowerby's verdict around D+57 (M3).
- **Ward layout matches canon:** Senna in the fourth bed on the right, Tonk in the fifth, and the sixth bed's rail for the cord (chs 2, 9, 14, 17).
- **Callie's hook tag:** "Fourth time… Third was the pen" follows the M3 "Third time, miss".
- **Home line** as a phrase: *Home line, command: D. Cole* in Toren's hand on D+37 (M3 checkpoint), then the departure page and the six lines (M3, M5).
- **Reserved truths are kept.**
  - Cinder's name and shape are not shown ("not four… not six").
  - The disk gets one clue, with its meaning, origin and fit unstated. `BOOK_MAP.md` §11 asks for one clue, not a resolution.
  - Katori's "rest", Dee's door count, Rook's third door and the weighbridge girl's name all stay withheld.
- **Plan adherence** (not canon): the map's Movement 6 row is met on company name, three-lead proofs, Jab choosing his terms, an incomplete accord, the machine repaired without a cure, and the lanes and ledger remaining.
  - **Departure 1:** the map says the Sag surge is "caused by the distant rip's collapse". The page deliberately never asserts a cause ("the same shape. That's all anybody can write"), and the M6 checkpoint lists it as withheld. This is a plan deviation, not an error. It is for the owner (§8).

### Apparent inconsistencies (with evidence)

1. **Callie's "discovery" was already shown in ch 39.**
   - ch 39:95 says: "The Ward was still in the steel when her knees went. It was still fixed."
   - The M3 checkpoint also says she funds the Ward "in the hook", distinct from "on herself".
   - Yet ch 43:523 says the lee wanted "her whole body behind it… the way it always had". Ch 45:203 says "I thought I had to be behind it. Every time. My whole life".
   - The emotional reading holds up: she saw it once while falling and never took it in. But the page never makes that bridge, so a reader who remembers ch 39 sees the book forgetting its own evidence. (P2.)
2. **Jab's range.** Feeling the whole Sag and naming individuals (Otto, Katori, the weighbridge girl, Dessa) at about 2 miles (ch 44) goes past the planted "a lean at a mile" (chs 27, 32).
   - It is partly primed in the same movement: at the wash (ch 41:325–327) he feels the drift bottom as "busy" and wanting nothing.
   - It costs nothing on the page. This is not a canon breach: no canon limit on range is supplied. It is a progression-payoff question (P2, optional).
3. **The disk's first appearance in Book Two is in ch 48.** The checkpoint confirms this is deliberate: the disk had not been mentioned in chs 1–40, and M1 withheld the *released to bearer* scene.
   - The ch 46 gesture (Toren's hand on his shirt) is therefore readable only by a Book One reader until ch 48.
   - This is an owner-level placement question, not an error (§8).

### Event and clock contradictions (P1)

1. **When the home three were weighed.**
   - ch 46:133: Tilda plans to weigh them "in the morning" (D+56).
   - ch 47:239: "Tilda weighed them this morning" (D+57), and the Director concludes "the store's had thirty-five back".
   - ch 48:19–21: on **D+58** the jars come from the east shed "where it had been locked since the fifty-fifth", and are then weighed with test weights, "Eight… My knot", three times.
   - The M6 checkpoint dates the weighing to D+58. Chs 46–47 contradict ch 48.
2. **When the fog came up.**
   - ch 44:241, Dessa: "*It came up at noon.*"
   - ch 46:141, Tilda: the weighman "sat down at the fourth hour when the fog came up".
   - Every other M6 time marker reads as an unmarked 12-hour clock (the ninth hour is morning; "the fifth hour, in the afternoon"). On that clock, the fourth hour is not noon.
3. **Ch 48's own clock.** ch 48:211 has "a lane at the fourth hour"; ch 48:275 has Senna lifted out "at the third hour" to watch it; ch 48:279 has Toren arriving "at the end of the morning". A lane at the fourth hour cannot end in "the end of the morning" on the book's clock. Also, ch 48:17 "the third hour of the morning" for a public weighing reads as 3 a.m.
4. **"Four days" late.**
   - ch 45:233 (Toren) and ch 46:97 (Tilda) both say four days.
   - The home side only ever heard "twelve days" (chs 22, 23, 24): out D+41, back D+53. That makes D+55 **two** days late.
   - The later dates (D+54, then D+55; chs 27, 32) were revised on the road, where home could not hear them.
   - Either a line of Dessa's own expectation is missing, or "Four" should be "Two". Low cost either way.
5. **Minor:** the grey coat promises the hook back "Morning… Same as always" (ch 46:23). It returns on the evening of D+57 with no reason given. Optional one clause.

## 7. Repair brief: three priorities, for the same author

The developed action stays. Do not shorten the wash or the Sag hold.

### Priority 1: make the counts and clocks agree

- **Location:** ch 43:77, :81, :247 (meal); ch 44:409 (jars); ch 48:21 (empties); ch 47:15 (beds); ch 47:383 ("fourteen days"); **chs 46:133 / 47:239, :243 / 48:19** (the weighing date); ch 44:241 against ch 46:141 (noon against the fourth hour); ch 48:17, :211, :275, :279 (ch 48's clock); ch 45:233 and ch 46:97 ("Four days"); optionally ch 41:303 (the vent's cost) and ch 46:23 (the hook "Morning").
- **Observed issue:** the table in §4. The largest item is the weighing, which happens three times on three different days (D+56 planned, D+57 reported as done, D+58 staged as the first weighing of jars locked since D+55).
- **Effect on the reader:** this is a book whose heroine's voice *is* the count. When Callie's own sums (11, 8) or the narrator's (five jars, six empties, four beds) are wrong, a tracking reader loses trust in exactly the thing the movement celebrates. The weighing reads as a duplicated scene.
- **Proposed scope:** about a dozen sentence-level edits, no scene changes.
  - **Meal:** 11 → 8 after D+51, "two nights and two cups"; 8 → 5 after D+52. D+54 stays as written.
  - **Jars:** "Five jars" → "Four jars"; "six" empties → "five".
  - **Beds:** "Four beds down on the right."
  - **Dee's term:** Jab's "fourteen" → "twenty" (or "when they're fit").
  - **Weighing:** keep ch 48's ceremony as the only weighing. In ch 46, Tilda says "I'll weigh them when they're let out of the shed"; in ch 47, "Tilda weighs them tomorrow, her own knots on the stoppers", and the Director says "So the store will have thirty-five back".
  - **Clocks:** pick one convention and fix ch 46:141 and ch 48's hours to it (e.g. "sat down at noon when the fog came up"; the lane "at the fourth hour" becomes the afternoon, with ch 48:279 "at the end of the afternoon").
  - **Late:** "Two days", or add Dessa's own expected date.
  - **Checkpoint:** update the M6 checkpoint lines (meal "11 cups", "six empties", the weighing) to match.
- **Strength to preserve:** the counts-as-character texture: "Say it", the sack turned inside out over the pot, "Not touched" said three times, the ledger closing to the Handful. Change only the numbers that are wrong, not the rhythm of saying them.

### Priority 2: bridge the two capability steps to what the book already showed

- **Location:** ch 43:523 ("the way it always had"); ch 45:97 and :203 ("My whole life"); ch 46:119. Optional: ch 44:37 (Jab's range).
- **Observed issue:**
  - **Callie.** ch 39:95 already showed the Ward staying fixed in the steel after her knees went, and M3 establishes that the Ward in the hook is not the Ward on herself. Ch 43 claims the opposite as lifelong, and ch 45 presents the realization as new.
  - **Jab.** His jump from "a lean at a mile" to naming the whole watch at two miles is free, with no cost or strain on the page.
- **Effect on the reader:** Callie's realization is the movement's best progression beat. A reader who remembers ch 39 either thinks the book forgot, or thinks Callie is slow. Jab's range reads as a power increase handed over at the moment it is convenient.
