## 7. Repair brief: three priorities, for the same author

### Priority 1: price the wash's two long holds by the book's own rates

- **Location:**
  - Callie: `chapter-31.md:117–121, 197–201, 273–299, 413–445`.
  - Toren: `chapter-29.md:601` through `chapter-31.md:175, 251–265, 287–311`, and the settling at `chapter-32.md`.
- **Observed issue:** see §4, L4 and L5.
  - Callie's still lean-to runs about 8 minutes on less than half a Handful and costs about ⅙. By M3 canon, her *cheapest* still hook-Ward costs about a Handful per 6–7 minutes, and the page itself says the lean-to draws more than that.
  - Toren holds a tipping, loaded cart for about 18–20 minutes, then does two struts, three steps and a rail, and none of it is priced. His established costs grow with how long he holds.
- **Effect on the reader:** the first four and a half chapters train the reader to do the sums with Callie. Those sums are the book's particular pleasure and its source of tension. At the climax the sums stop being done. An attentive reader of either age then feels the stakes soften at exactly the moment they should be highest, and the adult lens marks down Progression payoff and Action for it.
- **Proposed scope:** a few sentences, with no cut to the action.
  - **Callie, using what ch 29 already planted.** The wash plan says she would "put it up in the gusts… and let it down between them, and say *lane* and *down*". Carry that into the still phase: the lean-to goes *down* in the lulls and *lane* when the smoke comes (the wind is already gusting, "every time the wind comes", `chapter-31.md:131`). Then make her two "went down to look" readings match. If the arithmetic still overruns, let the scrap run out before the cut and put the cost on her own water, with Dee's line at the cut answering it. That would move "I'm stopping before I start on mine", so weigh it against the beat.
  - **Toren.** Give one "went down to look" after "Post's got it", plus one more at the cut, each with a stated number, so his ch 32 Handful is owed rather than merely announced. If the author wants a static strut resting on true stone to be cheap, the same way Callie's still hook is, that is a new rule of Guard economics. See §8, item 1. Otherwise the hold simply empties him and he has to go into his own reserve, which the page must then show.
- **Strength to preserve:**
  - The whole developed fight: the red call, Callie's lean-to off the lip, the ring stone, walking the cart onto the post, the strut-steps, the kills against the outside of the lee, "They're inside. You're not. Go."
  - **Do not shorten it.** Its length is earned by adaptation and cost. This repair adds price, not cuts.

### Priority 2: fix the two broken stagings in chapter 26

- **Location:** `chapter-26.md:5, 53, 57, 199, 239, 243, 279` (the compass) and `443–449` (the second crossing).
- **Observed issue:**
  - The ruin has two walls, but the text uses north, south and west walls.
  - The dark lane cannot be placed.
  - The second washout crossing asks a horse to take a loaded cart "backward" up a soft bank, "for the practice", with the fence ash aboard.
- **Effect on the reader:**
  - The dark lane is the chapter's idea, Katori's watch applied by Toren. When the reader can't see which end of which wall is dark, Toren's best tactical moment turns into fog.
  - The backward crossing makes Pell careless and Dee absent in the very chapter that establishes both as careful.
- **Proposed scope:** line edits.
  - Pick one compass layout, for example walls north and west with the fire south-east, and correct every mention of a "south wall" to the actual end or face. Check 279 against it.
  - For the second crossing, either:
    - make it a second obstacle later on the same stream, or
    - have Pell unload and carry the frame and Flasks across by hand first, then bring the empty cart back so the right wheel meets the soft side.

    Keep "It's two wheels on a cart, and you've got one arm", and keep the late left strut arriving "on time for the wheel".

## 9. Uncertainty and limits

def chapters(a,b): return [f'manuscript/chapter-{i:02d}.md' for i in range(a,b+1)]

INIT = re.compile(r'\b([A-Z])\.\s(?=[A-Z0-9(])')
def clean(t): return [l.strip() for l in t.split('\n') if l.strip() and l.strip()!='---' and not l.strip().startswith('#')]
def words(s): return re.findall(r"[A-Za-z0-9]+(?:['’][A-Za-z]+)*", s)

    parts=re.split(r'(?<=[.!?])["”’)\]]*\s+(?=["“(]?[A-Z0-9])',p)

VOW=re.compile(r'[aeiouy]+')
