## 1. Verdicts by question

### Q1 — Callie's lee economy and the Ward/radiation rule: **PASS**

- **Still lee:**
  - It is now priced as "cheaper than walking" but dearer than the door hook (`chapter-31.md:117–121`).
  - She starts at "about half" her stone (`:121`), which falls to "a quarter of the stone. Less" at "Three inside" (`:311–313`), then to a scrap at about 50 paces, then to her own water (`:441–447`).
  - The readings only go down, and nothing is free.
- **Gust/lull timing:**
  - The plan is planted at the fire (`chapter-29.md:421`).
  - It is made legible on the lip: the grey lifts in a line off the drifts upwind, with lulls of "five breaths, or twenty" (`chapter-31.md:123–133`).
  - It is carried through the extraction walk (`:337`), switched to continuous for the husk fight "and she could feel what that cost" (`:393`), and resumed afterwards (`:435`).
- **Own reserve and floor:**
  - Her own water pays for the lee after the stone is gone. It costs her cold hands and feet and ringing ears (`:443`).
  - She stops "at my floor", not under it (`:465`).
  - The cost carries on: the hook used as a stick (`:471`), her legs giving way and a nosebleed (`:485`), and Dee's "on the floor. Not under it" (`chapter-32.md:37`).
- **Ward does not block radiation:** stated outright: "The lee had never done anything about the ground… What the drift gave off, lee or no lee, was Jab's to draw" (`chapter-31.md:129`). No surviving line has the lee shielding ground emission.
- **Residual, owner-only (see §3):**
  - The still phase is not itemised gust by gust.
  - At a plausible 50–60% duty cycle, its implied rate is at best about the door rate, not clearly dearer than it.
  - This is inside author judgement and far from the draft's roughly 7× underpricing. It is not a repair defect.
