## 3. Exact changes, by priority

### Priority 1 — the price in the wash is restored

**How the rates are grounded.** Every figure below comes from rates already on the page:
- The still hook across a door costs a Handful in six or seven glass turns (M3 canon, restated in ch 31).
- Moving lee costs a tenth for 10 steps, then a twentieth for 14 steps (ch 28).
- Toren's sets are now priced **by the breath under load** (ch 29, new: "Every breath there's a wheel on it, I pay for" / "Breaths under a wheel… Ninety breaths of wheel for a half").
- Toren's own reserve is "two turns and a bit of the brass glass… how long it would hold a strut with no stone under it" (ch 26, clarified).

**Callie (ch 31).**
- She reads **about half** her settled Handful left at the red. The gusts, the two bank tries and the Edge took the rest.
- Standing still is costed as cheaper than walking but **dearer than a door**, and she works out that half is "not much more than three turns of the glass".
- She runs the **planted plan**: *lane* when she sees a gust lift the grey off the drifts upwind, *down* in the 5–20-breath lulls. Jab and Toren work and walk to her calls.
- **Readings as the scene goes on:**
  - about half at the red;
  - "a quarter of the stone. Less" when Toren comes under;
  - a scrap at about 50 paces.
- The scrap goes in the next gust. She keeps the lee up **off her own water**, and it costs her: cold hands, cold feet, and a thin high sound in her ears.
- With everyone out she stops **at her floor, not under it**: "I'm at my floor. I'm stopping." This replaces "stopping before I start on mine."
- The bodily cost carries to the cut: she walks with the hook as a stick, her legs sit her down, and her nose bleeds.
- The Ward/ground rule is stated at the lip: "The lee had never done anything about the ground… What the drift gave off, lee or no lee, was Jab's to draw."

## 7. Unresolved: owner-only questions

1. **The exact rate for a static lean.**
   - The page prices the lean as "less than a wheel… not much less". About twenty glass turns under it cost Toren's settled Handful plus the catch at the re-set, and his own reserve then paid for the post move.
   - At the gaps' rate (about 180 breaths of wheel per Handful), that implies a lean worth roughly ¾ of a wheel at a soft breathing rate.
   - The direction is honest, but the multiple is an author judgement. If you want a canonical per-minute Guard rate for static loads, rule on it.
2. **Callie's gust and lull cycle is described, not itemised.** Her readings (half, a quarter, a scrap, the floor) are consistent with the still and moving rates. No per-gust arithmetic is on the page.
3. **Dee's settled Handful** is spent "on the watch" in the cut, by precedent. The page does not dramatise that spend.
4. **Carried over from the reviews and not in this brief:**
   - Dee's derivable age (about 25);
   - Senna's "fifty-three" (about 73 now);
   - Rook's "eleven years" of lifting;
   - "eleven rooms, on the left" (unverifiable here);
   - the standing formula conflicts (sentence SD, Flesch, front-loading, section breaks);
   - the cold read's minor stumbles outside these priorities: the Dee/Miss Wren bridge, "in eleven", "Senna's twenty", Little's name first given in ch 32.
5. **Length headroom.** The movement is 9 words under the 55k ceiling. Any later addition inside M4 needs an equal trim.
