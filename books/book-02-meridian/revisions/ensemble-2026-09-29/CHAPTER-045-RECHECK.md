## Required fixes (exact, local)

1. **Knowledge contradiction (Q1), line 57.** The staged line limits knowledge of the Sag bottom to Toren and Jab. In canon, all six crossed the bottom with the cart on D+41: Dessa said "*Go now*", and Duchess "walked down across the bottom of the Sag" (ch 24:499–505). Rook also held the Sag road for six weeks (ch 37). This error is new in the staging. Canon's version was Toren-only ("He knew it").

   Replace:
   > Two of them knew it already. Toren had crossed it on the forty-first, with the watch on the lip and nothing coming up. He had knelt in it on the twenty-fifth with a woman bleeding in the fog.

   with:
   > All six of them had crossed it on the forty-first, with the watch on the lip and nothing coming up. Toren had knelt in it on the twenty-fifth with a woman bleeding in the fog.

   The next sentence ("Jab had knelt in its black water that same day…") and the rest of the paragraph stay as they are.

2. **Geography (Q6), line 35.** "On the far slope the lamp-line moved." puts the lamps on the south slope for a listener. Three paragraphs later the husk goes "up the east slope at the yellow blurs". Canon has "up the east slope" in both places. Replace "On the far slope" with **"On the east slope"**.

Nothing else is required.

**Q4: The four specific items.**
- **Jab's settle** is canon-safe and earned. It matches continuity ("lodging warmth stopped at the wrist… by his own *off*") and his spoken "It's to the wrist… It's mine". The ch 35 callback ("Dee had had to take his wrist") is accurate.

Not on me" and "A door. And a road. And the top of the Sag". It repairs canon's stray "Callie came down the lip to the top of the made road" by having her get up off her knees where she was holding.
- **The two new lines are safe.**
  - Jab's "Clean" is the established end-of-draw call.
  - Katori's "Now" pays off her own "I'll say" and sets up Toren's "At the end. Not after."
- **Keep "A stone. Full."** It is canon's wording, and Toren's next breath discloses the spend ("I've held a wheel in the bottom. That was four breaths"). No ledger figure depends on it. The provenance charges the D+54 Handful to "the corner strut and the dark lane", and "less than half" after the first hound fits that.

**Q5: "Everybody back."** This lands as the company's moment. After a day of the call meaning *step back*, "Nobody moved for it now. They were all behind the lamps already, the watch and the six together" turns it into *return* through an observable non-action. The one framing sentence before it is a short anchor of the kind accepted chapters already use (ch 37: "Everything above the line was what tomorrow could cost."). Toren and Dessa's exchange about whose call it was, and "In. Six.", keep the emotional force shared.

**Q6: Read-aloud.** The chapter has no semicolons. No new run-on sentences are introduced, and speakers are tagged wherever there could be doubt. The only consecutive same-speaker pair is canon's Wyck pair, and it is tagged. Repeated phrases ("the voice for a number" ×2, "At the end. Not after." ×2) are canonical. The one geography slip is fix 2.

**Q7: Architecture.** The chapter matches chs 33–40:

- company camera by default, with settles taking about 5% of the words (against about 17% in chs 33–40);
- the narrator uses "Dee" throughout;

Callie gets no settle here by design, and that is consistent with the packet.
