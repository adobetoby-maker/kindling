## 4. Camera settles (where private interiority is used)

**The rule used:** the default narrator is the company camera. It stays at Caul Hill with
the bodies and reports what the party can see, hear, count and say. Private thought or
sensation appears only after a paragraph or section settles into one person, stays there
while the beat matters, and returns through an observable action or someone else's line.
No paragraph holds two people's private thoughts. Group statements ("Nobody said the count
out loud. Nobody needed to.") report shared knowledge, not several private minds.

## 10. Exact Chapter 41 exit state

- **Where and when:** the party leaves the pump house on D+49 morning with the whole cart.
  They reach the mouth of the cut on the west side of the collapsed wash crown, about a mile
  on, and ch 41 opens there before light on D+50. Callie walks twenty paces in front of
  Duchess reading the road; Pell and Rook ride; Jab and Dee walk at the wheel; Toren walks at
  the back counting them.
- **Decision on the page:** "The wash. In pieces. Tomorrow, first light, if the wind's down.
  Not today." If the wind is up, they wait at the cut and write the cost. Pell's plan is
  wheels rolled, axle by two, bed by four or two and the horse, shafts one each, the
  carrier's board slid over the drift, Toren's strut-steps on the banks, Pell on canvas

- **Bodies:** as §6. Dee is a finger over her floor at exit, and ch 41 has her at "two
  fingers" the next morning. Callie cannot lie down because of her ribs.
- **Clocks and pressure:** the cycle on D+54, home no earlier than D+55. Sowerby can dose
  Senna off her blood "for a few cycles. Badly." Rook has one fever paper left. The breach
  (3 Handfuls under the line, inside two turns, *Once. For the Director.*) waits for the

- **Meridian (not visited):** Dessa's six blank lines; Tonk, Senna and Callie's mother as
