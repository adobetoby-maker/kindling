# Narration notes

The performance should feel intelligent, grounded, and unhurried. Dry humor may breathe; grief stays restrained. Clinical and institutional detail should land once, clearly, without becoming mechanical.

The book moves in six eight-chapter movements:

1. Chapters 1–8: intimate, medical, and institutional setup.
2. Chapters 9–16: north-yard training and increasingly physical instruction.
3. Chapters 17–24: working depth, institutional refusal, and preparation.
4. Chapters 25–32: the road north, finite reserves, and watchful danger.
5. Chapters 33–40: Caul Hill, with tactical clarity and controlled urgency.
6. Chapters 41–48: return, public proof, and a warm but restrained resolution.

Callie attends to weight, count, ground, and load. Jab attends to bodies, care, floors, and boundaries. Toren attends to people, sums, leadership, and the price of getting everyone home. The markup directs these shifts without asking the model to invent acting, sound effects, or non-canonical dialogue.
