## Operation facts to make legible on the page

- Ash: a clean Flask is eight Handfuls at the weighbridge; a thin one about seven.
  The station store holds thirty-one Flasks and a Handful (four thin). The party carries
  twelve clean Flasks. One is for the road. Three stay cached at the fence with Pell for
  the road home. Eight are for inside the fence. Nine are committed before any Caul Hill
  ash can be won, and the other three are untouchable.
- Pen rates: one contaminated man for fifty-two minutes cost nineteen Handfuls from the
  jars, plus Dee's settled Handful and Rook to his floor.
- Rotation: work, reserve and watch, in twenty-minute turns by Dee's glass. Rook works
  half turns. The rule is clean in with the left hand and dirty out with the right. Vent
  jars stay on the hill.
- Abort: when two Flasks are left inside, everybody comes out. Also any healer at their
  floor twice, palms warm after a vent, Jab below his floor once, or two healers out.
- If Rook drops out, Dee and Jab continue with Callie as watch. The deep window halves to
  one hour; if the boxes are not found in it, the party comes out without them.
- Why one mission: the carried store covers the approach, one deep entry of about two
  hours, and out. Anything more is paid for with ash lifted under the rip's sky. The
  boxes are in the deep rooms under the main building, beneath the wrong sky.
- Travelling: Rook, Dee, Pell (navigator, keeper of the ash ledger, cart and cache),
  Jab, Callie and Toren. Duchess and Katori's crew wagon go too.
- Home line: Katori (the blade on the watch), Dessa (home command and ledger), Wyck
  (the body lane; teaches the watch to stand), Ines, Otto, the weighbridge girl, Senna
  (the reasons) and Tilda (the tally and the store).
