## The north-gate pen (D+35), as staged

- **Positions:**
  - The pen is 40 × 20 ft with the drain in the middle (ch 1). The box sits on an iron depot stool 18 in from the edge of the drain, window toward the gate end, inside a chalk ring 3 ft out from the stool; on the drain side the chalk crosses the near edge of the grating.
  - The tech inside the ring, with elbow gloves and tongs.
  - Dee kneeling just outside the chalk on the window side, her bare right forearm held into the ring a foot from the box; Jab beside her; Rook on a crate behind Jab.
  - Six clean Flasks on the bench, from the east-shed allotment, not the store; three vent jars of spent ash on the floor by the gate wall.
  - Callie on the bench at the hatch end, about 20 ft off, with the counter and Dee's glass.
  - Geometry checks: Jab reaches the kneeling tech's arm from the ring's edge; Callie's four-foot lever over the stool rim keeps her hands outside the ring; the capsule rolls 18 in from the stool to the grating; the hatch end is about 20 ft from the stool. Toren is the only helper who crosses the chalk before the recall team closes on the tech.
  - Sowerby at the hatch end with a basin, cloths, scissors and blankets. Hallet behind his window.
  - Toren at the pen gate as runner; two grey coats outside with the hose.
- **Rules said aloud and written:** only the tech's hands on the box; only Dee's arm at the window; one turn of the glass; ticks every ten breaths; *red* means everybody out; *shut* means the arm comes away; Jab touches only Dee and Rook touches only Jab; clean in the left, dirty out the right; *vent*, *off* and *floor*.
- **The planned exposure:** 40–44 ticks per ten breaths at the bench over one turn. Jab felt the burden in Dee's arm without touching it.
- **The failure:**
  1. The slide stuck a third closed. The tech shoved, the box turned, and the four-screw back plate sheared.
  2. The lead plug and the capsule fell out. The box tipped, and the tech caught it on his right hand.
  3. The capsule split on the drain grating and grey-white powder spilled, some of it into the air.
  4. The counter went to a buzz; Callie called *red*.
- **Containment:**
  1. Dee: "Nobody breathe it", and Sowerby threw wet cloths.
  2. Toren Strided in and put up a ground strut against the rocking box: "Holding".
  3. Callie came down the bench side, reading the floor ("It's a bowl… Bench side's clean"). She used the hook as a lever over the stool rim to lift the box's front edge 1½ in, then put the Ward in the hook: "Taking" / "Yours" / "Mine".
  4. Jab pulled the tech's hand out; the glove stayed behind. He dragged the tech by the collar along the bench side to the hatch end.
  5. Toren went back under the box: "I'm under it". Callie released the Ward into a short Edge on a count of two. From five feet she cut the powdered sleeve open cuff to shoulder without touching skin, and Sowerby stripped the coat.
  6. Toren walked the lead case on its edge over the capsule. The counter fell from a buzz to 90 per ten breaths.
- **Recall:**
  - Sowerby washed the tech first. Dee: "Rook works, Jab under him, I'm the watch and the reserve, Callie the glass and ticks, Toren the jars." Start 9:23 in the evening by Hallet.
  - Chest ticks: 140 → 120 → 100 → 85 → 70.
  - Rook drew at the belly and vented into the floor until Dee stopped him ("Not into my floor"); the ticks under his knees read 50 against a background of 8. He lodged and came off at 27 minutes.
  - Jab worked alone with Dee as reserve and watch. The jar ran dry mid-draw and the pass-through took from Jab's own reserve. **He called "Floor" and took his own hand off.** The exact minute was not recorded; Dee's sheet says "a few minutes after Mr Marsh came off" (between 27 and 40).
  - The tech vomited at 40 min and shook at 44. At 48 Jab could not draw the hand: it was fixed. End at 52 min; chest 12, room 7–8.
  - Dee then drew the leftovers out of Jab and, later, about an hour's worth out of Rook.
- **The red call:** nobody left. The breach is reckoned on D+38 (see the red-call rule above).
- **Cost:** 2 Flasks and 3 Handfuls from the east-shed jars (19 Handfuls), Dee's settled Handful, and Rook to his floor. The weighbridge store is not debited. Callie's hook was taken for washing ("Third time, miss") and came back tagged *pen, 35th*.
- **Aftermath and message:**
  - Everyone was hosed at the gate like arrivals, and Hallet came out of his door.
  - The tech's message: "Tell the boy the ink'll come off now."
  - The tech's toll: right-hand damage (fixed), sickness for about a week, and slower work at the desk.
