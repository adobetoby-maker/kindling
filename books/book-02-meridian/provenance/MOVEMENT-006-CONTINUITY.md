## The return hold (D+55), as staged

**Terrain (from the M2–M3 canon):** the Sag a bowl ~300 paces lip to lip; the made road
straight down the north slope, across tilted slabs with black water in the joints, up the
south slope to the south lip; the east track with its lamp-lines; the dark lane between
lamps 3 and 4 running down toward the ditch; the west rim above the pipe's west mouth;
post 6 flat in the water; the dropped slab at the road's east edge beside it. Arrivals
come up on the east side, over the joints.

**Home line at their posts:** Dessa on the crate at the south lip, eyes shut, command; Katori
at the top of the dark lane (lit since the fourth hour); Ines on the west rim (bow); Otto and the two
yard lifters on the lamp-line with bars (one lifter down sick on a canvas behind the lamps);
the weighbridge girl on the lip path (blade right-handed; plaster off, leather binding);
Wyck on the lip above the lamps (bar left-handed, one day short of clearance); a gate-crew
man nursing the sick lifter. Dessa: 40 husks and hounds twice since the fourth hour; nothing through.

**Entry floors (Dee, at the north lip):** Toren stone 1 + own two turns and the bit; Callie
½ settled + own all back; Jab a hand and a bit, **floor a finger and a bit** (raised by the
sensing, not a draw; palm hot) *(repaired)*; Dee a hand over her floor ("I'm a watch
again"). Jars: lifter's first ¼, Callie's first 7½; fence 13 (5 + 8); dirty 4; home 24.
Toren: "They go back on the board… Anything that gets drawn today comes out of the lifter's.
Then the fence's. Not those."

**Sequence:**
1. Toren states the party's state across the bowl and asks "*What do you want?*" Dessa:
   everyone over "without anything following you up", and her watch drawn. Her orders:
   the made road; **Callie on the ditch side from the corner, not before**; Toren where the
   wheel needs him; Jab calling; nobody stops in the bottom; on a push, stand still.
1a. Jab's cue is stated to Dessa as "gather… Most times. A breath", with Callie's counter as
   back-up ("Callie's counter's on it after") *(repaired from "two breaths")*.
2. Down the quiet top half; a push mid-slope — everyone stands, nothing worked; the husk goes
   for the lamps; Ines kills it.
3. **The corner:** Callie kneels on the stone slab past the dropped one, still hook on its
   edge, bend east, one hand on the tail; a **lean-to along the road** the length of the cart
   and horse ("It's a line. It's not a door… It's dear"). Toren sets under the walking wheel
   from the stone slab ("Holding"). **A push arrives mid-drop** (Jab calls it **late**; Callie calls it too): three husks stand out of the
   joints and go for the worked things; two stand against the lee's outside; the third goes
   round the end — **Jab kills it with the lit hook**; Ines kills the two against the lee.
   Toren lets the strut go at the end. "Down." Callie **lets the lee down slowly, on purpose**
   ("like a rope paid back"). Callie: all the half and some of her own.
4. Up the south slope; the line opens; Otto and a lifter take Duchess. "Behind the lamps."
5. **The rotation** at the trestle table: Jab works, Dee watches and draws his leftovers each
   time; order by Dessa (sick lifter, Otto, Katori, the other lifter; then the girl, Wyck,
   Ines; Dessa is not busy — Jab felt the field "go round" her from the road; "It came up to thirty paces" *(repaired unit)*). Replacements by handoff under
   Dessa's command: Wyck takes Otto's bar; **Toren takes the dark lane from Katori**
   ("*Taking*" / "Yours" / "Mine" / "*Letting go*").
6. **The lane:** Katori's instruction — *don't block it, narrow it*: a strut at a slant from
   the third-lamp side leaves a pace. A hound hits the strut and comes through the pace;
   Toren, alone, **releases, lights the heel, kills, darkens, re-sets**. Katori, drawn,
   returns by choice: "**You hold. I kill.**"
7. **The big push at dusk** ("It's all of it… It's gathering and it's not stopping gathering"): the fog heaves up the
   whole bowl. The line holds behind the lamps; Wyck takes a husk on his bar and turns it
   off ("*Heels*"). Callie holds the still hook across the top of the made road above the
   draw station. Three hounds up the lane: Toren holds, Katori kills all three over the
   strut. **Toren's stone goes; he holds on his own till Katori says.**
8. At the table: *jar* → **the fence's fourth, not the home three**. Jab: "*Off. I'm warm.
   The palm. Round the mark. It's to the wrist… It's mine*"; vents long into the lip's
   dirty ground; sits; Dee draws him cool (the blister has broken). **He chooses to go back
   on** ("If you'll watch"); Dee: "Three. Then you're off." He draws the girl, Wyck, Ines.
9. Callie at her floor, not under ("*I'm stopping*") at the push's end; blood in her voice and on her chin — **a nosebleed**, identified by Keel that night (lung clear) *(repaired)*. The fog lies
   down with no count; some husks come apart on their own, some go back down — nobody can
   tell which. Counter at the lip 40, slope 70 (110 in the push). Dessa counts everyone.

**Costs:** the second yard lifter bitten on the calf (bounded new wound); Callie's floor and
ribs; Toren's stone gone and his own to a turn; Jab's broken blister and a lodging stopped
