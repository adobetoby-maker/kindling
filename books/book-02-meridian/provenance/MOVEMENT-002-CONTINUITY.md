## The Sag-line failure (D+25), as staged

- **Positions.**
  - South lip: Pell (whistle, a lent watch), Dessa (count), Wyck (not cleared), and the day holder in her hollow holding the sheet.
  - North lip: Rook, due to take the line at minute 20.
  - East track: Katori guarding; Callie and the weighbridge girl lowest, just above post 5; Otto and two yard lifters above them. Nobody lifts below post 5.
  - Toren runs jars. Jab stands in the west door. Ines is on the west rim above the pipe's west mouth with her bow.
  - Dee is at the yard gate, 200 paces back, by the Director's rule (objected to by D. W. and P.), with the kit, the stretcher crew, three graded jars of Flask, and a Handful already settled in her. Her "two minutes, three in fog" is for the whole party (bag, jars, two stretcher-bearers) and includes stopping for the first injured person she reaches, not a runner travelling light.
- **Success, as written:** the lift up in 40 minutes; nothing through the line; nobody below their floor; everybody out on three blasts.
- **The sequence:**
  1. Minute 18: warm iron at post 5 and two shadows (Toren); "It's going" (Callie). Katori moves the lifters ten paces up. Toren shouts "Six!"; Pell blows one "listen" blast.
  2. Minute 20: Pell calls the changeover. The holder refuses it with six moving (letting go to hand over would open a gap on six) and shouts "Keep your hands down, Marsh." Rook keeps his hands down. Nobody sets a new minute. The ledger records this as a failed decision.
  3. Minute 21: the slab at 6 drops, the sheet folds at the east corner ("Weight!"), and husks surge up. Toren, looking up the track, sees the holder leave her hollow and go down alone into the fog toward the corner. He does not call it.
  4. Three blasts. Callie, Toren and the girl are cut off below the surge and cross the bottom west. The girl falls correctly (wrist). Toren drops the jar bag, and a Flask and more is lost.
  5. Jab lights his knife to draw the husks, lighting through the belly with no gutter.
  6. The hounds go for the dark, into the pipe. Callie reads the stopped water and wards the hook across the west mouth; she has nothing on her. Toren's light fences the arch.
  7. Jab feels the lean at forty yards, hands the door to Toren ("She's worse") and crosses dark.
  8. The day holder was hit at the corner after the fold. Nobody saw the hit. She is holding one-handed ("Get back to your post, boy"). She collapses and the sheet goes. Rook takes the whole bottom alone, for 31 minutes.
  9. Jab holds her nine minutes and goes below his floor.
  10. The hounds come back east, to the dark. Toren arrives with Stride and fences them; Katori kills both. Asked "Where's the medic", Toren reports only what he can know: three blasts went, so she will have come to the lip.
  11. Dee arrives. She came up the road with kit and stretcher crew, stopped at the lip to do the girl's wrist ("nobody had the holder on the count"), and came down when Wyck asked "where's the holder" (her account, ch. 16). She takes over on settled ash and lifts Jab's hand off. Jab collapses.
- **Callie's part:** the hook held to her floor at 58 s. The hounds had gone quiet by about 30. Katori came down the rim and said "out".
