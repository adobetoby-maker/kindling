# Owner revision — Sustain, ash, and the Caul Hill mission

Status: `OWNER`. This revises the Sonnet 5 planning result without initiating
prose drafting.

## Locked direction

- Options A and B are one mission: recover the treatment-machine part at Caul
  Hill and secure the new, more dangerous, ash-rich rip at the same site.
- Rook's and Jab's Sustain can recall active radiation from a living body.
  This prevents ongoing injury during exposure; it does not reverse damage that
  has already fixed in tissue.
- The expedition needs large stores of clean, graded ash to keep the Sustain
  rotation functioning deep enough in the radiation zone to enter and return.
  The party cannot rely on ash yielded by an enemy it has not yet defeated.
- Jab's Book One "second thing" is Sustain: sensing and healing are parts of the
  same domain. This rare medical usefulness makes Meridian target him as a
  primary recruit.
- A surviving Homura cross-medic joins the teaching structure. The character's
  name remains owner-supplied. This teacher understands multiple access paths
  and teaches Kindled practitioners to increase their soul's safe usable working
  depth for ash: absorb, settle, hold, spend, vent, rotate, and replenish.
- Callie, Jab, and Toren remain the three protagonists. Their abilities solve
  different parts of the same expedition and climax.
- The new Riftspawn is a Sustain-echo requiring a team kill. It recalls loose
  ash and uses it to repair itself or nearby spawn; it does not make ash from
  nothing. Callie's Ward isolates the current, Toren's Guard/Edge creates and
  holds the opening, and Jab's Sustain identifies and interrupts the renewal
  pulse.

## Structural result

The Caul Hill sequence is the book's primary climax. A shorter return hold at
the Sag demonstrates publicly what the team became, but does not try to outscale
or repeat the Caul Hill battle. The repaired machine, recovered ash, Jab's chosen
recruitment terms, and the team's coordinated defense produce the residency and
company-name consequences.
