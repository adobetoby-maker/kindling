# Monroe 1.3 listening policy for Kindled

Owner preference: punctuation is part of performance direction, not merely
copyediting. A full stop creates valuable preparation and landing time for the
local narrator.

For this edition:

1. Do not merge two existing sentences into a colon, semicolon, or dialogue
   continuation merely because the result is grammatically compact.
2. Preserve intentional short declaratives, fragments, and repeated `And`
   openings when they carry character pressure or oral rhythm.
3. Add hierarchy to genuinely overloaded syntax with full stops, paired
   dashes, semicolons, and thought-paragraph breaks.
4. Prefer an additional clean restart over a longer syntactically correct
   breath when the listener would otherwise need to retain competing clauses.
5. Narration markup may add direction, but it must not conceal unclear prose.
6. Added timing in audio must be silent waveform-safe space, never stretched
   phonemes or a speech fragment.

Automated accepted/rejected labels are evidence, not authority. The editor must
hear the intended landing and may reject any nominally accepted change that
removes a useful stop.
