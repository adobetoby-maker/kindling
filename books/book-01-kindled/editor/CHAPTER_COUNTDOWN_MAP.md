## 1. Anchor and conventions

**D-0 / Meridian Day** is the night of Chapters 52–53: the north-road hound
fight in which Jab, Toren's party and Callie's party first act together. It is
Jab's 18th road day, Callie's 26th and Toren's 64th (counting his Kindling
morning as the first day).

**The three arrivals do not fall on one day.** The prose fixes them as follows:

| Party | Reaches Meridian | Day |
|---|---|---|
| Toren | south gate, about the fourth hour (ch. 50) | **D-2** |
| Callie | south gate, morning (ch. 51) | **D-1** |
| Jab | north road at dusk, into the fight (ch. 38 → 52) | **D-0** |

This is why the arrivals must be staggered. Each point comes from explicit text.

- Callie reaches Toren's Breach site on her arrival morning. By then its ash
  has already been lifted "with bins", and the mule is buried under a painted
  board (37:357, 387–391). So Toren's fight comes earlier.
- Callie's plain is dry and steaming at first light after the previous
  evening's rain (37:293–305, 37:305). Toren's fight and arrival happen in
  daytime rain (50:39, 50:265). They cannot share a morning, so Toren arrives
  the day before Callie.
- Callie and Toren meet as strangers on the hut steps late at night, after
  lights-out "at the first hour" (51:327, 51:345–389). Jab's fight begins about
  an hour after dusk (38:241 → 52). The rescue therefore happens on the
  following evening.
- Toren "could not find the door" after the Breach (50:229) but uses struts at
  the rescue (53:101). That fits Rook's "on a floor for two days" (50:237).
  Dessa's "Somebody tell Ines" (53:167) shows the two parties already know each
  other.

**Day boundary.** A day runs from dawn to dawn. A scene before first light
belongs to the previous day.

**Heading day.** Each heading uses the day of the chapter's first dramatized
scene. If a chapter runs longer, its full span appears in the Day column.

**What "to Meridian" means.** In headings, "to Meridian" counts down to
Meridian Day. Arrival at the gate is not the target. Callie's figures therefore
run one day longer than her remaining road, and Toren's run two days longer.

**When a heading is flagged.** Each heading uses the objective count unless one
of two things is true:

- **Knowledge.** The number would pre-announce a turn that nobody in the scene
  could know or reasonably estimate.
- **Arrival.** The chapter shows its viewpoint reaching Meridian before Meridian
  Day, so "N Days to Meridian" would be literally false.

**Heading format.** Replace line 1 of each chapter file with the Heading cell
exactly as written: `# Chapter N — Name: …`, using an em dash, a colon,
hyphenated title-case number words, and "One Day" in the singular.

## 5. Continuity verdict

**The shared chronology can be made true, but the book as assembled is not
chronological.** Toren's explicit elapsed time from Kindling to the gate is 61
days. Nothing short of rewriting events can remove it:

- two nights at Hobb's Wall;
- eight days to the bridge;
- the week of reasons;
- three days' recovery after the orchard;
- three empty days;
- Rook's 180-mile road;
- the Level;
- four days to Ferrow plus six beyond it;
- the Sag.

Callie's road takes 24 days and Jab's on-page road 9. The weave expected all
three clocks to start near nineteen and reach zero together. That premise does
not hold for the Hobb's Wall spine.

After the repairs, the heading sequence is true, but in book order it runs:

| Chapters | Road | Headings |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Callie | 25 → 15 |
| 5–8 | Jab | 9 → 7 |
| 9–14 | Toren | First → Eleventh Day |
| 15–22 | Callie | 15 → 11 |
| 23–25 | Jab | 7 → 2 |
| 26–33 | Toren | Fourteenth → Thirty-Fifth Day |
| 34–37 | Callie | 10 → 6 |
| 38 | Jab | Meridian Day |
| 39–41 | Toren | Thirty-Seventh → Thirty-Eighth Day |
| 42–50 | Toren | 25 → 2, as a catch-up |
| 51 | Callie | One Day to Meridian Day |
| 52–53 | Jab | Meridian Day |

Toren's seventeen headings through Chapter 41 use his own day count. That clock is truthful,
knowable to him, and gives nothing away. His countdown appears at Chapter 42,
where Rook first does honest Meridian arithmetic out loud. From there it visibly
races to meet the cliffhanger left at Chapter 38. That reads as a deliberate
catch-up rather than an error, so I recommend applying the map as it stands.

If the owner wants one monotonic countdown across all three roads, that is a
structural decision about the Toren spine (reordering or compressing it). It
cannot be done through time references. Under the pipeline rules it goes to the
owner, not into a repair pass.
