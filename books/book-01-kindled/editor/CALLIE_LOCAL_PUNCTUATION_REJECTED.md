2026-09-23 and is not part of the revised manuscript.

  fragments, removed useful commas, and flattened deliberate cumulative

- Chapter 2 produced no accepted change.
- The run was stopped during chapter 3.
- No candidate from this trial was copied into `revised/`.

Policy: use the local 35B model for whole-book performance analysis and
narration-risk location. Use the Monroe 1.3 editor/Codex lane for manuscript
punctuation and meaning changes. Never promote a local candidate merely because
the automated report labels a change accepted.
