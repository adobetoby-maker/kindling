   competence, and what Rook taught him, but must not announce a martyrdom in

3. Expand the minute-and-a-half crossing so its timing, danger, cart progress,

6. Preserve all canon, outcomes, chronology, injuries, power mechanics, voice,
   dry humor, road arithmetic, and chapter structure. Do not change who dies,
