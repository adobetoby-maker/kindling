## Quality gates

1. The revised prose is understandable once through without narrator rescue.
2. Every expansion earns its space through clarity, character, tension,
   physical action, humor, wonder, transition, or consequence.
3. Toren, Rook, Dessa, Milo, Wyck, Senna, and Garrick retain distinct pressure
   and diction.
4. Road arithmetic, training progression, fight physics, losses, and costs stay
   canonically exact.
5. No new power, lore, motive, outcome, or major event is silently invented.
6. Strong original lines remain strong; the editor does not make every sentence
   equally polished or equally short.
7. The movement retains its entry pressure, escalation, turn, and final handoff.
8. Narration markup and timing controls are not inserted into manuscript prose.

The final narration-preparation copy will be word-locked against the accepted
substantive edition, not against the original assembly.
