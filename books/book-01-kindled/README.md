# Kindled — Book One

By Monroe Jackson.

This is the recoverable three-protagonist assembly edition. It braids Callie,
Jab, and Toren on separate roads to Meridian. Book One ends after the three
roads have become one group; Meridian's medical and institutional consequences
belong to Book Two.

## Edition status

- `chapters/` is the word-preserving assembly source.
- `revised/` contains the working Monroe 1.3 prose edition. It never overwrites
  the source assembly. The first pass was word-locked; the owner subsequently
  authorized bounded wording, sentence, and paragraph changes that materially
  improve one-pass comprehension and listening quality.
- `narration/` contains the word-locked, punctuation-scored audiobook copy and
  performance artifacts made only after the prose edition is frozen.
- `editor/` contains movement verdicts, listening findings, and repair records.
- `SOURCE_MAP.md` records every compiled chapter's provenance.

The first assembly contains 53 chapters and 212,412 whitespace-delimited words
including Markdown chapter headings. The three retained source portions total
212,306 words before their headings are renumbered. No substantive seam rewrite
is included in this initial count.

At Calder's accepted 120–150 word-per-minute range, the untrimmed assembly is
approximately 23 hours 36 minutes to 29 hours 30 minutes. A 135 WPM center
target is about 26 hours 13 minutes before credits and deliberately held scene
breaks.

## Audio production

The callable full-book renderer is
`narration/run-kindled-book-one-calder.py`. It uses the original Calder HQ
identity, a 0.90 production tempo, 450 ms comma targets, 800 ms sentence-ending
targets, rolling three-chapter direction, and waveform-safe pause placement.
Large intermediate artifacts stay on Drive 2; nothing publishes until the
objective gates and a human listening pass clear.

## Structural rule

The visible travel estimates act as the handoff mechanism. Each protagonist's
road arithmetic introduces or returns us to that viewpoint. Movement blocks
shrink as Meridian approaches. The final three blocks are Toren's arrival,
Callie's arrival and first contact with Toren, and Jab's north-road fight where
the three parties finally act together.
