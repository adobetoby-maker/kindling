# Monroe 1.3 revision and listening plan

## Purpose

Preserve what makes Hobb's Wall work while repairing the places that become
difficult to understand at listening speed. Then review the new joins created
by the three-road assembly.

## Source protection

- Never overwrite `books/book-01-hobbs-wall/chapters/`.
- Keep `books/book-01-kindled/chapters/` as the first frozen assembly.
- Put Monroe 1.3 prose repairs in `revised/`.
- Put word-locked audiobook punctuation and paragraph scoring in `narration/`.
- Record every changed paragraph and its reason under `editor/`.

## Review order

1. Read the full 53-chapter assembly before changing prose.
2. Review Hobb's Wall in its existing movement blocks: source chapters 1–6,
   7–10, 11–14, 15–18, 19–23, and 24–26.
3. Locate defects with a reader or listener consequence: ambiguous attachment,
   overloaded causal order, run-on coordination, unclear attribution, residue
   from a character voice after returning to narration, a weak seam,
   underwritten consequence, repeated explanation, or a scene that moves too
   quickly to land.
4. Protect the road arithmetic, practical humor, Rook's restraint, the training
   progression, spacious fights, Milo's loss, and the Breach climax.
5. Separate repairs:
   - punctuation and paragraph breaks: word-locked narration correction;
   - wording, order, compression, and bounded expansion: owner-authorized
     Monroe 1.3 writing repair in `revised/`;
   - chronology or arrival logic: assembly seam repair, never hidden in TTS.
6. Re-read each repaired movement and both joins.
7. Run Book Narrator 1.2.5 over rolling three-chapter windows, using the
   established Kindled narrator identity and approved delivery master.
8. Do full-chapter listening QA and make pickups only at located failures.

## Completion standard

The edition is ready for full narration when a listener can understand every
paragraph once through, all three travel clocks are internally coherent, each
viewpoint handoff carries pressure forward, and there are no unresolved
meaning-level findings hidden inside the performance script.
