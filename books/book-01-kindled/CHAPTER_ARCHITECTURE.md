## Source roots used for this assembly

- Toren: `books/book-01-hobbs-wall/chapters/chapter-01.md` through
  `chapter-26.md` in this repository.
- Callie: the Monroe Light 1.3 Opus manuscript, chapters 1–17, imported from the
  `kindling-mj` working repository on 2026-09-23.
- Jab: the Monroe Light 1.3 Opus manuscript, chapters 1–10, imported from the
  `kindling-mj` working repository on 2026-09-23.

## Edition boundary

Hobb's Wall is the 107,358-word Monroe 1.2-era edition. Ember is a distinct
131,908-word Monroe 1.3 rewrite and is not silently substituted here. The Toren
chapters in this compilation therefore receive their own Monroe 1.3 editorial
and listening revision while the Hobb's Wall source stays intact.
