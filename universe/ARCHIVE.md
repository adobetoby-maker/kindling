# Archive — retired bibles

Owner ruling, 2026-10-27: *"Base everything on 2680 — make the cleaned Kindling 2680 bible
the master seed for the whole universe, and retire the older bibles as archive."*

**`universe/SEED-2680.md` is authoritative for the whole Kindling universe.** Everything
listed below is archive: kept for provenance, not to be used as a story source, a canon
source, or a prompt attachment for any new draft or rewrite. Where an archived file and
the seed disagree, the seed wins. Where an archived file says something the seed is silent
on, the seed is still silent; do not import it.

The common reason is the same in every case: these files made writing things down the
story. Notebooks, ledgers, logs, reasons columns, copied records, and found-document
openings were load-bearing devices, product differentiators, or the mechanism by which
institutions survived. The owner has ruled that out of the universe. The seed carries
what the plot needs through people, places, practices, and acts (`SEED-2680.md` §6).

## Using the founders' era

The seed's history (§2) says what happened between 2080 and 2680 in the form the 2680
world remembers it. A rewrite of the founders' era (Meridian, Homura, the first Home Line,
the first teachers) starts from that history and the seed's power system, cast rules, tone,
and motif budget. It does not start from the archived Kindled bible, and it does not
inherit the ledger, the second copy, the records office, or the found-document opening.
