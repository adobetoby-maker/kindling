**Post-apocalyptic progression fantasy / LitRPG**
**Protagonist:** Toren Voss, 13 at the series open

---

## The Series

*Kindling* follows Toren Voss, newly orphaned and newly awakened, as he's pulled
into the road-tutelage of Rook — a solo hunter mid-revenge-road who never wanted a
clan and gets one anyway. The Ember gives a person one door to power: a weapon
shape or a speed shape, never both. Reaching *unbound* — holding both, the way
Rook does — is not learned, it's connected. Every fight is a lesson and every
lesson costs something real.

**Tone:** He Who Fights With Monsters × Iron Prince as pacing/genre touchstones,
clean-room execution. Progression is diegetic (the Reading, the flame-ladder
Rank system), not a game-HUD.

## Two editions of Book 1

Book 1 was drafted twice, independently, from a shared starting architecture, by
two parallel authoring sessions — a deliberate bake-off, not a mistake:

| Edition | Directory | Distinguishing choices | Chapters | Words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hobb's Wall** | `books/book-01-hobbs-wall/` | Riftspawn taxonomy: Husks, Verge-hounds, Stilts, a Breach; Toren's Ch. 5 waypoint stand; the costliest loss (Milo) dies drawing fire off the cart on his own judgment; Rook gets his griefs' author under a knife and lets him go to hold the arch instead | 1–23 (Movements 1–5 locked, Movement 6 pending) | ~78,000 |
| **Tallow Gate** | `books/book-01-tallow-gate/` | Single Riftspawn species; Toren's Ch. 6 pumphouse stand; same costliest-loss choice (Milo), reached independently; Rook has the whole company in reach and declines to pursue any of it | 1–28 (complete) | — |

**Neither is canon.** Each edition's own canon — including named characters,
locations, and backstory specific to Cinder Company's history with Rook — has
diverged significantly since Movement 1 and is `books/book-01-<edition>/CANON.md`
+ `NAME_REGISTRY.md` **for that edition only**. A handful of mechanics were
deliberately kept identical across both by explicit agreement between the two
authoring sessions (the flame-ladder Rank system, the Yield threat-grading
system, the Riftspawn worked-power-echo principle) — those are documented
identically in each edition's own CANON.md rather than in a separate shared
file. **`universe/UNIVERSE_BIBLE.md` and `universe/NAME_REGISTRY.md` are
deprecated and should be treated as historical/unreliable** — they were a single
shared path both editions independently overwrote as their canons diverged,
so whichever session pushed last silently clobbered the other's entries. Left
in place for history rather than deleted; do not write to them going forward.
`universe/SERIES_MAP.md` is unaffected (Hobb's Wall-authored, not contested).
