**Post-apocalyptic progression fantasy / LitRPG**
**Protagonists:** Callie, Jab, and Toren Voss
**World:** Earth, 2150 — seventy years after the Fall, wormhole-borne power waking
a residual, dormant part of the human brain
**Status:** The selected working Book One is the 53-chapter, three-protagonist
`Kindled` assembly under `books/book-01-kindled/`. Hobb's Wall, Tallow Gate,

---

## The Series

*Kindled* follows three young people on separate roads to Meridian. Callie hunts
and reads ground while carrying her failing mother; Jab carries his injured
four-year-old brother while Sustain wakes under the burden; Toren, newly
orphaned and newly awakened, is pulled into the road-tutelage of Rook — a solo
hunter mid-revenge-road who never wanted a clan and gets one anyway. Their three
countdowns converge in one rescue outside Meridian. Every power answers a
different kind of responsibility, and every lesson costs something real.

**Tone:** He Who Fights With Monsters × Iron Prince as pacing/genre touchstones,
clean-room execution. Progression is diegetic (the Reading, the flame-ladder
Rank system), not a game-HUD.

### Legacy Toren comparison editions

Book 1 was drafted twice, independently, from a shared starting architecture, by
two parallel authoring sessions — a deliberate bake-off, not a mistake. A third,
**Undern**, is a hybrid reconstruction: Tallow Gate's manuscript as the chassis
(its own voice, pacing, and canon kept intact), with Hobb's Wall's ensemble
technique imported movement-by-movement, rewritten at Tallow's speed rather than
spliced in. See `books/book-01-undern/HYBRID_STATUS.md` for exactly which
movements have been reconstructed and the full import plan for the rest.

| Edition | Directory | Distinguishing choices | Chapters | Words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hobb's Wall** | `books/book-01-hobbs-wall/` | Riftspawn taxonomy: Husks, Verge-hounds, Stilts, a Breach; Toren's Ch. 5 waypoint stand; the costliest loss (Milo) dies drawing fire off the cart on his own judgment; Rook gets his griefs' author under a knife and lets him go to hold the arch instead; Toren proves the Edge+Stride blend by refusing to audit the changeover, structurally rhymed with a Breach's soulless version of the same thing; Meridian's archive reframes the family myth as a report, not a warning — the disk already "saved a world and ended one," tense unknown to anyone alive | 1–28 (complete) | 105,882 |
| **Tallow Gate** | `books/book-01-tallow-gate/` | Single Riftspawn species; Toren's Ch. 6 pumphouse stand; same costliest-loss choice (Milo), reached independently; Rook has the whole company in reach and declines to pursue any of it | 1–28 (complete) | 83,988 |
| **Undern** | `books/book-01-undern/` | Tallow Gate's text throughout, except Movement 4 (Ch. 15–18), reconstructed so Homura/Vera/Cinder Company are disclosed to the whole group at once (Hobb's Wall's technique) rather than privately to Toren, while keeping Tallow's own facts and voice — no Tull, no Hoyle, no Lowry Bend | 1–28 (Movement 4 reconstructed, Movements 1–3/5–6 still pure Tallow Gate pending the same pass) | 86,571 |

The comparison editions are not silently substituted into the selected
three-road assembly. Each edition's own canon — including named characters,
locations, and backstory specific to Cinder Company's history with Rook — has
diverged significantly since Movement 1 and is `books/book-01-<edition>/CANON.md`
+ `NAME_REGISTRY.md` **for that edition only**. A handful of mechanics were
deliberately kept identical across both by explicit agreement between the two
authoring sessions (the flame-ladder Rank system, the Yield threat-grading
system, the Riftspawn worked-power-echo principle) — those are documented
identically in each edition's own CANON.md rather than in a separate shared
file. **`universe/UNIVERSE_BIBLE.md` and `universe/NAME_REGISTRY.md` are
deprecated and should be treated as historical/unreliable** — they were a single
shared path both editions independently overwrote as their canons diverged,
so whichever session pushed last silently clobbered the other's entries. Left
in place for history rather than deleted; do not write to them going forward.
`universe/SERIES_MAP.md` is unaffected (Hobb's Wall-authored, not contested).
